Audit 5: two course corrections (2026-11-06)

Good progress since Audit 4: G3 closed for arbitrary cells, the generic G4 topology/whole-call theorem and the genuine singleton seam proved, the Candle skeleton committed, and the cell-0 witnessed callback passing the compiled mutation probe. Two things need correcting.

1. The 875-cell exact-entry lift does not appear to be on the production path

You are building per-cell closed HOL4 theorems for every genuine cell (16-cell chunks of about 10 minutes, 317/875 so far). But G3 (3454acc3f, cc4444c85, 63ee21818) already proves, for arbitrary valid inputs, that an accepted fixed-word run makes the production entry evaluate to M_success (Num 1).

The production G5 callback runs the fixed checker on the call's own payload at runtime. Its compute_fast_eval_ok follows from that universal theorem and needs no per-cell HOL4 theorem. The G4 Candle-side instantiation gets its whole-call verdict from Kernel.compute, not from HOL4. The check that the callback actually fires on the genuine call is the G6 compiled probe, which takes seconds.

Per-cell closed theorems are only needed for an exact-identity whole-call callback with a precomputed result. That is the one-branch-per-inequality design plan v2 rejected, and it is not fast checking.

Audit 4 point 4 meant "prove it generically", which G3 now does, not "authenticate every real cell". That wording was ambiguous.

Request: pause the lift unless it has a stated role on the production path. If you keep it (for example as a test oracle or fallback), name that role and its gate in the gate map, say which decision it informs, and do not let it gate G4 or G5.
