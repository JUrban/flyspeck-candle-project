# Audit 3: Candle NL first fast theorem gate map

## Verdict

The route is finite and the mathematics is not the problem, but the schedule is not yet converging. The cause can be fixed. The numerics have been stable since Oct 26: 875 cells accepted in about 9 s. The Candle end of the chain has also worked: a genuine `matan` original theorem (Oct 15, reflected route) and a slow-route case-16594 cell-0 `m_cell_pass`, reproduced twice.

What keeps slipping is the middle of the chain, between the fast checker and the Candle theorem. It slips because the chain is found by walking it, not because individual proofs fail. The gate map is the right reset. However, it omits obligations already on record and leaves the first installed fast-branch run to the last gate.

**Scope.** This review covers the 2026-11-04 gate map, plans v1 and v2, Review1, Response1 and progress notes v4.86 to v4.343 (mostly Oct 26 to Nov 4). It does not inspect source code; theorem shapes are as the notes describe them.

**Terms.** *HOL4* is the metatheory: the fixed-word checker, its refinements, `compute_eval` and `compute_fast_eval_ok` are proved there. *Candle* is the CakeML-verified HOL Light kernel and REPL, where the cell, topology and original Flyspeck theorems live. A *Candle entry* is a set of Candle `compute` equations plus the call evaluated with them.

Work on known gates proceeds at a sensible pace. G1, the nine ordinary families through the evaluator layer, took about 2½ days (Nov 2–4).

Each boundary correction changed the composition target, and the instruction families were then carried through a new representation. They have now passed through four layers:

1. HOL4: fixed-word execution refines exact object-rational states (Oct 27).
2. Candle: certificate-driven exact-rational operations (Oct 28–29).
3. Candle: an object-q analytic invariant matching layer 1, bypassing the decimal 10^12 representation (Nov 1–2).
4. HOL4: `compute_eval` of the exported Candle entry related to the exact objects (Nov 2–4, G1 and G2).

The delta product Hessian, today's blocker, was proved on Oct 27, again on Oct 28–29, and is in progress again as G2b. The analytic core is reused. Still, this is why both "done" and "current subgate" have been reported for it.

Review1's first recommendation was to write the complete conditional composition before filling proofs. Response1 adopted it as step 1. No progress note records that it was done.

## What the gate map gets right

The gate map is a real improvement over the 900-line appended log that plan v2 has become. Keep these parts:

- One short, current status page, with the highest completed gate reported instead of "close".
- One structural theorem for the 21-entry Hessian, not 21 numerical proofs.
- Rule 5: every new obligation attaches to a gate with an exit criterion.
- Four named assumptions that would invalidate the estimate.
- The public metric stays at 0/1 until G5.

The corrections so far were real gaps, found by cheap audits, and overclaims were retracted when found. Nothing below argues for discarding the architecture.

## Findings on the gate map

Two links of the composition chain sit in no gate, and a third obligation, the default clock, is not carried by any exit criterion. Findings are ordered by how much they could move the date.

Each link must hold for the original theorem. The two highlighted links have no gate, and the clock bound inside the fourth has no exit criterion.

**1. G5 and G6 need a Candle runtime that, by the notes, does not exist, and the estimate excludes it.**

- v4.313: the installed runtime has only case-10173-specific fast branches. The genuine case-16594 call falls through to `compute_pure_fast_eval` and exhausts the 16 GiB managed heap.
- A trusted fast evaluator can only be added inside the verified kernel. Under plan v2 that changes `candle_kernelProg` and its 44-theory downstream cone (R4, 20–36 h elapsed).
- G5 already needs this: "yielding the literal original theorem" requires `Kernel.compute` to return the whole-call equation through the fast branch.
- The gate map nevertheless excludes "any later one-time verified runtime/compiler rebuild". It also puts the first theorem before the second-family gate, reversing v2's R3, R4, R5 order.
- A new kernel means a new CakeML binary. DMTCP checkpoints of the current REPL, including the prepared Flyspeck/NL context, cannot be restored on it, so G6 also rebuilds that state.
- Decide which callback this is. A case-specific callback (exact-call match, as on Oct 13) breaks v2's design rule and pays for a throwaway rebuild. A generic plan-driven callback makes `compute_fast_eval_ok` a universal property, so G3 and G4 must hold for any decoded plan, not be "instantiated for the authenticated complete program".

**2. Job and topology authentication have dropped out of the gates.**

- v4.321 (LR-193), v4.322 step 4 and v4.324 step 4 require the complete call to authenticate 875 encoded jobs, 1,749 topology tokens (875 leaf, 874 glue) and the job-to-packed-cell correspondence.
- v4.271: a count of 875 accepted cells is not evidence that they cover the root domain.
- None of this is in G0–G6, and G4's mutation list lacks the topology and job mutations that v4.321 required.
- Open question: the existing 1,749-token adapter was built around the old two-run entry. Is its premise per-leaf `m_cell_pass` (reusable as is) or that entry's complete-check Boolean (needs a bridge)?
- Open question: does a whole-call Candle entry exist in the explicit-radius representation, or only the 175-equation cell-0 entry (v4.325)? If it must be written, its Candle correctness theorem is G5 work, not instantiation.
- The fold runs in the Candle REPL, by `compute` or as CakeML-compiled HOL Light code under the fixed managed heap. Measure the existing adapter's cost on the current binary rather than assume it.

**3. The default-clock obligation is in no exit criterion.**

- `compute_fast_eval_ok` is a statement about `compute_eval` at `compute_default_clock` = 10^9. Review1's finding 2 was this obligation for case 10173.
- The new step theorems carry clock floors ("every clock at least 32", "at least 4096", v4.326 and v4.327), and v4.322 a common clock-floor contract. No gate states the bound for the whole call.
- One call over 875 cells and 167 positions is where the bound could bind. Whether it is comfortable depends on how Candle's clock is consumed, so compute it rather than assume it.

**4. G3 contains an unplanned integration build.**

- v4.338: the semantic and evaluator artifacts name different `compute_exec` identities, and a preflight requested 186 theories. v4.340 works around this with an existing coherent cache.
- G3's clean-build exit criterion probably needs that rebuild on one identity. It is not in the plan, and working rule 4 leaves unclear whether it is permitted before G3 and G4 are green.

**5. "Negative-delta cases covered" needs an input-quantified theorem.**

- The recorded evidence is proof-producing evaluation on genuine cell-0 inputs (v4.338, commit `2d899e860`). That note lists the exact-object relation as the next step, and no later note closes it.
- A callback over 875 cells, let alone a generic one, needs the relation for arbitrary inputs. Point G2's status at that theorem or reopen the item.

**6. Executable risk is again tested last.**

- The Nov 2 vertical-slice correction made representative operations, a progressive prefix and one complete genuine cell mandatory before the original theorem, each with a confirmed branch hit.
- The gate map has no execution gate between G0 and G6. As far as the notes record, no case-16594 call has ever hit an installed fast branch.

**7. The estimate cannot be checked while work proceeds.**

- There are no per-gate estimates or caps; v1's rule capping each packet at twice its estimate is gone.
- There is no record of predicted versus actual time.
- The four invalidating assumptions omit the runtime (finding 1), topology (finding 2) and clock (finding 3).

**8. Minor: label each gate with its logic, representation and repository.**

- The same `codex/nl-object-dispatch-eval-v1` branch is called "CakeML" in v4.331 and "Candle" in v4.338 to v4.343. The gate map cites "Candle commit `800fe4cba`".
- Unlabelled layers make status lines look contradictory, as with the delta Hessian.

## Requested changes

A and B are the priority: together they turn "is the chain fully mapped?" into a question answered in days. C–F are edits to the gate map itself.

**A. Write the end-to-end skeleton now, time-boxed to 1–2 days.**

- HOL4: state `compute_fast_eval_ok` for the exact callback that will be installed. Leave the G2c, G3 and G4 lemmas as `cheat`, and state the whole-call default-clock bound as its own lemma.
- Candle: state `whole_call = T ⊢ original_statement`, with the remaining Candle-side obligations (job correspondence, topology fold, adapter premises) as explicit hypotheses. `Kernel.compute` will later discharge the `whole_call = T` hypothesis.
- Both must typecheck against the real definitions and the real exported equation set. The count of cheats and hypotheses becomes the progress metric.

**B. Run the runtime end to end early, in parallel with the proofs.**

- Build a development Candle runtime containing the real plan-driven callback code, with its soundness proof cheated. If the kernel can be compiled through the sexpr/`cake` route already used for standalone checkers, that may avoid the full cone; otherwise state the R4 cost.
- Run the genuine cell-0 call. Record the branch hit, time and heap.
- Turn the result into cell-0 `m_cell_pass` and compare it with the existing slow-route theorem.
- Measure the time to rebuild the prepared Flyspeck/NL state on the new binary.
- Then place R4 explicitly in the gate order.

**C. Add a jobs-and-topology gate** with its own exit criterion, and restore the job and topology mutations to G4.

**D. Add the whole-call default-clock bound** to the G3 and G4 exit criteria.

**E. Give every gate an estimate and a 2× cap, and log predicted versus actual.** After two or three gates, "4–8 days" will have a measured meaning.

**F. Set a stop-loss.** If A reveals another representation layer, or G2–G5 exceed about twice the planning range, pause for an architecture review instead of writing another correction note. Options to have ready:

- Generate the Candle equations and the HOL4 object semantics from one source, so layer 4 comes from one generic theorem instead of per-constructor bridges. Candle's equations are cv-style programs, the same family as HOL4's `cv_compute`/`cv_transLib`. Check whether such a bridge exists before `matan`, `hminus` and `asn`/`acs` each need layer 4 again.
- Finish the benchmark with delta specialization turned off, which v4.263 left inconclusive.
- Use one kernel call per cell instead of one whole call, which also keeps each call's clock requirement small.

## Bottom line and questions for the worker

If A and B find no new obligations, 4–8 focused days for the proof gates is believable. With the runtime build on top, plan on two to three calendar weeks to the first fast theorem, not one. If they find something, it is known in days rather than at G5, and that is the moment to decide whether this architecture is the one to scale.

The first fast theorem does not settle all-NL. Plan v2 rightly defers that estimate. Afterwards, the number to watch is the marginal proof cost of the second family.
