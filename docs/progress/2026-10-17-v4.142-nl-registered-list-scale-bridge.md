## New fast-path bridge

The literal registered implementation of `fsn_interval_list_scale` is no
longer an opaque evaluator step.  The new proofs cover its actual two-stage
implementation:

1. recursively compute the unrounded interval products;
2. recursively outward-round the resulting list at the fixed decimal scale;
3. compose both recursive evaluators through the literal registered wrapper;
   and
4. prove that this source result is exactly the bounded interval-list object
   used by the existing center and box correctness proofs.

The proofs are general over the scalar and list.  They do not trust a fixture
result, rerun the slow generic checker, or replace the registered source with
an extensional shortcut.
