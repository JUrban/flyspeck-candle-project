## Outward-rounding finding

The formally split box is slightly wider than the nearby exact-rational box
used in the first exploratory theorem.  `split_domain` outward-rounds every
midpoint, as it should.  The test therefore does not identify the two boxes or
reuse a verdict proved only on the narrower one.  It regenerates square-root
enclosures and reruns the reflected checker on the formal split endpoints.

This exposed one general handoff issue: the nonlinear driver transported
checker-selector endpoint terms back to the live domain, but not a theorem
that already exposed the corresponding normalized vectors.  The driver now
supports both representations through proved endpoint identities.  No
mathematical source term or verifier theorem was weakened.
