# v4.181: literal polynomial multiplication bridge

Date: 2026-10-20 UTC

Status: **DEVELOPMENT / NON-RELEASE**

## Headline

The compact authenticated Taylor multiplier is now connected to the unchanged
literal polynomial evaluator.  A successful typed six-dimensional product is
proved to evaluate through the registered historical `fs_result_mul` call at
all clocks above a fixed structural floor, and the literal postfix instruction
`Num 4` is proved to consume the top two stack entries and return that product.

Together with the previously proved constant-step and recursive-program trace
machinery, this closes the first nontrivial arithmetic instruction on the path
from the six-coordinate prefix to one complete arbitrary genuine job.  It does
not yet prove a complete coefficient or term program, packet recursion,
default-clock kernel dispatch, or an original Flyspeck theorem.  The fast-route
literal original theorem count remains **0**.

## Verification

Focused HOL builds exported all new theories and theorems.  The final builds
reported:

- `compute_registeredCase10173ResultMulAboveEval`: 13.1 seconds;
- `compute_registeredCase10173PolynomialMulStepEval`: 19.8 seconds.

Those are theory-builder times after dependencies were available; Holmake
startup and dependency scanning added roughly 40--50 seconds per invocation.

The multiplication proof exposed two local contract details:

1. the clock theorem must state the actual six-element radius invariant rather
   than leave the final completion cost dependent on an unconstrained list;
2. matrix-scale/outer/add traces preserve row lengths, but their generated
   `MAP`/composition equalities require normalization before the fixed 6x6
   arithmetic becomes visible to the clock proof.

Neither issue changed the numerical algorithm or the authenticated result.
