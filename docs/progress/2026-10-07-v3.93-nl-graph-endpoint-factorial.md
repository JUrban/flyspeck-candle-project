## Decision for the orders-of-magnitude objective

This closes the requested native separation on a complete genuine
certificate.  Both derivative/Taylor work and endpoint representation are
material, but neither is an orders-of-magnitude explanation.  The combination
buys 2.74 times in native execution; proving only one side and then expecting
it to close the formal gap would be a category error.

The historical approximately 340-times generic-native versus specialized-C++
result remains a starting architecture comparison, not a current residual on
case10173: controlled native work has already reduced that fixture's optimized
numerical residual to about 1.35 times.  On case16594, the current reflected
batch remains about 225.8 seconds versus 0.776 seconds for this exact native
development target, roughly 291 times.  That ratio combines unintegrated
low-work instructions, encoded arithmetic/execution, conversion, and theorem
handoff; it is not assigned to endpoint representation or the delta
specialization alone.

The bounded plan is therefore:

1. retain the small general proof packet for the two positive authenticated
   delta families, but do not extend it to rejected chain fusions;
2. integrate signed directed word arithmetic and the authenticated graph into
   one compact, total bounded executor with exact fallback;
3. execute the complete actual 875-cell data flow and charge conversion,
   preparation, evaluation, final bounds/verdict, theorem handoff, repeats,
   and memory against the existing 22.58-second tenfold gate;
4. require exact output identity and 875/875 acceptance; and
5. transfer the winning graph/representation combination to a second genuine
   family before broad proof expansion.

Further native work stays active, but the next native decision should test
generality on that second family or a genuinely different low-work enclosure
algorithm.  Another case16594 scalar micro-specialization is not justified by
this factorial.
