## The certificate failure was a box-identity mismatch

The first specialized executable initially passed all 167 instructions on
cell zero but stopped at cell 123, instruction 82, in the whole-box reciprocal
certificate for `atan`.  One-unit padding was insufficient.  Two units passed
that isolated check, but later cells exposed analogous failures at the other
angle chains and at an inverse after a specialized radicand.

The durable cause was not inadequate proof slack.  The native certificate
capture used independently rounded source-box endpoints, while the packed
Candle input deliberately uses the Taylor box reconstructed from directed
center endpoints and upward radii.  The two enclosures can differ by a few
units at scale `2^40`.  Generic execution had tolerated this distinction, but
the new direct box Hessian made it observable.

The native harness now has an explicit `--taylor-reconstructed-box` axis.
With that axis fixed, two independent specialized captures are byte-identical
and accept 875/875.  Their certificate identity is

```text
307c5c699055c9fb147678b2a32c52320e1b84a1a5e2b288cbfcff3688b4849b
```

The packer authenticates generic, source-box-specialized, and
Taylor-box-specialized certificate streams as different profiles.  Mixing the
new profile with the old source-box identity fails before output is written.
No certificate padding appears in the accepted result.

## The two larger gaps remain separate

The matched specialized verified checker is still **24.19 times** slower than
the matched 0.422735-second native numerical evaluation.  This is the current
formal-execution/representation gap on the 875-box case16594 batch.

The historical roughly **340-times** fixed-scale-native versus specialized-C++
observation is a different, algorithmic gap on a different comparison.  It
must not be silently replaced by the 24.19-times number.  Conversely, it must
not be multiplied into the case16594 result as though the workloads were an
exact factorial.  It remains evidence that specialized numerical algorithms
and work per box can dominate even when enclosure quality is comparable.

## Bounded proof packet and concrete larger-gap plan

The current specialization is bounded as follows:

1. prove one reusable fixed-scale/real refinement theorem for each of the two
   delta role families, using the existing exact polynomial role semantics;
2. connect successful direct execution to the universal analytic checker
   theorem without per-expression calculus proofs;
3. rerun the unchanged complete 875-box checker and one second genuine
   expression family; and
4. stop this packet after those obligations and the matched repeat, rather
   than absorbing inverse-root or whole-chain redesigns.

The broader performance lane remains active in parallel:

1. profile the specialized compiled Candle checker against the existing
   checked-operation trace.  The present 10.23-second checker versus the
   earlier 6.34-second operation trace suggests a state/parser/allocation
   component, while the trace versus 0.423-second native target leaves a much
   larger bounded-word representation/code-generation component;
2. treat direct lowering of trivial source-authenticated leaves as a small
   general follow-up only if a paired Candle prototype transfers the native
   result materially;
3. make the next substantial native prototype algorithmic: hold exact
   arithmetic, reconstructed boxes, outward rounding, and the final Taylor
   contract fixed while replacing one full dihedral numerical block with a
   specialized enclosure algorithm patterned on the historical verifier;
4. require complete-batch acceptance, bound tightness, subdivision impact,
   preparation, recurring time, and memory on both the current genuine batch
   and an independent expression family before beginning its proof; and
5. if that algorithmic prototype does not deliver a large recurring factor,
   prioritize a lower-overhead proved fixed-layout/word execution plan rather
   than accumulating more expression-specific instructions.

This keeps the proven specialization proportional to its measured 1.86-times
benefit while preserving an explicit route toward the larger objective.
