## Question tested

The current rational nonlinear jet definitions contain syntactically repeated
subexpressions:

- first-jet inverse recomputes the reciprocal and its square;
- full inverse recomputes the squared and cubed reciprocal;
- full square root recomputes its derivative interval and `s + s`; and
- full arctangent recomputes the same derivative interval four times.

This is one concrete source-level concern raised by the performance research
note. A bounded candidate introduced `let`-bound cval subexpressions while
leaving every rational operation, normalization point, enclosure, opcode, and
checker interface unchanged.

## Matched genuine-cell timing

The complete raw checker was run twice in each mode on the same first 128
authenticated case-10173 cells. Calls were ordered established, candidate,
candidate, established to limit monotone-order bias.

| Established | 19.537 s | 20.545 s | **20.041 s** |
| Shared subexpressions | 22.357 s | 20.948 s | **21.653 s** |

The candidate was **1.612 seconds slower**, an **8.04% regression**
(`0.926x` the established throughput). Every call accepted all 128 cells,
returned the same closed result, and left the axiom inventory unchanged. Peak
sampled RSS remained 17,982,344 KiB.

The matched wrapper selects established or candidate steps through the same
single flag test, so the comparison does not charge that dispatcher only to
the candidate. The result shows that source-level `let` introduction does not
produce useful evaluator sharing here and can add allocation or evaluation
overhead.

## Plan consequence

Do **not** integrate this candidate into the production nonlinear checker.
Syntactic duplication in these formulas is not evidence of duplicated runtime
cost under the current evaluator, and further mechanically adding `let`
bindings is not justified.

The result narrows the common nonlinear target:

1. preserve the proved lazy-polynomial completion and fixed-outer checker;
2. do not revisit GCD fuel, exact-slot scans, canonical-guard rebuilding,
   fixed/rational transition conversion, or naive expression sharing, all of
   which now have negative or low-ceiling real-data measurements;
3. investigate a genuinely different measured rational-operation or bounded
   representation boundary only when it preserves enclosure tightness and
   certificate coverage; and
4. keep the direct S2 pipeline and complete nonlinear/LP S3 as the controlling
   completion criteria.
