case-16594 inverse, instruction 7.  The proof uses the complete Taylor state

The kernel proves all of the following:

1. `bounded_taylor64_inverse` accepts both reciprocal certificates and
   returns the complete expected fixed-word Taylor result;
2. the production `bounded_analytic64_program_step` selects the inverse
   operation and consumes exactly one inverse certificate;
3. the step is compositional for arbitrary remaining stack values,
   certificates, and environments;
4. the genuine source word state covers its exact-rational object state; and
5. the general inverse refinement theorem yields an ordinary exact-object
   inverse step whose result is covered by the fixed-word result.

The last item is the intended semantic relation.  The binary fixed-scale and
exact-rational intermediate states are not required to have identical bytes.

## What “one instruction” means

This is not progress of only `1/167` operation implementations.  The existing
fast checker already executes the complete 167-position program for every
cell.  Those positions reuse a small general instruction language.  The first
cell contains 10 square-root, 14 inverse, and 7 arctangent occurrences; the
remaining positions are polynomial/arithmetic or recognized delta
operations.  General word-to-object refinement theorems already exist for
the operation families.

This proof is instead a genuine-data instantiation of that reusable inverse
theorem.  It checks that the representation, certificate, and theorem shapes
meet on an actual case-16594 operation before attempting a complete-program
handoff.
