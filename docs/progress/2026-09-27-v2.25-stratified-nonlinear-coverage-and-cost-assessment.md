## Assessment of the September 25 performance note

The source review in
`docs/advice/Candle_NL_Performance_Research_2026-09-25.md` is useful, but the
subsequent controls now give its candidate explanations different weights.

Already covered or materially improved:

- one universal analytic soundness theorem covers every supported expression;
- one shared source program and shared jets replace expanded derivative
  programs;
- source/point preparation, internal multi-box batches, compact computed
  verdicts, and authenticated support checkpoints are in the current path;
- large diagnostic Taylor stacks do not cross the production theorem
  boundary; and
- proof-producing `REAL_RAT_REDUCE_CONV` work was removed from untrusted
  square-root hint generation while the reflected checker stayed decisive.

Measured secondary or negative explanations:

- numeric instead of 128-pair GCD fuel changed a 512-call control only from
  about 6.9 ms to 5.5 ms;
- constant and variable completion cost about 6 ms per six-dimensional
  result;
- explicit interval endpoint-product sharing regressed, while a proved
  Hessian-only projection produced no stable genuine-batch benefit;
- fixed evaluator setup is tens of milliseconds and is already amortized by
  the internal batch boundary; and
- a generic instrumented ordinary interpreter was 2.59--2.63x slower than
  Candle's fast evaluator on exact matching outputs.

The strongest remaining measured numerical explanation is dense rational
algebra in the fallback path.  Across eight authentic boxes, rational
additions and multiplications consumed about 84.1% of algebraic-plus-nonlinear
force time and cost about 3.50x as much per instruction as proved
fixed-polynomial steps.  A bounded fixed-addition tail subsequently reduced
authentic eight-box compute by about 20.1% without changing acceptance.  The
broader proved fixed-scale hybrid reduced a genuine 64-box compute by 2.56x
and its complete proof by 2.15x.

The next discriminating implementation experiment should therefore extend
the proved fixed-scale invariant through the measured hot outer algebraic
path, run a complete proof-producing batch over several distinct real boxes,
and compare preparation, computation, theorem handoff, accepted boxes, and
subdivision count.  Another isolated GCD-fuel, constant, projection, or
evaluator-setup micro-optimization is not justified unless a new authentic
profile contradicts these controls.

This preserves the general soundness theorem and compact verdict while
testing the most credible remaining source of numerical cost.  It also keeps
the genuine-leaf integration lane moving: complete source theorems and a
larger bounded-memory coverage scan remain separate follow-ups.
