## Question

The v3.63 historical-angle splice improved the old exact fixed-nonlinear
candidate by approximately two times.  This packet tested whether the larger
remaining gain came from generic postfix traversal, repeated source/certificate
walking, stack construction, or thirteen intermediate Taylor completions.

The candidate extracts a compact plan from the authenticated 54-instruction
source only after validating the expected complete outer skeleton.  For every
box it evaluates:

- seven source polynomial constants;
- six source polynomial coordinate inputs and coefficients with the original
  square-root certificates;
- the historical angle value, gradient, and Hessian;
- a direct accumulator containing only domain, center jet, and Hessian; and
- one final Taylor completion and certificate verdict.

The generic outer postfix evaluator and per-job source patch traversal are not
used in the recurring candidate.  Plan preparation is measured separately.

## Broader performance conclusion

The requested arithmetic-versus-algorithm native factorial is already closed
on the genuine 4173-box family:

- exact `int128`/dyadic versus padded outward `double`, with the direct graph
  fixed: 1.628 times recurring;
- generic deferred versus direct graph, with padded `double` and conversion
  placement fixed: 1.087 times recurring;
- optimized exact direct native versus specialized historical C++: about
  1.35 times on this fixture.

Consequently, the original approximately 340-times gap remains a valuable
starting diagnosis, not the residual gap after the native prototypes.  More
same-fixture native scalar or graph tuning is unlikely to explain the current
Candle time.  The much larger remaining ratios are at the encoded execution
boundary: approximately 34 times for the matched first-order historical graph
and 16 times for its larger Hessian graph.

The compact negative result also rules out a simple explanation based on
outer postfix control alone.  A late supplied-result Taylor calculation takes
only about 0.235 seconds for 128 boxes, while separately computed historical
first- and second-order stages total about 1.13 seconds before handoff.  The
compact complete calculation still takes about 5.94 seconds.  These separately
measured phases are not additive controls, but they locate the next question
inside numerical generation, conversion, validity, and job traversal rather
than final theorem assembly.

## Next bounded experiment

The next cheap experiment will use the same genuine 128 jobs and compact plan
to time matched computed stage batches:

1. canonical job/root shape and box-validity checks without numerical work;
2. seven constants plus six coordinate-square-root terms, accumulated without
   a final theorem-shaped result per term;
3. the combined historical angle result;
4. one final completion from supplied aggregate data; and
5. the already measured complete compact verdict.

Each lane will return a compact numerical digest or verdict, execute twice in
balanced order, and keep exact fixed-scale representation and inputs fixed.
The existing native stage ledger supplies the matched control.  This separates
generic derivative/Taylor work from encoded arithmetic and conversion without
changing enclosure policy.

The decision is stage-driven:

- a disproportionate coordinate-root cost motivates one general direct
  `sqrt(x_i)` fixed-scale instruction and range/containment lemma;
- a disproportionate angle cost redirects work to the historical angle
  representation or evaluator backend;
- dominant shape/conversion cost motivates a stable checked decoder and
  prepared job representation;
- broadly uniform overhead makes an evaluator/compiled-computation route the
  larger architectural project, rather than more numerical special cases.

Only a stage with a large measured recurring gap earns an implementation
prototype.  Any prototype must again preserve complete-batch acceptance and
charge reusable preparation separately.  The next native generality test is
a second genuine expression family, not another micro-optimization of
case 10173.
