case16594 fifth-sibling traversal.  It did not use a positional shortcut or a
basename dispatch.  The plan is derived from the exact authenticated source
DAG identities:

- 167 source occurrences reduce to 76 logical plan nodes;
- logical node 75 is the authenticated result root;
- the three historical-dihedral chains are selected by their exact source

## Exact outcome and work comparison

The exact sequential and compact bound streams are byte-identical.  The
padded sequential and compact streams are also byte-identical.  Both
executors therefore preserve all 661 accepted terminal boxes, all 84 domain
subdivisions, the one padded representation subdivision, and every numerical
bound in their respective arithmetic lane.

The work counters explain the small result.  In the exact lane, compact
execution reduces outer dispatch steps from 203,406 to 96,222 and source-DAG
cache hits from 107,184 to 21,924.  It does **not** reduce the 7,444,565
interval products, 19,488 square-root steps, 3,654 inverse steps, or 8,526
arctangent steps.  The padded lane has the same pattern: outer steps fall from
203,239 to 96,143 and cache hits from 107,096 to 21,906, while its 7,438,453
interval products and nonlinear-kernel counts are unchanged.

This is a decisive negative gate for source-plan scheduling as a performance
project.  More than half of the dispatch traversal can disappear without a
material batch-time gain because the derivative, interval, nonlinear, and
Taylor work remains.  The compact source scheduler will therefore **not** be
ported into the proof or `Kernel.compute` lanes merely for speed.

## Keep both large gaps explicit

The experiment strengthens, rather than closes, the earlier diagnosis:

1. The original roughly **15-times Candle/native** gap is still a checked
   execution, encoded representation, evaluator/allocation, and theorem-handoff
   problem.  This native experiment does not measure or solve it.
2. The original roughly **340-times generic-native-fixed-scale/specialized-C++**
   gap remains an architectural warning about actual numerical work, arithmetic
   choices, enclosure algorithms, and search.  Removing redundant source
   dispatch accounts for only about two percent on the present genuine batch.

The existing held-axis evidence now assigns several bounded factors without
mistaking them for an orders-of-magnitude solution: padded arithmetic is about
1.6--2.1 times faster than exact arithmetic; the historical primitive gives
about 1.56 times on the complete case10173 whole-box batch while radically
improving acceptance; compact source scheduling gives only about 1.02 times;
and earlier zero-support/sharing experiments likewise failed to remove the
dominant completed interval work.

## Bounded specialization decision

The current historical square-root proof remains bounded to the already
authorized small reusable lift: the symmetry-aware matrix constructor and
the connection from the proved root scalars to the universal square-root
Hessian formula.  That machinery is useful for a fixed-layout historical
primitive, but this negative scheduling result does not justify a wider
per-expression proof project.  If the remaining lift is not small and
general, it pauses.

## Concrete larger-gap plan

The next steps deliberately separate the two gaps.

### 1. Measure the checked-execution boundary on fixed mathematical work

Use the already authenticated source-DAG/refinement result and one prepared,
fixed-layout whole-box program.  Run the same exact arithmetic and return the
same compact bound/verdict data through the checked evaluator.  Compare it
against the exact native lane on the genuine 1,302-box batch, accounting
separately for:

- one-time source authentication and program preparation;
- encoded numerical execution;
- result decoding and theorem handoff;
- total elapsed time and peak memory; and
- exact bounds and certificate acceptance.

This experiment is not a proof port of the rejected scheduler.  Its purpose
is to hold the mathematical work fixed and locate the roughly 15-times
boundary.  Any representation or executor change must preserve the fixed
program's exact verdicts and pass on a second source family before scaling.

### 2. Require a static multi-fold screen before another native rewrite

The next native architecture candidate must remove actual interval products
or nonlinear/Taylor operations, not only interpreter dispatch.  Before code
or proof investment, statically count a complete authenticated three-angle
block or reusable triangle-`U` primitive on case16594 and compare its required
products, divisions, roots, and transcendental calls with the current
7.44-million-product traversal.  Shared-delta evaluation, compact zero
support, coordinate-root specialization, and source scheduling have already
failed their material-gain gates and will not be repeated.

Only a candidate predicting a multi-fold complete-batch reduction earns a
native prototype.  That prototype must use the genuine boxes, preserve or
improve bound tightness and certificate acceptance, and include preparation
in the amortized batch comparison.  A native numerical winner can then define
a reusable proved instruction in the universal checker; there will be no
per-expression calculus proofs.

### 3. Scale by complete certificates, not isolated kernels

The deciding measurement remains complete traversal/batch time and accepted
certificate coverage.  Per-box arithmetic wins that cause extra subdivision
do not pass.  Reusable preparation may be expensive and is charged once; the
reported recurring cost must state the batch size over which it amortizes.
