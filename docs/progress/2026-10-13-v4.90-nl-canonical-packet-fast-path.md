## Fail-closed packet contract

The decoder requires:

- exactly 10 top-level fields and complete consumption;
- exactly 6 radii;
- exactly 6 center intervals and 6 box intervals;
- exactly 6 five-field square-root certificates;
- exactly 5 center reciprocals and 9 box reciprocals;
- word payloads strictly below `2^64`; and
- booleans encoded only as `Num 0` or `Num 1`.

Proved adversarial cases reject wrong widths, overflowing words,
noncanonical booleans, trailing outer structure, and non-packet values.
Malformed packets return `NONE`; they cannot reach an accepting verdict.

Logical source/program identity is intentionally not inferred from a packet
basename or untrusted tag.  Authentication of the registered equation role
and call schema remains the next kernel-dispatch obligation.

## Concrete next implementation milestone

The next milestone is **FAST-NL-2K**, with this acceptance test:

1. recognize the exact registered case-10173 equation/program role and call
   schema;
2. decode the canonical packet and invoke this complete checker;
3. prove the resulting specialized evaluator satisfies
   `compute_fast_eval_ok` for every accepted packet;
4. translate it into Candle's kernel lineage ahead of the general-pure and
   generic fallbacks; and
5. obtain the genuine cell-0 assumption-free equality theorem from the
   actual Candle runtime, with no generic numerical replay.

The principal remaining uncertainty is translation-state integration: the
specialized checker and Candle kernel currently descend from separate saved
translator states, whose constructor stamps cannot be spliced.  The bounded
route is to retranslate the required packet/checker dependencies in the
kernel lineage, not to build a new provenance or execution architecture.  If
that dependency slice proves unexpectedly broad, the work stops at the
measured boundary and reports its exact size before expanding it.

The first mechanical bound is now available.  The existing proved checker
translation descends through **33** program-theory layers from
`compute_boundedCase10173PacketProg` to `basisProg` and contains **345**
source-translation calls.  The saved state reported **911** translated
constants before the packet layer and **936** after it.  These totals include
the shared basis/runtime, so they are an upper bound rather than the kernel's
incremental dependency count.  They are nevertheless too broad to copy into
the kernel blindly.  The next bounded implementation step is therefore a
mechanical reachable-definition inventory and overlap comparison with the
existing kernel state, followed by staged translation checkpoints for only
the missing slice.

After FAST-NL-2K, **FAST-NL-2T** applies the existing universal analytic and
source correspondence to recover the original case-10173 theorem.  Then
**FAST-NL-2B** captures a small varied family of genuine certificate packets
and measures shared preparation, decode/check execution, theorem handoff,
total time, acceptance, and peak memory.  Only that varied batch can support
general production-throughput claims.
