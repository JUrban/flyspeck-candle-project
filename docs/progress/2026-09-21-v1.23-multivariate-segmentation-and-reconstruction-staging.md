relocated copies. A third single-Taylor build was stopped while still in the
common HOL closure after the multivariate transform passed, rather than carry

fresh sequential restore, its own command feeder, and its own evidence log.
This directly addresses the repeated-closure cost without continuing from a
partially loaded or failed proof state.
