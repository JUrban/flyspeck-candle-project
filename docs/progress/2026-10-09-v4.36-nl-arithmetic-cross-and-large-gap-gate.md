## Bounds, costs, and limitations

Relative to the exact lane, padded upper bounds are tighter on one node, equal
on the 84 domain-subdivision nodes, and wider on 1,259 nodes.  The median
padded-minus-exact upper delta is approximately `3.27e-9`, the mean is
`-4.04e-5`, the maximum is approximately `2.96e-6`, and the minimum is
approximately `-0.0543`.  Despite those small changes, all fixed decisions
and the complete online tree are preserved.

Fresh native builds cost 14.28 seconds for exact batch, 10.92 seconds for
padded batch, 15.62 seconds for exact streaming, and 12.49 seconds for padded
streaming.  Those are reusable costs and are not hidden in recurring timing.
Peak RSS is approximately 15.9 versus 14.3 MiB for fixed batch and 17.9 versus
17.0 MiB for streaming.

This is a useful approximately two-times arithmetic result.  It is not an
orders-of-magnitude result, and the padded path is an untrusted discriminator,
not a replacement for checked computation.  Its purpose is to assign part of
the native cost and select the next proof target.

## The two broader gaps remain explicit

The older comparisons continue to diagnose different boundaries:

1. the roughly **15-times Candle/native** gap concerns encoded execution,
   checked-word representation, evaluator dispatch, allocation/GC, and
   theorem handoff; and
2. the roughly **340-times initial generic-native/specialized-C++** gap
   concerns the numerical graph, arithmetic choices, enclosure algorithms,
   and resulting search.

The new 2.05-times result addresses only one axis of the second item.  The
whole-box propagation result separately assigned about 1.18 times recurring
speed and reduced the online search from 1,344 to 1,302 nodes.  These gains
are real and plausibly composable, but they do not redefine the old
340-times starting observation as closed or as the current residual on a
matched workload.

## Bounded proof decision

The accepted historical square-root Hessian representation proof stays
bounded to its next general interval-containment lift.  The whole-box
invariant remains the preferred general proof architecture, because it both
reduces work and tightens bounds.  However, this experiment does **not** yet
justify proving a complete padded hardware backend or adding more
case-specific calculus lemmas.

Before widening either proof packet, require the same whole-box and
exact-versus-padded gates on a second, structurally distinct genuine source
family.  A failure there is evidence to revise the representation or
instruction set before paying the general proof cost.

## Concrete next large-gap experiment

The corresponding case10173 arithmetic/algorithm factorial must not be
repeated.  It already held padded arithmetic and conversion placement fixed
while comparing the best generic graph with the authenticated direct
historical data flow.  That direct graph won only 1.087 times recurrently and
1.030 times with preparation charged; the optimized padded native plan was
already faster than the historical C++ control.  On that fixture the next
boundary is proved execution, not another native graph rewrite.

The missing independent held-axis leg is case16594.  Its next native
experiment will hold the newly accepted padded arithmetic, rounding, genuine
boxes, three historical-dihedral operations, and final Taylor rule fixed while
changing only the remaining derivative/enclosure plan:

1. derive a source-authenticated compact outer plan from the same 167
   instructions, retaining exact source-node identities and the three
   historical role identities;
2. use structured/sparse jet operations only where static source analysis
   proves their support, and reuse exact common subexpressions across the
   three chains; arbitrary outer indexes, basenames, or aliases remain
   rejected;
3. screen the operation plan and a small genuine prefix first.  It must remove
   a material fraction of interval products or nonlinear-kernel work and
   preserve every prefix decision before earning a full run;
4. compare that plan with the current whole-box source evaluator under the
   same padded backend on all 1,344 fixed nodes and from the genuine roots,
   reporting acceptance, visited boxes, bound margins, products,
   divisions/transcendental calls, recurring time, preparation, and peak
   memory; and
5. stop the native rewrite if the operation screen or complete run gives only
   another small constant factor.  In that case the measured next target is
   the proved fixed-layout `Kernel.compute` executor and its separately
   measured encoded-execution boundary.

This is the clean complement to the experiment reported here: the present
cross holds the algorithm fixed and changes arithmetic; the case16594 compact
plan will hold arithmetic fixed and change derivative/Taylor/enclosure work.
The whole-program profile provides a feasibility check before implementation:
approximately 43.8% of exact time was already in the three historical
instructions and 36.9% in remaining multiplication and square-root work.
A proposed outer plan that cannot substantially reduce either group cannot
produce the desired multi-fold result and should be rejected from static and
prefix evidence.

Decision gates remain complete-batch time and certificate acceptance.  A
material winner must also transfer to a structurally distinct genuine family
before it receives a general source-to-plan theorem.  The case10173 result is
useful independent evidence, but it is not permission to add a
case16594-specific calculus proof.
