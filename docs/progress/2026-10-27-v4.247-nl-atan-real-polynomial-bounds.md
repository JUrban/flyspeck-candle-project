## Proof result

`compute_boundedAtanRealRefinement` and proves that the executable
arctangent kernel's exact alternating polynomials enclose HOL4's real
arctangent on every nonnegative input.  The main exported result is
`bounded_atan_alternating_bounds_nonnegative`.

argument.  It establishes the lower and upper polynomial derivatives, reduces
both derivative gaps to a positive power divided by `1 + x^2`, proves strict
monotonicity away from zero, and derives the two enclosure inequalities.
