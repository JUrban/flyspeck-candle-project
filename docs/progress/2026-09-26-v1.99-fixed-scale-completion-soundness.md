## Research-note triage

The observations in
`docs/advice/Candle_NL_Performance_Research_2026-09-25.md` are useful, but the
current measurements do not give every candidate equal priority.

- **Measured and important:** recurring rational normalization/GCD work.  On
  64 distinct genuine boxes, signed fixed-scale execution took 13.10 seconds
  versus 105.14 seconds for the rational backend, an **8.03x** recurring
  speedup.  All 130 compared complete outputs were identical.
- **Already substantially covered:** repeated setup and evaluator entry.  The
  existing internal batch reuses prepared source programs and evaluates many
  boxes in one session.  Cold source preparation (4.54 seconds) and box setup
  (15.67 seconds) remain visible and must be charged once in amortized reports,
  but they do not explain the measured recurring arithmetic ratio.
- **Architecturally covered:** returning unnecessary intermediates.  Detailed
  results remain useful for differential validation; the intended checked
  path can keep them internal and return a compact verdict.
- **Plausible but not yet measured as dominant:** constant/sparse
  specialization, avoiding unused value or gradient components in a
  Hessian-only consumer, sharing endpoint products, and moving proof-producing
  work out of untrusted certificate preparation.

The next discriminating performance experiment should therefore use the
proved integrated checker on several prepared boxes, comparing compact verdict
and diagnostic-result modes while charging preparation once.  A sparse or
Hessian-only variant is justified only if that profile leaves a material
evaluator gap.

## Remaining proof boundary

The general analytic lift still has to prove that the fixed scalar errors
majorize the Taylor remainder and each partial remainder over the whole box.
It can reuse the established dimension-generic calculus and Taylor theorems;
the new completion lemmas handle the numeric handoff and rounding.  After that
the fixed postfix operations can be lifted by induction, followed by the
complete genuine 16-leaf theorem and a matched amortized profile.
