## Decision

Per-division acceleration is closed.  The millions of exact quotients are a
real cost surface, but replacing them one by one is worse.  The next bounded
prototype must remove or amortize rounding across a whole supported block and
start from the current generic fixed winner.  It remains gated by full
acceptance, bound quality, total batch cost, and theorem handoff before any
formal integration.
