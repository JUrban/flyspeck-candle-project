## Why this packet ran

The previous direct-plan factorial reduced exact dyadic evaluation to about
0.244 seconds but left two ambiguities:

1. exact rational-to-fixed conversion and expression constants were still
   reconstructed inside every box evaluation, unlike the prepared

2. the remaining exact/hardware cost was not attributed between coordinate

all counters and rounding operations with the complete evaluation. A second

inside the measured cold batch; it is not omitted or treated as trusted
evidence.

The prepared lane continues to derive every value from the authenticated
source and exact job data. It changes where conversion occurs, not the
numerical formula or final bound.

## Consequence and next bounded experiment

Reusable preparation belongs in the intended architecture. Expression
coefficients and source structure should be converted once, while any
externally prepared box encoding must be checked against the original exact
box by a general correspondence theorem or computed validation. Moving data
outside the evaluator must never move its validity outside the proof.

The next native experiment remains within the angle core and holds the
formula, exact dyadic representation, rounding scale, and acceptance test
fixed. The historical interval library has a fast path that multiplies two
positive intervals with two endpoint products, whereas the exact native
prototype currently evaluates all four endpoint products for every interval
multiplication. A sign-classified exact interval product can select two
products whenever interval signs determine the extrema and retain the
four-product case when both operands straddle zero.

This is cheap to test and generally reusable. The gate will record actual
endpoint-product counts, byte-identical bounds, complete acceptance,
preparation, recurring evaluation, cold total, and the same stage breakdown.
Only a material full-batch improvement advances to a general interval-product
lemma. A loss redirects work to a more compact dihedral instruction or
arithmetic backend; it does not revive root or final-sum optimization.

The original approximately 15-times encoded-Candle/native gap remains
separate. It will be remeasured only after the native angle plan passes the
material and multi-certificate gates.
