## Failure and repair

Making `Dopen` reachable from `ptree_Decl` also makes `ptree_ModPath` and its
recursive `path_to_mods` helper reachable from the translator.  The generated
translation theorem therefore carried a totality predicate through
`ptree_modpath_side`, the mutually recursive declaration converters, and
ultimately `parse_prog_side`.  The old bootstrap script did not discharge that
new path.

The repair proves, without new assumptions:

- the generated `path_to_mods_ind` predicate by the exported structural
  theorem `tokensTheory.path_induction`;
- totality of `ptree_modpath_side` using that result; and
- totality of `ptree_decl_side`, `ptree_decls_side`, and
  `ptree_structure_side` using
  `cmlPtreeConversionTheory.ptree_Decl_ind`.

Registering those results before translating `ptree_TopLevelDecs` removes the
Dopen-specific wrapper precondition.  The existing PEG-loop proof then again
discharges `parse_prog_side` unchanged.
