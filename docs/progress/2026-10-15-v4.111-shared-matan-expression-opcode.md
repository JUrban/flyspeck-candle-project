## Architectural decision

The original Flyspeck definition cannot be normalized globally to existing
`sqrt`/`atn` nodes: positive inputs use `atn (sqrt x) / sqrt x`, negative
inputs use a logarithmic branch, and captured real source boxes cross zero.
The proved series opcode is therefore the faithful shared-language route for
those crossing-zero boxes.  Strictly positive occurrences can still reuse the
existing `sqrt` and `atn` machinery where that is advantageous.
