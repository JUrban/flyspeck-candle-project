## Candidate architecture

The v3.09 experiment showed that merely adding source-level `let` sharing to
the rational nonlinear formulas was 8.04% slower. The new candidate changes a
real representation boundary instead:

1. inverse, square-root, and arctangent scalar interval enclosures and domain
   checks remain the established exact-rational computations;
2. each scalar derivative interval is outward-rounded once to Candle's
   existing `10^12` fixed scale;
3. the six-dimensional gradient and Hessian propagation uses the already
   implemented fixed-scale raw-product, directed-rounding, list, matrix, and
   Taylor-completion operations; and
4. nonlinear results remain fixed-scale for following outer algebra rather
   than converting the complete dense result to rationals and back.

The square-root certificates, arctangent range calculation, domain predicates,
source programs, job data, and final strict acceptance predicate are unchanged.
The extra directed rounding can widen an enclosure, so numerical acceptance
had to be tested across complete real certificates before proof investment.

## Complete case-10173 result

One candidate verdict consumed and accepted all **4,173 / 4,173** genuine
case-10173 cells in **401.740 seconds**, again with no hypotheses or axiom
growth.

The exact same captured plan previously measured:

- established fixed-outer mean: **661.785 seconds**; and
- proved lazy-polynomial-only mean: **582.241 seconds**.

Against the established mean, fixed nonlinear propagation saved **260.044
seconds**, a **39.29% reduction** or **1.647x speedup**. It was also **31.00%
faster** than the lazy-only path even though this first fixed-nonlinear
prototype still uses the established eager polynomial evaluator. The two
optimizations are structurally composable, but they should be combined only
after the new nonlinear invariant is established.

## Plan consequence

The candidate has passed the intended pre-proof gates:

- a matched repeated genuine-cell timing;
- unchanged acceptance on a distinct complete 875-cell certificate; and
- unchanged acceptance on the complete 4,173-cell target certificate.

This materially changes the next nonlinear priority. Further micro-profiling
or representation speculation should pause while the reusable soundness layer
is built. The proof should establish:

1. containment for fixed-scale interval multiplication, list/matrix scaling,
   outer products, and directed result completion using the existing fixed
   arithmetic theorems;
2. general analytic invariants for fixed inverse, square root, arctangent, and
   pi-half results;
3. tagged item and complete instruction-step preservation, including
   fail-closed malformed inputs;
4. the paired compiled-program invariant and certified acceptance theorem; and
5. the existing raw-certificate, topology, and source-theorem handoff without
   changing theorem interfaces.

After that closes, compose the already-proved lazy polynomial evaluator with
the fixed nonlinear step and rerun complete genuine certificates. Until then,
the 1.65--1.92x measurements are performance evidence only.
