consumed another **19.59%**. The final numeral decision was negligible.

This isolates the intended LP architecture more sharply:

- accelerating the last numerical comparison cannot materially improve the
  complete checker;
- recreating individual source-row correspondence theorems inside every
  terminal remains the wrong boundary;
- source rows and their variable basis should be authenticated once and
  connected to a reusable coarse master/table theorem;
- each terminal should supply ordinary sparse selector/coefficient data to a
  complete computed infeasibility checker, followed by one general soundness
  handoff;
- terminal-specific coefficients and malformed-plan rejection remain checked
  per certificate, while master-row identity, source correspondence, and any
  genuinely shared basis facts are charged once across the batch.

This agrees with, but is independent of, the earlier reflected-terminal
negative result: its per-row source reconstruction made the replacement
slower. The new profile shows that the legacy route also repeatedly spends
nearly all its time constructing theorem chains. A useful replacement must
remove both forms of repeated construction rather than merely move them.
