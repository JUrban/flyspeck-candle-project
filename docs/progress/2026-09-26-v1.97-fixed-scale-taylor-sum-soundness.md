## Research-note assessment

The review in
`docs/advice/Candle_NL_Performance_Research_2026-09-25.md` correctly identifies
several plausible costs, but the current measurements distinguish their
priority:

- Rational normalization and GCD work is the leading measured recurring cost.
  On 64 distinct genuine boxes the reflected fixed-scale backend took 13.10
  seconds versus 105.14 seconds for the rational backend, an **8.03x**
  recurring-computation speedup, with exact equality of all checked complete
  outputs.
- Source preparation and box setup remain material cold costs (4.54 and 15.67
  seconds in the repeat experiment), but internal batching and the stable
  analytic checkpoint amortize them.  Repeated evaluator setup is therefore
  relevant to small calls but is not the next proof-critical redesign.
- Compact batch results are already exercised.  Full diagnostic conversion
  is retained only for exact cross-checks, not required on every recurring
  check.
- Constant/sparse-node specialization, unused projected jet components,
  duplicated interval endpoint products, and proof-producing untrusted hints
  remain credible follow-up targets.  They are not yet measured explanations
  of the residual gap and should be profiled on the proved integrated path.

The most useful next discriminator after genuine-leaf integration is a warm
multi-box profile comparing a compact verdict with full diagnostics while
charging shared preparation once.  Constant/sparse shortcuts should follow if
that profile shows evaluator work, rather than subdivision quality or theorem
handoff, still dominates.
