## Semantics and scope

`filter`, `partition`, `uniq`, and Patricia-tree `undefine` now propagate
explicit change/all-unchanged results instead of observing physical identity.
The differential oracle checks structural results, callback and exception
order, and exact reuse of the original object on unchanged paths.  The selected
graph contains no call of `qmap` outside its definition and recursive body and
no call of `Print_types.unsuppress` outside its signature and definition.  The
normalized bindings fail explicitly if either presumed-non-use site is called;
the generator freezes the exact occurrence inventory and aborts on drift.

Jordan's binder-exclusion site uses structural equality.  For separately
allocated but structurally equal variables, this can select a differently
spelled fresh binder from the physical-identity implementation while remaining
alpha-equivalent.  This is a recorded assurance boundary: any difference in
the exact selected-run binder or theorem fingerprints blocks promotion.
