## Nonlinear reconstruction path

The pinned nonlinear corpus contains 463 reconstruction trees, 7,479 serialized
leaves, 22,941 explicit subdomains, and maximum depth 40. The largest case has
167 leaves and 491 subdomains. A focused profile of that exact case is active.

Reflecting partition traversal can reduce theorem-traffic overhead, but it does
not by itself remove Flyspeck's serialization axiom: current `Iarg_leaf`
reconstruction imports a theorem through `Serialization.mk_ser_thm`. A useful
long-term nonlinear checker therefore has two stages:

1. reflect and authenticate the complete tree/partition reconstruction; then
2. implement and prove the interval/nonlinear leaf checker needed to replace
   serialized leaf import while preserving the public theorem and allowed-axiom
   interface.

Measured largest-case phase data will decide whether partition reflection is a
material first optimization or whether work should move directly toward the
leaf checker.
