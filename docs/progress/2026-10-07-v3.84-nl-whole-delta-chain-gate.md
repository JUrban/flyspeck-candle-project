## Bounded question

After authenticating and accelerating the three numerator/radicand pairs, the
next native screen attempted to replace each exact source chain

```text
pi/2; -d(delta)/dx[3+i]; 4*x[i]*delta; sqrt; inverse; mul; atan; add
```

with the existing direct jet-composition implementation.  The source gate
requires exact polynomial identities, the eight exact opcodes, matching
coordinate pairs `(x1,x4)`, `(x2,x5)`, and `(x3,x6)`, and exactly one chain of
each kind.  It consumes the same supplied square-root certificate at the same
logical source position.

The first smoke initially exposed a setup dependency: fixed copies of the
supplied square-root certificates were only prepared under the older
fixed-kernel flag.  The chain now requests that conversion explicitly.  No
certificate, domain rule, or bound was weakened.

## Precise rejection

With the correct supplied certificates available, the exact-mixed candidate
stops on genuine job 0 before returning a bound:

```text
prepared dihedral arctangent domain failure
center=-858015810201:-858015208760
box=-1223297192714:-589914679453
scale=1099511627776
```

The center quotient is approximately `[-0.78036,-0.78036]` and remains
inside the verified arctangent kernel's `(-1,1)` domain.  The directly
composed box quotient is approximately `[-1.1126,-0.5365]`; its lower endpoint
crosses `-1`.  Thus this is not an arithmetic overflow, source-recognition
failure, missing certificate, or final certificate rejection.  It is an
enclosure-quality failure at the intermediate arctangent contract.

The sequential delta-pair lane accepts all 875 cells because its ordinary
Taylor completion tightens the intermediate quotient before applying
`atan`.  The attempted whole-chain fusion discarded that useful tightening.
The padded-hardware candidate was not pursued after the exact lane failed on
the first genuine cell.

## Decision

This whole-chain variant is rejected before timing, full-certificate
execution, or proof work.  It does not change the positive decision on the
two general delta instructions:

- `4*x_i*delta` and `-d(delta)/dx_j` remain independently authenticated;
- their sequential combination still accepts 875/875 and improves exact
  native recurring time from 2.377 to 1.293 seconds; and
- their bounded, coordinate-parametric soundness proof remains worthwhile.

The next algorithm experiment must preserve the successful intermediate
tightening.  The narrow candidates are:

1. complete only the quotient Taylor result before `atan`, while keeping the
   authenticated numerator/radicand preparation and avoiding the earlier
   polynomial expansions; or
2. prove and implement a tighter quotient enclosure whose domain is at least
   as strong as that completion on the genuine boxes.

Another raw box-value composition, a looser certificate, or an arctangent
domain exception is not justified.  Any candidate must first pass job 0, then
all 875 cells with bound deltas and complete-batch timing before proof work.

On the executor side, the independent next gate remains the proved 128/192
mixed operation trace.  This rejected numerical chain does not weaken the
radix-22 multiplication result and does not justify pausing the bounded-word
work.
