## Held-arithmetic whole-jet result

The first coarse propagation experiment has passed its bounded native gate.
Both lanes use the authenticated 167-instruction fifth-sibling source, exact
checked `2^40` arithmetic, directed rounding, computed square-root
certificates, the same three historical dihedral instructions, reconstructed
input boxes, source-DAG identities, and final Taylor acceptance rule.

The baseline constructs a completed Taylor result after each generic source
operation.  The candidate instead carries:

- a center value and gradient; and
- a whole-box value, gradient, and symmetric Hessian,

then performs the Taylor completion once at the complete-expression boundary.
This is an untrusted native discriminator of a general propagation
architecture, not an expression theorem or release result.

Nine warmed, alternating repetitions on the fixed 1,344-node historical path
gave:

| Lane | Preparation | Evaluation | Complete batch | Negative nodes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Repeated Taylor completion | 0.069990 s | 0.503707 s | 0.573696 s | 682 |
| Whole-box jet | 0.071105 s | 0.425699 s | 0.496804 s | 703 |

The candidate is **1.183 times** faster in recurring evaluation and **1.155
times** faster with preparation charged.  It preserves every one of the 682
baseline-negative nodes and adds 21 earlier negative decisions.

The complete bound comparison is stronger than the decision count:

- candidate tighter: **1,260** nodes;
- exactly equal: **84** domain-subdivision nodes;
- candidate wider: **0** nodes; and
- baseline negative decisions lost: **0**.

The candidate-minus-baseline upper-bound delta has median approximately
`-0.0076823`, mean `-0.0808832`, maximum zero, and minimum approximately
`-43.2510`.  This is consistent with avoiding repeated widening from
intermediate Taylor reconstruction, rather than merely reducing dispatch.

## What changed and what did not

On the fixed path, interval products fall only from 7,722,701 to 7,701,281,
about 0.28%.  The large structural change is completed Taylor results:
73,080 become 1,260.  Directed floor/ceiling quotient calls fall from
5,977,937 each to 4,995,473 each.  This explains why the gain is materially
larger than the rejected dispatch and sparse-root experiments even though the
same scalar operations and historical primitives remain.

The result is promising but not remotely the orders-of-magnitude endpoint.
It identifies one reusable source of unnecessary work and, unusually, also
improves enclosure quality.  It does not remove the independent checked-word
execution overhead, and it does not reproduce the much more specialized
historical C++ numerical library.

A first non-balanced padded-hardware probe of the same whole-jet algorithm
kept 703 negative nodes and took 0.2100 seconds of evaluation versus 0.4261
seconds in its adjacent exact probe, with one broad-node representation
failure treated as a mandatory split.  This approximately two-times signal
agrees with the earlier representation factorial, but it is not promoted as
accepted timing evidence until balanced repetitions and an online run are
complete.  It suggests that propagation cadence and arithmetic representation
are largely independent gains rather than alternative explanations.

## Proof and next-experiment decision

The existing historical square-root Hessian representation proof remains
bounded.  Its fixed row and 6-by-6 cval correspondence passed and was committed;
the next local proof step is only the general interval-containment lift.  No
proof of the three-percent coordinate-root candidate will be started.

The whole-box result earns a general proof-design packet, not a
case16594-specific calculus proof:

1. define a whole-box jet invariant relating center value/gradient and box
   value/gradient/Hessian intervals to every supported analytic expression;
2. prove preservation once for constant, variable, negation, addition,
   multiplication, inverse, square root, and arctangent;
3. prove the authenticated source-DAG executor simulates those expression
   rules, including exact reuse identities and square-root certificate order;
4. instantiate the existing universal Taylor theorem only at declared
   completion boundaries; and
5. retain an exact fail-closed fallback when the whole-box domain or fixed
   representation cannot establish a result.

Before that proof grows, the native lane should complete two further gates:

- balanced exact-versus-padded runs of the identical whole-jet algorithm,
  including root-started traversal, to finish the arithmetic/rounding cross;
  and
- the same whole-jet architecture on a second genuine expression family.

If both remain green, the high-payoff formal experiment is a compact
`Kernel.compute` whole-jet executor returning one batch verdict and theorem
handoff.  That directly measures the separate encoded-execution gap.  Larger
specialized numerical instructions, especially historical-style dihedral and
elementary-function bounds, remain a subsequent architectural target because
the current 18% gain does not explain the old roughly 340-times starting
comparison.
