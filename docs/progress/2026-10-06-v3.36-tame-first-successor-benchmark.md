## Cost and early performance signal

The retained cold run took 134.99 seconds wall time (133.81 seconds user, 1.16 seconds system) and peaked at 1,090,432 KiB RSS. This includes HOL startup, the stable seed support, reflected successor definitions/proofs, two identical theorem-producing successor evaluations, identity checks, and theorem handoff to the test.

After a separate live session explicitly acknowledged that all of that preparation was ready, 1,000 repetitions of the exact theorem-producing `Kernel.compute` workload produced 2,000 child graphs in 4.255 host seconds. That is approximately 235.02 two-child batches per second, or 470.04 child graphs per second, at 1,092,964 KiB post-run RSS. The verified runtime deliberately exposes zero-valued process clocks, so the recurring measurement used 250 ms host polling and has about one polling interval of upper uncertainty.

This is encouraging evidence that the chosen graph representation and basic transition machinery are executable. It is not yet a projection of complete-enumeration throughput: the full run must also pay frontier management, all pruning decisions, final classification, much larger graph values, and garbage collection.

The independent Isabelle first-step session took 15 seconds total including session startup. Its theory commands took 2.617 seconds elapsed / 2.688 seconds CPU; the printed `next_tame0` projection itself took 1.381 seconds. Those numbers establish a reference boundary but are not a cross-runtime speed comparison.

Development accounting is intentionally not limited to the green measurement. Before the retained result, two approximately 125-second cold runs found a recursive-equation proof issue; later superseded cold runs exercised progressively stronger fixtures. A 100,000-repeat probe was stopped after roughly 390 seconds and a subsequent 10,000-repeat probe after more than 240 seconds because their persistent session had become GC-stressed. The final ledger records these as preparation/development costs, not performance evidence. The known attempts already exceed 26 minutes, plus unmeasured focused proof iteration and prepared-session startup.
