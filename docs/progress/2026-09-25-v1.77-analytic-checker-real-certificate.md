## The analytic bridge and complete checker are now universal

The analytic-expression language now has one reusable calculus and Taylor
bridge. For every dimension-valid supported expression, a successful domain
check establishes its value, gradient, and Hessian semantics and the existing
Flyspeck Taylor theorem supplies the whole-box upper bound. A new expression
therefore needs a mechanical source correspondence and checker data, not a
separately developed calculus proof.

The executable checker compiles one analytic AST and evaluates it twice:

1. one full jet at the exact box center;
2. one full interval jet over the whole box;
3. domain guards, box validity, and the complete Taylor upper bound as data;
4. one general soundness theorem from numerical acceptance to the original
   source inequality.

The small nested end-to-end proof includes polynomial, reciprocal, square root,
addition, and negation nodes. It computes the exact upper bound `-2048/2048`,
derives an assumption-free source theorem, and leaves the global axiom set
unchanged. The stable checker checkpoint is:

```text

```
