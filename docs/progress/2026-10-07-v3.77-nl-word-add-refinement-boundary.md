## Scope and next gate

This is an implementation/refinement milestone, not yet a performance claim.
The `cv`/`cval` compute datatypes and 62-equation contract are unchanged, so
the new functions do not yet execute inside a closed `Kernel.compute` call.
Likewise, signed interval representation, wide multiplication, and directed
wide shifts remain open.

The next bounded decision is not to add all of those operations blindly.  It
is to map the smallest useful carry-chain operation into an experimental
compute-executor node, retain the exact-natural operation as overflow
fallback, and measure a closed repeated workload.  The experiment must charge
input construction, executor time, result/theorem handoff, and fallback.  A
loss stops the datatype extension; a win advances wide multiply/shift and the
complete 875-cell case16594 gate.

The broader native result remains the architectural context: safe graph
lowering bought only about 1.045 times under exact arithmetic, exact versus
hardware costs about 3.22 times, and the matched reflected/native boundary is
about 98 times.  The word work therefore targets a measured formal-execution
problem; it is not being justified by the rejected 85-step specialization.
