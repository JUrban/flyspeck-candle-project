launch, a reverse traversal of all current generated `.uo` manifests showed

step.  It was the front of another roughly day-scale verified runtime rebuild.

case-10173 certificate and `compiler64Prog` carries a theorem solely for that
literal.  Rebuilding the whole chain for this one case would not be a reusable
all-NL architecture.
