## What this packet established

A new explicit-radius whole-call executable now combines the numerical cell
checks and compact topology replay while forcing both to consume the same
canonical job stream:

```text
job = (outer_boxes,(center_boxes,source_boxes))
source descriptor = (plan_codes,(source_instructions,packed_cells))
packed cell = (radii,certificates)
```

The numerical side consumes `packed_cells` and `jobs` in lockstep and rejects
failed entries, nonzero terminators, or unequal stream lengths.  The unchanged
job value is passed to the topology runner.  This avoids an earlier candidate
layout that embedded radii and certificates in each job and would therefore
have broken the existing logical job decoder.

The accompanying conditional contract skeleton proves:

1. decomposition of the whole-call numerical and topology components;
2. conversion from encoded topology execution to the existing logical
   topology runner; and
3. the singleton-root `m_cell_pass` conclusion conditional on canonical job
   representation, the matching per-job leaf theorems, and the generalized
   topology contract.

The last three items remain explicit proof obligations.  This skeleton is an
end-to-end contract/type probe, not a completed soundness theorem.
