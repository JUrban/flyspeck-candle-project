## September 25 research note: measured disposition

The note
`docs/advice/Candle_NL_Performance_Research_2026-09-25.md` is useful.  Its
source findings were hypotheses rather than timing claims, and the focused
experiments now separate them as follows.

### Already covered

- The supported expression language has one reusable analytic soundness
  theorem; genuine formulas do not receive separate hand-written calculus
  proofs.
- Several boxes execute inside aggregate calls that return compact acceptance
  verdicts.  Per-cell facts are derived from an aggregate theorem without
  rerunning the numerical checker.
- Source, plan, per-box preparation, numerical compute, theorem extraction,
  and live handoff have separate timing markers.
- Square-root endpoints are proposed by direct ordinary exact arithmetic in
  untrusted preparation.  The former `REAL_RAT_REDUCE_CONV` callback is kept
  only as a focused oracle; the reflected checker validates every proposal.
- The fixed/rational tagged evaluator already limits the worst exact-fraction
  growth.  On the earlier genuine 64-box batch it improved numerical time by
  **2.56x** and complete proof time by **2.15x**.

### Relevant measured costs

- **Enclosure quality and subdivision are now the leading issue.**  Only 9 of
  these 32 legacy leaves accept whole.  Improving raw arithmetic without
  tracking accepted domains could therefore make total verification slower.
- **Reusable preparation is large.**  Source and point-plan work here totals
  103.091 seconds versus 26.188 seconds in the numerical phase.  Domain-aware
  sharing is useful, but the square-root guard result shows that it cannot be
  applied blindly.
- **Exact rational nonlinear operations remain credible targets.**  The
  tagged backend's earlier speedup is direct evidence that representation
  matters.  The residual rational `inv`, `sqrt`, and `atn` mix should be
  measured before changing another arithmetic layer.
- **Theorem handoff is measurable.**  The prior 12-cell proof spent 6.193
  seconds extracting source theorems and 4.067 seconds on live handoff/gluing.
  A compact forest/root handoff is still worth a bounded experiment.
- **Avoidable duplicate preparation was real.**  Candle commit `944cb10e`
  reuses the exact authenticated cell variants between discrimination and
  proof.  The relevant phase fell from 9.613 seconds to 0.033 seconds while
  preserving theorem digest
  `7cf22acbe5ab0f7bce693d199f91dbe7`.

### Lower priority after measurement

- Fixed evaluator setup measured at about 40 ms or below the 0.05-second
  observer resolution.  It does not explain the current multi-second gap.
- Numeric versus 128-pair GCD fuel, constant/zero completion, explicit
  interval-product sharing, and a proved Hessian-only projection were
  secondary, neutral, or regressive in focused controls.  They should not
  displace the measured coverage, preparation, and rational-instruction work.
- Full diagnostic results did add avoidable output/conversion work, but the
  aggregate production path already returns only a compact verdict.

## Next targeted experiments

1. Run a bounded adaptive-subdivision policy over the 23 rejected whole leaves
   and report final accepted-cell count, preparation reuse, arithmetic mix,
   integer growth, memory, and total proof time.
2. Compare tighter source envelopes or legacy certificate-directed splits on
   the same pinned leaves.  The acceptance vector must remain a first-class
   metric alongside seconds per box.
3. Profile residual rational `inv`, `sqrt`, and `atn` instructions inside that
   accepted adaptive batch before extending the fixed representation.
4. Test one compact forest/root soundness handoff against the current
   per-cell extraction and glue path.

The note helps explain the remaining gap, but not through one single cost.
Current evidence says the dominant scaling problem is the combination of
loose whole-leaf enclosures and reusable preparation, followed by numerical
representation and theorem handoff.  Repeated evaluator setup and the tested
micro-optimizations are not presently leading explanations.
