## Universal calculus milestone

Candle commit `f2de36f` proves the polynomial analytic correspondence at a
dimension-independent coordinate boundary.  Variables are interpreted by an
arbitrary total environment `rho : num -> real`; updating coordinate `i` with
the real argument `u` gives the one-dimensional function used for
differentiation.

The kernel now proves, for every expression, environment, and coordinate:

```text
candle_poly_value_fun_has_real_derivative:
  !e rho i.
    ((\u. value_fun (update rho i u) e)
      has_real_derivative d_fun i rho e)
    (atreal (rho i))
```

and, for every pair of coordinates:

```text
candle_poly_d_fun_has_real_derivative:
  !e rho i j.
    ((\u. d_fun i (update rho j u) e)
      has_real_derivative dd_fun j i rho e)
    (atreal (rho j))
```

These are structural theorems over the AST.  They are not generated anew for
each expression, and their statements contain no fixed dimension or fixed set
of six variables.

## Development finding

An early compact proof of the finite-list correspondence used one broad
`MESON_TAC` after the structural induction.  The second-derivative case grew
past three million search nodes.  That disposable restore was stopped.  The
durable proof specializes each induction hypothesis once, splits validity by
constructor, rewrites value and first-derivative correspondence explicitly,
and uses only small fully instantiated congruence steps.  The clean replay has
no open-ended quantified MESON search.
