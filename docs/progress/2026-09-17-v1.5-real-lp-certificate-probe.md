## Why focused LP probes were run

The action-170 traversal is long but produces regular archive progress, so it
does not need speculative interruption. While it runs, the cheapest useful
action-184 risk reduction was to combine the new bounded Marshal decoder with
the actual HOL LP verifier on one real certificate, then test the largest
prepared shard separately. Fresh copies of the canonical post-action-164
checkpoint allowed those experiments without replaying actions 0--164 or
reusing any failed state.

The probe deliberately omitted action 170 and therefore grants no cumulative
credit after action 169. It loaded current sources 171--183 only as focused
development context, then selected the sole certificate in the small real
`hard_2.dat` shard. Separate markers distinguished checkpoint/frontend work,
decoding, inequality initialization, and proof execution.

## Legacy-checkpoint bridge correction

The first actual clean-checkpoint probe, v221, exposed a namespace error in
the proposed late bridge: unqualified `open_in` denoted a HOL theorem after
the checkpoint was restored. The next fresh probe, v222, confirmed the
qualified channel aliases and exposed a second checkpoint-only mismatch: the
legacy executable predates `Cake.Word64`. Both processes were stopped at the
first bridge error and their partially loaded states were discarded.

The corrected late bridge uses `Stdlib.open_in`/`Stdlib.open_out` and models
the required int64 operations as exact modulo-2^64 arithmetic over CakeML's
unbounded integers. It validates the `2^63` signed boundary and `-1`, and
renders negative values with OCaml-compatible spelling. This is a development
bridge for the legacy checkpoint only. Candle commit `67a224e` remains
unchanged and uses the preferred `Cake.Word64` representation; the eventual
fresh action-zero replay must confirm that committed runtime.

## Checkpoint and replay policy

The clean post-action-164 checkpoint is the normal focused-development
predecessor. Every probe receives a fresh reflink copy; a failed or partially
loaded process is discarded rather than continued. The v220 post-action-170
and post-action-183 checkpoints will similarly be copied before continuation.
A fresh action-zero replay is reserved for cumulative confirmation of a
coherent successful 165--184 repair batch and the committed `Cake.Word64`
runtime, rather than repeated after individual exploratory changes.

The older v208 action-170 process was reviewed against v220 and stopped at
`3201 / 19715`. Its action-165 and action-170 normalized inputs are
byte-identical to v220, the action/commit phrases through 170 are identical,
and its source-version differences affect only the post-170 batch. Because its
already-tokenized stale tail made its checkpoint unusable for current
continuation, finishing v208 would duplicate v220 while consuming one CPU and
about 18 GiB RAM. Its partial log and retirement receipt are preserved; no
checkpoint or release sentinel was created.
