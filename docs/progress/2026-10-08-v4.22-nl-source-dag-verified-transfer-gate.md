## What transferred

The native source-DAG experiment found 167 authenticated source occurrences,
76 role-sensitive structural identities, and 91 exact reuses per box.  Its
complete 875-box result improved from 0.451968 to 0.307713 seconds, a 1.4688
times speedup, without changing any final bound record.

The new CakeML development checker implements the same conservative boundary:

- identity is exact source structure plus declared specialization role;
- arbitrary algebraic equality, basename equality, and numerical coincidence
  do not authorize reuse;
- every box gets a fresh 76-slot cache;
- a repeated square-root, inverse, or arctangent node may reuse its result only
  if its complete typed certificate equals the certificate authenticated on
  first evaluation; and
- out-of-range identity, source/identity length mismatch, changed certificate,
  malformed input, trailing bytes, or failed final negativity rejects.

The pinned plan proves 167 identities, 76 unique nodes, root identity 75, and
91 reuses.  The packed byte parser, 875-cell count, end-of-input condition, and
final strict-negativity predicate are unchanged from the held control.

## Broader performance decision

This result attacks the checked-execution/representation part of the gap; it
does not collapse the separate algorithmic gap to the historical specialized
C++ verifier.  The original approximately 15-times Candle/native and
340-times generic-native/specialized-C++ comparisons therefore remain
explicit, distinct architectural signals rather than numbers to multiply.

The previous 2-by-2 native factorial also remains decisive.  Exact arithmetic
made the historical three-dihedral enclosure relatively more expensive, but
that enclosure was still 16% slower per box under padded arithmetic and 26.8%
slower under the proof-relevant exact backend.  No per-leaf historical
specialization proof is started.

The bounded next order is:

1. prove the source-DAG evaluator equivalent to sequential evaluation under a
   general plan/cache invariant, then connect the packed verdict to the
   existing analytic checker theorem;
2. use sealed forest parents to compare exact-DAG and tighter historical
   enclosures on complete closure, visited-node count, elapsed time, and
   memory; the historical path must reduce visited nodes by more than about
   21% to repay its exact per-node cost;
3. test a complete low-work expression plan on an independent source family,
   rather than accumulating case16594-only instructions; and
4. treat a proved bounded-word arithmetic backend as a separate reusable
   target after the expression-plan/data contract is stable.

The deciding measures remain complete-certificate or complete-forest
acceptance and total elapsed cost, with reusable preparation charged once and
reported separately.
