## What ran

The probe obtains the actual Iarg targets through `Break_case_exec`, reconstructs
their exact source bounds, and reuses one prepared family of informal Taylor
functions across all 21 subcases.  Search and adaptive-precision replay are
untrusted planning only; every production leaf must still be independently
proved by the reflected checker.

The old planner required three narrow normalizations before it could consume
the real source expression:

- expand rotations and `delta_x4` introduced after the first rewrite pass;
- expand `sol0`/`const1` in the correct order; and
- normalize divisions introduced by those expansions to inverse/multiply form.

For planning only, opaque `hminus` is replaced by the exact lower endpoint of
its proved enclosure.  The interval is only about `3.1e-16` wide, and the
substitution affects no theorem: the trusted reflected proof continues to use
the complete seven-dimensional `hminus` interval.
