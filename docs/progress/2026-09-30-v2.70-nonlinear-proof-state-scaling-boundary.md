## Checkpoint workflow finding

A checkpoint successfully retained the two completed eight-cell raw results,
but a restored copy exited before evaluating even a small follow-up fragment.
Reloading the combine module failed before its first value was emitted, and a
local test fragment likewise failed before evaluation. This reproduces the
known limitation of retaining very large exported values in an interactive
checkpoint: it is suitable as failure evidence, but not a dependable edit loop.

Future expensive fixtures should keep large results local to the computation
that consumes them. Checkpoints should be placed before that changing fragment,
not after exporting the large theorem/data values.

## Architectural conclusion

The experiments support the architecture proposed in the September research
notes more strongly than another host-tactic micro-optimization:

- keep the proved universal analytic bridge and source reifier;
- represent numerical jobs and topology as ordinary authenticated data;
- have one reflected complete-certificate program consume that data and return
  a compact verdict/result;
- prove one general soundness theorem for that complete program; and
- perform one compact final theorem handoff.

The next prototype should not build a theorem per cell, maintain a growing
host compact stack, or specialize append theorems over decoded certificate
payloads. Bounded execution may still be used internally, but its accumulator
must remain computed data whose invariant is checked by the reflected program,
not a growing host theorem.

## Next boundary

Before another complete multi-hour replay, a small complete-certificate
prototype should process the genuine sixteen-cell fixture through:

1. numerical checks;
2. topology/state updates entirely as computed data;
3. a compact final verdict; and
4. one general soundness theorem application.

It should then be scaled to a larger prefix specifically to test result size,
program size, rational growth, and final handoff cost. Only after those remain
bounded should another 875-cell complete run begin.
