## Discovery now produces a checked untrusted schedule

The earlier roots-0--31 grouping discovery proved the same 32 original roots
through 49 selected final cells, but paid for failed groups while learning
which adjacent roots could safely share preparation.  It reported:

```text
attempts:          28
successful groups: 18
group sizes:        4,4,2,2,2,2,2,2,1,1,2,2,1,1,1,1,1,1
theorem digest:     7f5df8d12a23b58b73fee9e254b83753
```

A new generator consumes that completed discovery log and emits both a JSON
record and an ML schedule fragment.  It fails closed unless the log contains
the terminal success marker and exactly one well-formed result record.  It
also checks positive group sizes, exact root coverage, the reported success
count, and that the attempt count is not smaller than the success count.  The
JSON record binds the schedule to the discovery log's byte length and SHA-256
identity.

The schedule explicitly carries no proof authority.  It only tells the
existing reflected proof adapter how to group roots.  Every selected final
cell is still recomputed, transported to its exact live Flyspeck domain, and
glued through the existing kernel theorems.  Root order, root and cell counts,
the theorem digest, hypotheses, and axiom identity remain fail-closed replay
conditions.

## Measured preparation reuse

The generated replay is compared with the exact sequential replay over the
same roots, cells, and theorem output.  The earlier manually pinned grouped
replay took 655.834 seconds; the 16.1-second difference from the generated
replay is ordinary run variation and is not attributed to schedule
generation.

| Phase | Sequential | Generated groups | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Source preparation | 415.694 s | 232.817 s | -182.877 s |
| Point-plan compilation | 117.165 s | 62.036 s | -55.129 s |
| Final-cell preparation | 110.824 s | 104.952 s | -5.872 s |
| Aggregate reflected proof | 146.323 s | 134.143 s | -12.180 s |
| Source-theorem extraction | 25.447 s | 41.884 s | +16.437 s |
| Live handoff and glue | 44.436 s | 44.875 s | +0.439 s |
| Complete restored process | 885.927 s | 639.734 s | **-246.193 s** |

The generated grouping therefore gives a **1.3848x** end-to-end speedup and
saves **27.79%** on this exact theorem-producing comparison.  It directly
confirms the preparation-amortization hypothesis in the September 25
nonlinear performance research note.  It does not show that every region of
the 1,061-root workload groups equally well.
