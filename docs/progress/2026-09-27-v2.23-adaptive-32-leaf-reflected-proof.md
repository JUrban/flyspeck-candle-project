## Performance interpretation

This materially sharpens the September 25 research-note assessment:

- Loose whole-leaf enclosures are important, but shallow adaptive splitting
  recovers this 32-leaf sample: 29 leaves need at most one split and all 32
  need at most two levels.
- Variant construction and reflected arithmetic are both material.  Their
  costs are comparable on the 60-grandchild depth-two discriminator.
- Theorem-side work is now equally important.  Source extraction plus live
  handoff/gluing took **170.781 seconds**, nearly the aggregate proof time.
  A compact forest/root soundness handoff is therefore a measured target, not
  a speculative micro-optimization.
- Exact variant reuse avoids reconstructing all selected point certificates,
  although the current candidate matching still costs about 22 seconds.
- Fixed evaluator setup, GCD-fuel construction, and the previously tested
  constant/Hessian/product micro-optimizations remain lower priority.

## Next

The architecture is now correct and numerically viable on a nontrivial
genuine sample.  The dominant remaining scaling questions are amortized plan
preparation and compact theorem handoff, not evaluator startup.
