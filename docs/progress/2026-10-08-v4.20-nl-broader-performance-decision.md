## Correct interpretation of the two gaps

The two ratios remain separate:

1. The matched current case16594 specialized CakeML checker is about **24
   times** slower than its exact native numerical counterpart.  Parsing and
   conversion account for only about 3% of checked wall time, so this is
   chiefly a generated representation/execution boundary.
2. The historical approximately **340-times** figure is the *starting*
   generic-fixed-scale-native versus specialized-C++ comparison on case10173.
   It is an architectural warning, not a measured residual for case16594 and
   must not be multiplied by the 24-times ratio.

On the original case10173 fixture, earlier held-axis native work has already
reduced the optimized exact-native numerical residual to about **1.35 times**
the specialized C++ control.  The open algorithmic problem is therefore not
to recover an unexplained 340-times factor on that same fixture.  It is to
transfer and generalize the low-work architecture across independent
Flyspeck expression families without losing enclosure quality or proof
economy.  The negative case16594 historical-dihedral result shows that one
formula block does not transfer automatically.

## Bounded proof decision

The existing proof packet remains deliberately bounded:

- finish the reusable real-semantic refinements for the already successful
  `-d(delta)/dx_i` and `4*x_i*delta` role families;
- retain the general interval-square refinement and shared arctangent
  analytic obligations needed by the complete checker;
- do not add a historical-dihedral theorem, cross-role delta-sharing theorem,
  or expression-specific calculus proof; and
- require the complete 875-box repeat plus an independent expression family
  before enlarging the reusable instruction vocabulary.

## Concrete route to the larger improvement

### 1. Checked-execution gap

The next checked experiment will transfer the exact source-DAG schedule, not
the rejected historical formula.  One authenticated plan compiler should
turn exact repeated source nodes into fixed slots, with certificate-bearing
operations reused only when the exact certificate identity also matches.
The unchanged 875-box payload is the gate.  It must retain fail-closed source,
role, and certificate checks and materially transfer the native 1.589-times
recurring gain.  A weak transfer stops that packet before a large soundness
proof.

The matched complete checker, parser-only executable, and checked-operation
trace then delimit the remaining generated-execution cost.  Measurements will
separate recurring execution, theorem handoff, reusable translation/compile
preparation, peak memory, and required repeats.  Input-format work is not a
priority because its maximum observed payoff is about 3%.

### 2. Algorithmic/subdivision gap

The historical enclosure remains useful only as a forest-level discriminator.
Once the current nonlinear closure supplies stable parent boxes and split
identities, run the exact DAG and historical enclosure on those identical
parents and record:

- parents accepted without splitting;
- visited children and maximum depth;
- total numerical wall time and memory; and
- final certificate acceptance and upper-bound containment.

The historical lane must reduce checked nodes by more than about **21%** to
recover its 1.273-times recurring per-node penalty.  If it does not, abandon
that route.  If it does, repeat on the complete forest and an independent
expression family before proving anything.

The next numerical algorithm candidate after that gate should be a complete
low-work expression plan, not another scalar micro-operation.  Its design
will be taken from the successful case10173 architecture but screened on a
different genuine source DAG and its real subdivision frontier.  Complete
certificate time and acceptance, with preparation charged separately and
amortized, remain decisive.

### 3. Arithmetic representation

The padded-hardware lane is retained only as an upper-bound discriminator.
Its approximately 1.7-times recurring result says a better proved arithmetic
backend may be worthwhile after the graph/plan boundary is stable, but it
cannot by itself meet the orders-of-magnitude objective.  No production proof
will be started from that untrusted lane.

This sequence keeps the successful small specialization proof bounded while
continuing both the formal-execution and algorithmic investigations.  It also
avoids committing proof effort to a faster micro-kernel that does not improve
the complete certificate or the subdivision tree.
