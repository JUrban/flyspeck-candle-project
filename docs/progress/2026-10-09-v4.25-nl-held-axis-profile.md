## Held-axis numerical result

The next native discriminator used the exact same 1,344 genuine boxes visited
by the accepted fifth-sibling historical replay.  Both lanes used the checked
exact mixed-int128/int192 backend, binary scale 40, the same source program,
the same inputs, and the same generic outer evaluator.  The only mathematical
change was replacing three authenticated delta-dihedral source-DAG motifs by
the historical enclosure.  Nine balanced complete repetitions per lane gave:

| Exact lane on the fixed 1,344 boxes | Accepted boxes | Preparation | Evaluation | Batch |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Generic source DAG | 7 | 0.072150 s | 0.463107 s | 0.535257 s |
| Historical dihedral | 682 | 0.071143 s | 0.499936 s | 0.571079 s |

Thus the historical lane is **1.080 times slower in numerical evaluation** and
**1.067 times slower including preparation** on an identical box set, yet
accepts 682 boxes rather than 7.  Its complete-forest win therefore comes from
bound strength and pruning, not cheaper per-box work.  This agrees with the
previous complete replays: checked historical visits 1,344 nodes and completes
in 0.577105 seconds, while checked source DAG visits 9,896 nodes and completes
in 3.976066 seconds, a **6.890-times complete-batch improvement**.

The independent arithmetic axis remains the previous accepted historical
forest comparison: with algorithm and 1,344-node path fixed, padded native
arithmetic was **1.835 times faster** than checked exact arithmetic.  It is
still only an untrusted performance discriminator.

## Keeping the two large gaps separate

The old approximate gaps remain active architectural targets and must not be
folded into the 6.89-times pruning result:

1. **Candle versus native fixed-scale, about 15 times.**  This is chiefly an
   execution/representation target: checked words, evaluator dispatch, data
   movement, allocation/GC, and theorem handoff.
2. **Native generic fixed-scale versus specialized historical C++, about 340
   times.**  This is chiefly an algorithm-and-arithmetic target.  The present
   profile confirms that a strong enclosure can be locally more expensive and
   still win decisively by pruning, but it does not explain away the enormous
   remaining per-box/work gap.

An immediate reuse probe tried the older kernel's existing block rounding for
delta and triangle-`U` polynomials through the newer recognized historical
instruction.  It failed closed on genuine job 0 with `sign-directed delta
value is empty`.  The run is preserved, the one-line experimental gate was
reverted, and the failure was not reclassified as subdivision.  That partial
implementation is therefore not a proof target and does not establish that
wide block arithmetic is unsound in general; it establishes that the old
rounding arrangement cannot simply be reused on the newer broad-box path.

The next cheap discriminator is consequently a native/C++ parity trace on one
identical inequality and box family.  It will align visited boxes, specialized
calls, interval products, directed divisions, transcendental kernels, and
bytes allocated across specialized C++, generic native fixed scale, and the
new historical native instruction.  Existing fixtures and the exact-output
native result keep this a measurement task rather than another architecture
build.  The trace will determine whether a fresh checked-wide block prototype,
a compact whole-expression instruction, or further mathematical
specialization is the next high-leverage experiment.

The feasibility gate is deliberately strict:

- do not formalize the failed inherited block-rounding path;
- if the parity trace attributes a material recurring share to rounding, build
  a fresh checked-wide block prototype with an explicit containment oracle on
  the same 1,344 boxes before specifying or proving it;
- if that prototype gives only a small constant-factor gain, do not formalize
  another arithmetic variant;
- use that parity trace to choose between a compact whole-expression
  instruction, additional generally recognized specialized primitives, and a
  checked narrow-word executor with exact overflow fallback;
- validate any winning route on a second genuine expression family before
  extrapolating to the Flyspeck workload.

The complete-certificate or representative-batch result remains the decision
metric.  Reusable preparation is recorded separately and amortized; a faster
microkernel that weakens bounds or increases subdivision does not pass.
