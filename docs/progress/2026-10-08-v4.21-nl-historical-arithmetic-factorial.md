## Decision

This candidate fails the per-leaf proof gate under both arithmetic models.
The larger exact penalty is partly a representation/rounding effect, but the
remaining 16% padded penalty is strong evidence that the historical
derivative/enclosure path performs intrinsically more work for case16594.
No historical-dihedral instruction or expression-specific calculus proof is
started.

The tighter enclosure remains eligible only for the already planned
forest-level discriminator.  With the proof-relevant exact backend, it must
reduce visited nodes by more than roughly 21% to repay its recurring per-node
penalty.  Stable parent boxes and split identities from the current nonlinear
closure will be used for that test; complete forest acceptance and elapsed
time, rather than isolated bound width, remain decisive.

The broader route is unchanged but now more sharply ordered:

1. finish the bounded verified transfer of exact source-DAG reuse and measure
   its real 875-cell theorem-producing gain;
2. use the stable forest to test whether tighter bounds reduce subdivisions
   enough to outweigh their measured exact per-node cost;
3. prototype the next complete low-work expression plan on an independent
   source family before proving new analytic instructions; and
4. treat a proved bounded-word arithmetic backend as a separate reusable
   target after the plan/data boundary is stable.

The original approximately 15-times encoded/native and 340-times
generic-native/specialized-C++ observations remain useful historical
baselines, but neither is a current universal residual.  The matched current
case16594 checked/native boundary is about 24 times, while controlled native
work has already brought case10173 close to its specialized baseline.  The
objective remains a complete-certificate, amortized orders-of-magnitude
improvement across expression families.
