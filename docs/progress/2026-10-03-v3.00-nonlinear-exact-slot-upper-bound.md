## Interpretation

Simply deleting the exact-slot validation traversals does not accelerate the
current evaluator. The slower result can reflect equation-set layout or
evaluator effects rather than a negative intrinsic cost for validation; it
therefore does not assign a precise isolated cost to either scan. It does,
however, reject the proposed easy optimization: there is no measured basis
for investing in a fused exact-check/patch proof now.

This result, the direct-well-formedness result, and the shared-input encoding
breakdown collectively rule out three tempting structural changes as the
next high-value step. The remaining recurring majority is the certified
Taylor arithmetic and topology computation inside `Kernel.compute`. Further
work should use existing instruction/phase measurements first and add only a
small discriminator that separates genuine Taylor arithmetic stages or
number-growth behavior not already measured.
