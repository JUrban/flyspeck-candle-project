# V4 complete input-closure gap audit — 2026-08-30

## Scope and conclusion

This audit compares the literal v1.3 `input_closure` schema with accepted V4
head `eb198d3`.  No complete input-closure validator or complete field tuple
exists yet.  The accepted entry, inherited-FD and task-identity helpers are
structural prerequisites only.  After one small header/filter fragment,
positive progress requires outside-parent observation; caller-supplied scalar
booleans must not be accepted as substitutes.

## Initial-state blocker

Generic initial-object-edge validation does not provide the missing authority.
The accepted root helper closes the unique setup/input roots, but
`stream-endpoint`, `input-root-entry` and initial `output-root` lack
domain-specific joins.  Bootstrap/runtime VMA edges currently accept a
nonempty authority role and arbitrary index rather than binding retained
runtime authority.  The inherited-FD projection is not joined to stream edges
or initial FD/OFD state.  A complete initial-state seed is therefore still
unsafe to expose.

## Next safe action

The last purely structural leaf should contain exactly
`schema,kind,policy,build_filter`, require the three exact input-closure
constants and exact equality with `enumerate_isolated_native_build_filter_v6`,
and explicitly exclude filter-install observation.  After that leaf, the next
implementation must add genuine outside-parent evidence rather than another
caller-asserted scalar fragment.  All complete closure, seed and outer result
consumers remain fail-closed throughout.
