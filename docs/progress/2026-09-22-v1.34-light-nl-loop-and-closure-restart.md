## The intended nonlinear development split is now operational

Small numerical-representation, whole-calculation, and theorem-handoff
experiments use the post-Taylor `cv-staged-base-checkpoint-v2` through the
fresh-copy harness committed as Flyspeck `c6f32fd4`. A real archived endpoint
fixture completes in 14.23 seconds wall, including restore and startup, while
its theorem-producing calculation uses 0.58 CPU seconds. Every experiment
starts from a fresh read-only reflink copy and must emit its own success
marker, so a failed or partially loaded state is never reused.

This checkpoint is deliberately below the complete nonlinear verifier. It is
already sufficient for exact-number encodings, interval/Taylor arithmetic
plans that use the staged checker, and the boundary from a computed verdict to
an ordinary HOL theorem. Native HOL Light or archived certificates may supply
untrusted exact fixture data; the Candle driver reconstructs and proves the
claimed theorem rather than trusting the fixture's result.

The full nonlinear closure is now reserved for two bounded jobs:

1. capture authentic leaf derivative, Hessian, interval, subdivision, and
   reconstruction payloads for reuse as lightweight fixtures; and
2. validate a coherent candidate end to end from a clean verifier state.

This avoids repeating the hours-long prerequisite load after small experiment
edits without weakening the eventual integration test.
