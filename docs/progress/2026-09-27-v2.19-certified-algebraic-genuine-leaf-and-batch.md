## Consequences for the performance research note

The new evidence sharpens the earlier assessment:

- rational normalization remains the largest measured arithmetic cost;
- retaining fixed results across the final algebraic tail is sound and useful,
  but its measured 1.27x gain is incremental;
- source, certificate, and variant preparation dominates this small cold
  batch and is the clearest amortization target;
- the compact checker already avoids returning full intermediate jets; and
- the next discriminating change is a **generally proved aggregate batch
  boundary** that authorizes several accepted boxes from one computed result.

That aggregate boundary should preserve the current per-expression universal
soundness theorem and exact source/box identities.  Untrusted preparation may
propose grouping and adaptive split axes, while Candle checks the complete
compact verdict and the exact cover before deriving the final Flyspeck
theorem.  Further inverse, square-root, arctangent, conversion, or allocation
micro-optimization should be prioritized from profiles of larger accepted
real batches rather than from source inspection alone.
