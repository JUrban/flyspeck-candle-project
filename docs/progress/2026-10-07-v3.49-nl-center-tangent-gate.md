second-derivative bounds over the box. The native historical-formula lane was
instead constructing a complete second-order jet at both points even though
the center Hessian was discarded.

## Gate decision

The experiment confirms that generic derivative work is measurable, but it
does not explain the large remaining gap. The center-tangent lane is still
4.565 times the 0.082490265-second specialized historical C++ batch and is
slower than the 0.256804446-second best generic dyadic/fused lane. Therefore
there will be no standalone Candle proof for this local specialization.

The optimization is retained as prototype machinery for a larger raw
primitive: its proof becomes worthwhile only if the complete-expression path
reuses it and wins the complete-batch gate materially. This bounds the current
specialization effort rather than allowing a sequence of small local lemmas.

## Concrete larger-gap experiment

The next factorial native experiment works at the complete authenticated-box
boundary:

1. evaluate each top-level formula term into only its center tangent and box
   Hessian, accumulate those data, and perform Taylor completion once;
2. run that compact numerical graph first with the current exact dyadic-shift
   arithmetic, holding representation and rounding fixed;
3. if the graph is materially better, run the same graph with a
   development-only direct outward hardware-endpoint backend to isolate
   arithmetic/rounding cost from derivative/Taylor work;
4. require 4,173/4,173 acceptance, report enclosure changes, memory,
   preparation, recurring execution, and cold complete-batch time, and then
   repeat on a small multi-certificate family before selecting a proof target.

This produces the missing two-factor comparison. It also matches the
historical verifier's raw-primitive boundary more closely than another local
dihedral identity. Exact source/number conversion may be cached as reusable
preparation, but its cost will remain in the cold total and will only be
amortized where the same prepared object is genuinely reused.

The two strategic gaps remain separate. The original approximately 15-fold
Candle/native and approximately 340-fold generic-native/historical-specialized
measurements remain the starting diagnosis. The current best native candidate
has narrowed the latter fixture ratio to 3.113, but it is still an unproved
development result and has not yet reduced end-to-end formal cost. A fresh
Candle/native measurement waits for a native graph that passes the larger
gate; a small local winner is not enough.
