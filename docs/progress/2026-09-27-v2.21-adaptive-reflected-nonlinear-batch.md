## Assessment of the September 25 research note

The source review in
`docs/advice/Candle_NL_Performance_Research_2026-09-25.md` remains useful, but
the current evidence gives its suggestions different priorities.

### Already covered or substantially addressed

- One shared source program and the universal analytic soundness theorem avoid
  per-expression calculus proofs and expanded derivative programs.
- The tagged fixed/rational evaluator already attacks exact-rational growth;
  the earlier genuine 64-box measurement improved the numerical phase by
  **2.56x** and the complete theorem-producing batch by **2.15x**.
- Source preparation, point-plan compilation, per-box certificate variants,
  numerical evaluation, source-theorem extraction, and live handoff are now
  timed separately.
- Several boxes are executed inside one aggregate compute, which returns only
  an acceptance verdict.  Per-cell source facts are logical consequences of
  that verdict, not separate evaluator calls.
- Square-root hint generation is ordinary untrusted exact preparation; the
  checked certificate, source authentication, and general soundness theorem
  remain authoritative.

### Relevant measured costs

- **Reusable preparation is material.**  In this sample, shared source and
  plan preparation took 16.145 seconds, and the 12 per-box variants took about
  10 seconds.  The proof harness currently demonstrates a removable 9.613
  second duplicate variant preparation.
- **Numerical execution remains material.**  The two aggregate discriminator
  calls took 9.001 seconds.  Exact rational normalization and the remaining
  rational inverse, square-root, and arctangent paths therefore remain
  credible targets.
- **Theorem-side reconstruction is not free.**  Extracting 12 source theorems
  took 6.193 seconds and live handoff/gluing took 4.067 seconds.  This is large
  enough to test a direct forest/root soundness handoff, but smaller than the
  combined preparation and numerical work in this sample.
- **Enclosure quality remains decisive.**  Four of the first eight original
  leaves required one extra split, while the next four accepted whole.  The
  adaptive 12-cell batch closes all eight original domains; raw per-box speed
  without acceptance coverage would give a misleading comparison.

### Lower-priority findings at present

- Repeated fixed evaluator setup measured at about 40 ms or below the
  observer's 0.05-second resolution in earlier controls, so a persistent
  compiled evaluator context is not the next bottleneck.
- Focused experiments on the 128-pair GCD fuel, constant completion,
  interval-product sharing, and Hessian-only multiplication were secondary,
  neutral, or regressive.  They should not displace the measured preparation,
  arithmetic, and enclosure-quality work.
- Returning full diagnostic terms did cause avoidable top-level output cost;
  the production aggregate path now returns a compact verdict.  Diagnostics
  remain a development-only option.

## Next discriminating experiments

1. Reuse the exact prepared cell variants between adaptive selection and the
   proof-producing batch, eliminating the measured duplicate preparation.
2. Prove a compact forest/root handoff that consumes the aggregate verdict
   directly, then compare it with the present 6.193-second per-cell source
   extraction plus 4.067-second live handoff/gluing path.
3. Extend the adaptive sample beyond the first eight genuine leaves and charge
   source/plan preparation once.  Report accepted original leaves, final cell
   count, rational/fixed operation mix, integer growth, and memory together.
4. Profile the remaining rational nonlinear instructions (`inv`, `sqrt`, and
   `atn`) before changing their representations.  A fixed-scale refinement is
   worthwhile only if it preserves or improves accepted-box coverage.

This evidence supports the note's central diagnosis without implying that
every source-level opportunity is important.  The remaining gap is a mixture
of reusable preparation, numerical representation/execution, theorem
reconstruction, and subdivision quality; it is not explained by evaluator
setup alone.
