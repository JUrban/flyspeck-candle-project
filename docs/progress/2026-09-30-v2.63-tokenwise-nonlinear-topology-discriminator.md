## Question

The complete reflected nonlinear prover must bound the amount of retained HOL
term structure in every step. Four-cell batching crosses the earlier retention
failure, but a measured authentic segment spent about as much time reducing
compact topology as it spent in `Kernel.compute`. Could topology be reduced one
postorder token at a time while preserving the accepted-job and active-stack
invariants, thereby reducing both live term size and recurring time?

## Measured result

Measurements were synchronized from fresh copies of the same sealed
predecessor. Times below are externally observed wall times between flushed
phase markers.

| Phase | Existing seven-token batch | Seven one-token proofs |
|---|---:|---:|
| certificate encoding | 1.391 s | 1.394 s |
| proof preparation | 0.000 s | 0.000 s |
| numerical `Kernel.compute` | 96.388 s | 97.446 s |
| theorem handoff | 1.184 s | 1.181 s |
| compact validation | 0.001 s | included per token |
| compact reduction | 30.371 s | 20.110 s total |
| per-token soundness composition | not repeated | 39.285 s total |
| profiler start through proof verdict | 152.309 s | 186.074 s |
| restore, proof, and sealed checkpoint | 183.55 s | 216.60 s |
| peak resident memory | 2,253,076 KiB | 2,253,076 KiB |

Tokenwise rewriting saved 10.261 seconds in compact reduction, but repeated
theorem instantiation, conjunction construction, and `MATCH_MP` reconstruction
cost 39.285 seconds. The final wall time regressed by 33.05 seconds, or about
18.0%, and memory did not improve.

The composition cost also decreases token by token as the accepted-job theorem
shrinks: 10.189, 7.682, 6.378, 5.239, 4.080, 3.293, and 2.423 seconds. This is
direct evidence that repeatedly rebuilding the evolving invariant, rather than
the numerical checker, accounts for the apparent saving being lost.

## Decision

Do not replace four-cell batch topology with per-token proof boundaries. Keep
the active complete run and its theorem architecture unchanged. A future
topology optimization should retain one soundness composition per bounded
batch while reducing the 30-second batch rewrite, rather than multiply
soundness reconstruction across individual tokens.

The complete 875-cell run remains the current correctness milestone. Its exact
postflight will validate the final source theorem, digest, assumptions, axiom
set, segment sequence, and sealed late checkpoint before any performance
redesign is promoted.
