## The held-axis question was already partly closed

The complete 875-box case16594 factorial had already crossed the two requested
axes while holding source, boxes, final predicate, and scale fixed:

- generic source DAG versus historical derivative/enclosure structure; and
- checked exact versus padded hardware arithmetic.

The historical structure was locally 1.16--1.27 times slower but materially
tighter.  At forest level its tighter bounds reduced the genuine fifth-sibling
search from 9,896 to 1,344 visited nodes and produced a 6.890-times complete
batch improvement.  On that same accepted 1,344-node historical path, padded

new factorial therefore holds the historical algorithm, source program,

changes only the hardware backend's outward quotient padding.  Its unsafe

not the next numerical experiment.

The historical observations remain separate architectural reference points:

1. approximately **15 times** from the earlier native exact computation to

2. approximately **340 times** from the original generic native fixed-scale

already changed the algorithm, enclosure strength, data flow, and arithmetic,

## Next cheap native discriminator

The current historical path evaluates three authenticated permuted dihedral
motifs independently.  Each invocation recomputes the same tetrahedrally
symmetric delta value, gradient, Hessian, and square root at the center and
over the box.  The next bounded experiment will hold checked exact arithmetic,
outward rounding, the historical formulas, boxes, and final Taylor contract
fixed while sharing that delta jet across the three motifs:

1. compute one center delta jet and one box delta jet;
2. transport them through the already authenticated self-inverse coordinate
   permutations;
3. retain the coordinate-specific `delta_x4`, triangle-`U`, denominator,
   arctangent, and Taylor work; and
4. require identical pruning classifications, complete forest acceptance,
   bound containment, preparation time, recurring time, and memory on the
   same 1,344-node path and its streaming replay.

This changes derivative data flow while holding arithmetic fixed, complementing
the rounding factorial.  It directly targets the profile in which the three
historical instructions account for 53.4% of directed quotients and 42.9% of
profiled time.  If sharing produces only a small constant-factor improvement,
it will remain an unproved native result and attention will move to the
verified execution/representation boundary.  A material complete-forest win
must also survive a second genuine expression family before it becomes a new
proof target.
