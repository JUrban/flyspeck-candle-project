## Result

The crossing-zero branch required by real residual Flyspeck `matan` sources
now has a reusable exact-rational proof layer.  It computes interval data for
the function, first derivative, and second derivative and proves all three
results by instantiating Flyspeck's existing analytic contracts:

- `Matan_eval.matan_abs1`;
- `Matan_eval.dmatan_abs1`; and
- `Matan_eval.ddmatan_ge_neg02`.

The supported packet is fail closed.  It requires an ordered input interval,
lower endpoint at least `-0.2`, absolute upper bound at most `0.9`, and
nonzero Taylor-remainder denominators.  The implementation uses exact
rational polynomial bounds and remainder data; it does not replace Flyspeck's
analytic argument or introduce per-expression calculus proofs.

The proof scripts instantiate each finite contract premise explicitly.
Broad final `MESON` calls were removed after focused trials showed exponential
search growth.  The resulting proofs are deterministic and reusable across
boxes.
