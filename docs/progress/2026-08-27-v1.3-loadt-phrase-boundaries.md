## Implemented behavior

- `#flyspeck_loadt "literal";;` is a standalone, exact source action.  It
  requires a manifest identity, evaluates every occurrence, records the
  logical identity only after success, and never neutralizes state.
- Ordinary `needs`, `loads`, and `#use` are recognized as loader actions only
  at a true top-level phrase start.  Tokens inside definitions, function
  bodies, and conditionals remain parser input.
- A loaded source ending in a declaration without trailing `;;` is submitted
  before EOF; the parent scanner then resumes at a fresh phrase boundary.
- The exact strictbuild normalization converts its three standalone `loadt`
  phrases to `#flyspeck_loadt` and makes the legacy dynamic `use_file_b`,
  `needs`, and `reneeds` bindings fail explicitly if reached.

The selected static route must still prove those disabled helpers are unused.
No `Toploop` shim, no successful no-op, and no blanket directive rewrite is
introduced.
