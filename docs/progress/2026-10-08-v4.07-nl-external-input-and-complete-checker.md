## Corrected verified-program basis

The first external-input executables exposed a harness defect rather than a
numerical result.  The bounded-compute translation root extended only
`Word64Prog`; programs that quoted `TextIO.inputAll`, `TextIO.print`, or
`CommandLine.arguments` therefore compiled without those basis functions in
their translation environment and crashed.  Those executions are rejected
as performance evidence.

CakeML commit `068a2a3fe` makes `basisProg` the one canonical translation root
for the bounded checker family.  A complete 26-theory rebuild and verified
x64 compilation passed in 49:39.57 wall time with 8,167,336 KiB peak RSS.
The resulting PIE executable rejects empty input normally, confirming that
link mode was not the cause of the earlier failures.

## Streaming fail-closed parser

CakeML commit `70af8d939` adds a single-pass character-list parser.  It avoids
the quadratic risk of repeated indexed access to list-backed `mlstring` data
and validates the header, cell/record counts, widths, tags, flags, signs,
unused padding, exact record count, and exact end-of-input.

At a 512 MiB CakeML heap setting, the two genuine packed fixtures both
accepted:

| fixture | elapsed | peak RSS |
|---|---:|---:|
| first | 0.38 s | 401,408 KiB |
| second | 0.47 s | 400,512 KiB |

A 128 MiB setting exhausted the heap in 0.19 seconds at 132,608 KiB RSS.  The
parser is therefore cheap in elapsed time but its materialized input string
has a nontrivial live-memory cost.  This is recorded separately from numerical
execution.

Truncation, trailing data, magic drift, invalid tag, invalid flags, invalid
sign encoding, inverse padding drift, and arctangent padding drift all
rejected with normal exit status zero.

## Complete streaming checker

CakeML commit `490757508` connects each decoded cell directly to the
authenticated 167-instruction prepared program and its 31 decoded scalar
certificates.  It retains neither an 875-cell decoded list nor a trusted
verdict.  Acceptance requires:

1. exact parse and certificate consumption;
2. successful total checked-word execution;
3. a well-formed Taylor result and valid domain;
4. a strictly negative completed upper bound for every cell; and
5. exact end-of-input after cell 875.

The logical checker theory passed in 40.26 seconds with 913,096 KiB peak RSS.
Cold translation of the reusable prepared plan plus combined parser/executor
took 6:26.24 with 7,992,560 KiB peak RSS.  Verified x64 compilation took a
further 3:52.45 with 5,437,592 KiB peak RSS.  These are reusable preparation
costs and must not be charged once per cell or certificate.

The unchanged genuine batch then failed closed in the first cell.  A narrow
diagnostic distinguishes parse failure, executor/certificate failure, invalid
domain, and final-bound failure.  Its result was:

```text
rejected:first-bound
elapsed=0.10 user=0.03 system=0.06 maxrss_kib=93184 exit=0
```

Thus the first cell parses, its 31 certificates support successful complete
execution, and its domain is valid, but the compact checker's completed upper
bound is not strictly negative.  This preserves the failure and prevents an
invalid performance claim.

## Performance interpretation

The existing held-axis native experiment already separates two costs on all
875 genuine boxes while preserving exact bound streams:

- generic versus authenticated data flow costs about 2.06--2.14x;
- Boost multiprecision versus built-in fixed-width endpoints costs about
  1.90--1.97x; and
- both changes together cost 4.06x.

That result explains only a small fraction of the larger architectural gap.
The new complete-checker failure identifies an earlier correctness boundary:
the current compact multiplication rounds individual product-rule terms
before summing them, whereas the winning native lane accumulates wide raw
products and rounds once after aggregation.  Enclosure tightness must be
restored before evaluator overhead can be measured on an accepted batch.

This does not collapse the two historical gaps.  The roughly 15x
Candle/native gap and the much larger generic-native/specialized-C++ gap remain
separate questions.  The exact case16594 comparison cannot yet be stated as a
complete-checker ratio because the Candle lane correctly rejects instead of
finishing the batch.
