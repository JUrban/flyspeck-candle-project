## Performance interpretation

These regional replays are the representative multi-box experiment suggested
by the September 25 performance note: they use varied genuine boxes, preserve
the actual adaptive-cell factor, charge source/plan preparation, perform the
proved fixed/rational computation, and include theorem handoff.  They therefore
provide a more useful end-to-end discriminator than another isolated GCD-fuel,
constant-node, or evaluator-setup microbenchmark.

The sequential adapter holds memory constant, but the 40--48 minute regional
times show that source preparation, numerical checking, and theorem handoff
all remain material.  The complete scan and full 1,061-root proof will supply
the right scope for deciding whether to extend bounded-scale arithmetic
further into the remaining rational nonlinear operations.
