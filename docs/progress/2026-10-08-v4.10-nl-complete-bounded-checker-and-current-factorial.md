## The last genuine failure and its general repair

The previous complete checker was not failing on the first cell.  Its
natural-number status diagnostic was being observed through the wrong runtime
representation.  A word8 diagnostic showed that the first cell passed, and a
complete word8 localizer found the first real failure at zero-based cell 220,
instruction 82, the sixth arctangent.

An independent native trace and the verified executor matched through
instruction 81.  The failing arctangent input bound was

```text
[-33939760703, 372457639893] / 2^40.
```

The old implementation formed `x*x` through generic interval multiplication.
Because that operation treats its two operands independently, a mixed-sign
interval acquires a negative lower endpoint even when both operands denote the
same value.  Adding one then produced a needlessly weak denominator and
correctly rejected the supplied reciprocal certificate.

The repair is a general checked interval-square operation:

- a nonnegative or nonpositive interval retains the established directed
  multiplication path;
- an interval crossing zero receives exact lower endpoint zero and directed
  upper endpoint `max(lower^2, upper^2)`; and
- invalid inputs and every arithmetic overflow still return `NONE`.

The arctangent Taylor instruction uses this square operation at its three
correlated-square sites.  It introduces no instruction-index, cell, source
basename, or case16594 numerical exception.  Closed HOL tests cover positive,
negative, and genuine crossing intervals and the cell-220 reciprocal
certificate.

After rebuilding the complete dependency path, the cell-220 discriminator
returned byte zero three times in 0.20--0.21 seconds.  Byte zero requires all
167 complete stack-state hashes to match the independent native trace, the
31 certificates to be consumed exactly, and the terminal upper bound to be
negative.  The full 875-cell results above then passed without another
arithmetic change.

## Bounded next packet and larger-gap plan

The next specialization packet remains deliberately small:

1. integrate only the three `4*x_i*delta` and three negative delta-derivative
   roles already authenticated by exact source identity and proved integer
   semantics;
2. lift those roles through the existing compact Taylor interface, with one
   general correspondence per role family rather than per-expression calculus
   proofs;
3. rerun the unchanged 875-cell payload and require complete acceptance,
   source-authenticated dispatch, sound outward bounds, and a material
   complete-batch improvement; and
4. stop that proof packet if the native approximately 2.05-times graph factor
   does not transfer.

The general interval-square refinement and shared arctangent analytic theorem
remain reusable release obligations.  No rejected inverse-root or whole-chain
specialization is reopened.

After the six-role batch is measured, compare its actual execution directly
with the compiled operation trace.  If list/stack/state traversal still costs
materially more than the trace, the next gate is one authenticated fixed-layout
plan with fixed six/21 component state and coarse generally proved
instructions.  If the actual run is already near the trace, do not spend more
proof effort on layout; profile the bounded-word operations instead.

The operation trace itself is still about 14.8 times slower than exact native
evaluation, whereas the present native arithmetic/rounding ceiling is only
about 1.8 times on the authenticated graph.  Therefore a hundredfold total
gain cannot responsibly be projected from scalar rounding alone.  Reaching it
will require some combination of:

- fewer generally justified Taylor/derivative operations;
- a lower-overhead verified representation or execution plan; and
- reusable specialized numerical blocks whose complete-certificate enclosure
  and acceptance first win in native experiments.

The next native algorithm experiment will be chosen from the post-lowering
profile, not in advance.  It must hold exact arithmetic and the final Taylor
contract fixed while replacing one dominant common block, and must pass the
complete 875-cell batch before proof work begins.  A second genuine expression
family remains mandatory before enlarging the general instruction vocabulary.

This keeps the current proof effort bounded while maintaining a concrete route
toward the larger objective.  Preparation, recurring execution, theorem
handoff, required repeats, memory, bound quality, and complete-certificate
acceptance remain separate decision measures.
