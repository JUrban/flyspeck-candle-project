pristine collector, or PFT oracle.  They wait on the CakeML cold proof,
deliberate Candle repin/regeneration, canonical bootstrap and schema-6 link.

all per-target success receipts, independent approval and finalization.  At
the audit checkpoint the live immutable sweep was 107/130, 23 pending, one
retained interrupted attempt, `closed=false`, `candidates_unapproved`, and
`promotion_allowed=false`.  Continue its existing controller; do not restart
or parallelize the contractually serial sweep.

Direct S2/S3 approval separately waits for the V4 pristine collector and
receipt path plus two nonce-distinct reference runs.  The accepted V4 design
and current positive leaf validators create no execution or candidate
evidence yet.  This blocks pristine comparison/approval and release, not the
parser diagnostics.

PFT completion blocks none of the direct gates.  The active run is an
inadmissible P1 oracle and differential aid, never S2/S3 evidence.

## Resources and valid acceleration

Do not overlap the final canonical replay with another `Holmake`; its
prelaunch gate requires no live `Holmake` and at least 120 GiB
`MemAvailable`.  Historical canonical bootstrap expectation is roughly eight
hours and 72 GiB peak RSS, with a 110-GiB aggregate alert and the
user-authorized exceptional ceiling of 120 GiB.

Each parser attempt is serial and independently limited to 16 GiB.  Formal
worst cases are 3.3 hours for 20 and 66.7 hours for 400, although the pilot is
intended to produce a much tighter measured estimate before the 400 run.

The useful external speed advice is already reflected here: use saved failed
HOL heaps and warm dependency traversal for development; batch front-end
failures; require the 20-node gate before spending on 400.  `-j2`, native
cache, x64 specialization and theory splitting remain development
experiments.  None substitutes for the serial cache-disabled final replay or
its receipts, and the contractually serial reference sweep must not be
parallelized.
