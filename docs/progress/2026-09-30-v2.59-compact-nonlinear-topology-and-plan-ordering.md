## First complete-run failure and diagnosis

The initial 875-cell numerical run passed chunks 0--11 (384 cells) and then
rejected chunk 12. Bisection localized the first rejection to planned cell
399. A direct stable/variable/raw comparison showed that authenticated engine
leaf 398 itself accepted and that the variable and raw encodings were equal.

The apparent contradiction was a plan identity bug. The plan builder mutated
`next_leaf` inside both arguments of a tree constructor. Runtime argument
evaluation visited siblings in the opposite order from the later
left-to-right cell flattening, so the 15 requested fallback splits were
attached to the wrong logical leaves. In particular, planned cell 399 was
not the claimed whole leaf 398; the intended split of leaf 388 had not been
made, and the raw checker correctly rejected that unsplit numerical cell.

The repair removes the mutable sibling-order dependency. The recursive
builder now threads the next ordinal explicitly: it completes the left
subtree, passes the returned ordinal into the right subtree, and returns the
final ordinal with the shape.
