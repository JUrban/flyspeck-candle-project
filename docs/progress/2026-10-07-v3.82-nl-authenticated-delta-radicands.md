## The held-axis experiment

The three expensive 85-step polynomial payloads in the authenticated
case16594 source are exactly:

```text
4 * x1 * delta(x)
4 * x2 * delta(x)
4 * x3 * delta(x)
```

The native harness now recognizes these by decoding the postfix payload into
an exact rational multivariate polynomial.  It dispatches the specialized
formula only after equality with `4 * x[coordinate] * delta` is established.
For case16594, preparation additionally requires exactly one match for each
of coordinates 1, 2, and 3 and none for the others.  No outer index, basename,
or inequality identifier authorizes the path.

The specialized evaluator computes the delta value, gradient, and Hessian
once and applies the general product identities for
`4 * x[coordinate] * delta`.  It is parameterized by the coordinate; it is
not three handwritten expression implementations.  The existing exact-mixed
and padded-hardware backends, `2^40` grid, surrounding analytic graph, boxes,
and acceptance test remain unchanged.  This directly separates analytic
data-flow work from arithmetic representation and rounding.

## Proof and larger-gap decision

This result clears a bounded proof-interest gate: `4*x_i*delta` is a reusable
expression family, the source recognizer is general, the local numerical win
is large, and three real occurrences improve a complete certificate by about
46 percent.  A proof packet is justified if it stays limited to:

1. a general polynomial/source-recognition theorem;
2. the coordinate-parameterized value/gradient/Hessian identities; and
3. one containment theorem connecting the instruction to the universal jet
   evaluator.

It must not grow into three per-expression calculus proofs.  The completed
general radix-22 word-multiply proof remains the current executor proof
packet; the analytic instruction proof can start after the 39-step paired
experiment confirms the right combined boundary.

The larger performance problems remain distinct:

- the historical approximately **340-times** result diagnosed the original
  generic-native versus specialized-C++ architecture on case10173; later
  controlled native work reduced that fixture's residual to about 1.35
  times;
- the present exact algorithm change improves a different real family, but
  its optimized native batch is now faster, so the unchanged 225.8-second
  reflected batch is about **140 times** slower than the 1.614-second
  recurring candidate; and
- the proved 64-bit product is promising, but only a real 128/192 mixed trace
  and then theorem-producing all-cell execution can reduce that formal
  execution gap.

The concrete path to an orders-of-magnitude improvement is therefore
factorized rather than assigned to one trick:

1. finish the authenticated delta numerator/radicand family and screen one
   whole dihedral instruction in native code;
2. finish the proved 128/192 multiply, shift, narrowing, and fallback trace in
   the actual operation mix;
3. give winning analytic instructions general containment theorems;
4. give the bounded executor one decode/refinement theorem with conversion at
   the batch boundary rather than per arithmetic operation; and
5. decide on full 875-cell theorem time and then confirm a second real family.

Complete-certificate acceptance, reusable preparation, theorem handoff,
memory, and total elapsed time remain the gates.  Neither the native result
nor the separate compiled CakeML microbenchmark is release evidence.
