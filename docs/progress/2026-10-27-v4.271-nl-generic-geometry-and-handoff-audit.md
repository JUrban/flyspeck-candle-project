## Geometry correction

The generic checker previously validated the lengths and endpoint ordering of
the packed box and center environments but did not validate their relationship
to the supplied Taylor radii. That was insufficient for the analytic handoff:
an untrusted producer could pair a successful center calculation with
unrelated box or radius data.

CakeML commit `6a9272048` adds a generic fail-closed geometry guard. For every
coordinate it requires:

1. valid box and center intervals;
2. the center interval lies inside the box; and
3. expanding the center interval by the supplied radius covers the box.

Checked signed additions reject overflow. A proved integer form removes the
word-level branches and exposes the four exact ordering/coverage inequalities.
The all-cell object-semantic theorem now retains those inequalities for every
accepted packed cell instead of discarding geometry after evaluation.

The genuine fixture contains 5,250 coordinate triples. All 5,250 are exact
expansions, not merely covered. The fresh checker accepted all 875 cells in
**7.92 seconds** at **1,050,112 KiB** peak RSS. Reducing only the first radius
by one word unit rejected in **0.08 seconds**. Evidence is under
`/project/flyspeck-candle-runs/v728-nl-generic-geometry-gate-dev-001`.

Focused logical builds remained 28–68 seconds. The exact packed-checker
translation rebuilt in 224.15 seconds. No kernel, REPL, compiler, bootstrap,
checkpoint load, or runtime rebuild ran.

## Exact analytic handoff found

The existing HOL Light library already contains the reusable low-level
analytic result needed for a non-point center representation:
`candle_fs_result_complete_proxy_analytic_contains_box`. Its premises use an
arbitrary center point `y`, nonnegative radii, `m_cell_domain`, center
value/gradient containment, and whole-box Hessian containment. Therefore the
next proof need not redevelop Taylor calculus or force the packed center
interval to be a single point.

The remaining bridge is now explicit:

1. bind each original rational source box to a point `y` contained by the
   packed center interval;
2. derive `m_cell_domain` and source-box containment from the new packed
   geometry plus the source-box correspondence;
3. identify the accepted object-q program result with the fixed-scale Taylor
   result consumed by the existing analytic theorem; and
4. lift all accepted leaf results through the existing authenticated
   case-16594 subdivision/topology plan to its literal original theorem.

The fourth item matters: a declared count of 875 accepted numerical cells is
not itself a proof that those cells cover the original root domain. The old
complete case-16594 route separately authenticates and consumes its topology.
That adapter can be reused, but it must be connected explicitly rather than
being inferred from cell count or fixture identity.
