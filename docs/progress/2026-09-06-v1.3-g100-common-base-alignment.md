The full raw records are not yet identical.  Structural decoding localizes
the remaining difference completely to generated identifiers:

- all 300 ordinary term-constant names are shared;
- three anonymous generated constants have identical canonical type
  signatures and pair as `_22929`/`_22943`, `_103338`/`_100472`, and
  `_103357`/`_100491` (Candle/native);
- twelve generated type-variable names differ, with every numeric suffix in
  Candle exactly 10,574 above its native counterpart; and
- after alpha-normalizing those generated type variables and applying the
  three signature-unique anonymous-constant pairs, the complete term-
  declaration and primitive-definition multisets are structurally equal.

Thus the intended logical base contents are now aligned, but the current raw
serializer contract still observes implementation-dependent generated names.
No exact-state claim is made from alpha-equivalence.
