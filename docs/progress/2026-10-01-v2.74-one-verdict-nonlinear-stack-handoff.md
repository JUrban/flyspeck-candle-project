## Performance interpretation

For this 16-cell sample, the theorem handoff is only about 2.5% of the combined
compute time. The measured recurring bottleneck is therefore still evaluator
execution of the numerical checker, not reconstruction of the compact result.
The architecture nevertheless removes the former per-leaf theorem-retention
failure and provides the intended small verdict-to-theorem boundary.

The next meaningful performance experiment should use more than one genuine
segment with expression preparation amortized once. Optimization should focus
on the computed arithmetic representation and evaluator work while retaining
this compact proof boundary.
