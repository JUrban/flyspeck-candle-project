`Generator.notame` decision after the already-green authentic seed and first
successor batch.  The reflected predicate rejects exactly when these two
conditions do not both hold:

- `countVertices <= 15`; and
- AFP `tame11b`: each vertex has face degree at most 7 when no incident final
  face has size at least 5, and at most 6 otherwise.

representation.  Focused `Kernel.compute` fixtures establish:

- the authentic `p = 0` seed is retained;
- ordinary degree 7 is retained;
- ordinary degree 8 is rejected;

- a 16-vertex graph is rejected.
