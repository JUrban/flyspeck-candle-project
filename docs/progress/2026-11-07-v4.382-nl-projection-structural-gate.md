## Dependency/version finding

The sealed v490 checkpoint contains the early ten-definition version of
`Candle_cv_flyspeck_direct_source_compat`.  The complete October source census
explicitly reloaded the later compatibility module before scanning.  An
initial version of this new driver omitted that reload and therefore exposed
`rotate2`, `rotate3`, `const1`, and `sol0` as apparent additional residuals.

A one-record discriminator established that the selected parameter formula
and the whole prepared-source formula are alpha-equivalent and have identical
rendered length.  Reloading the current compatibility module made the exact
parameter reproduce the prior result: only `hminus` remained.  The final
driver now declares this dependency explicitly rather than weakening its
expected residual set.

This was a diagnostic state-version issue, not a new source-language family
and not a theorem failure.  The failed disposable runs are preserved under
`nl-stock-projection-sample-structure-v1-dev-001` through `dev-011`; the
successful one-record discriminator is `dev-012` and the final complete gate
is `dev-013`.

## What this closes—and what it does not

This closes the cheap preflight requested before a timed projection run:

- exact archive identities map back to the production direct-source path;
- the supported sample does not hide a new expression-language residual;
- the unsupported sample fails in the already measured five families; and
- repeated sample records can reuse source preparation safely within one live
  session.

It does **not** establish numerical acceptance, current Candle certificate
topology, stock-route throughput, or the complete-NL CPU/RAM projection.  The
next bounded step is to build one reusable prepared stock boundary and time a
representative supported subset, keeping restore/preparation, certificate
search, `Kernel.compute`, theorem handoff, reconstruction, and peak RSS
separate.  Unsupported records remain explicit rather than being silently
excluded from the final coverage fraction.
