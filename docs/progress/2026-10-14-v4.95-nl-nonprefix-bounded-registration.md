## Interpretation

The temporary exact-registration bridge is not prefix-specific: one indexed
non-prefix chunk now passes through the same proof-producing boundary.  This
supports generating a complete aligned chunk ledger if needed to close the
first original inequality.

It does **not** yet produce the original case-10173 Flyspeck theorem.  Before
spending roughly 32 CPU-hours on all 261 chunks, the next discriminator should
prove and exercise the actual Candle theorem handoff for a bounded chunk and
consume that equality through
`candle_case10173_hist_checked_jobs_source_sound`.  That establishes that the
chunk equalities can be assembled into the intended source-level theorem,
rather than merely proving many detached evaluator results.

The reusable packed-checker refinement remains the production objective.  It
is the route that can use the complete 10.24-second numerical run without
repeating generic evaluation or carrying hundreds of exact call constants.
