## Replay and evidence policy

Each emitted program is an exact byte prefix of
`candle/flyspeck_full_build.ml` ending at a stratum boundary.  No suffix program
is emitted.  A retry or later boundary must start in a fresh process and replay
its authenticated cumulative prefix from action zero.  The plan explicitly
records that no CakeML process/kernel state was captured and that suffix launch
and checkpoint-replay claims are unauthorized.

The separate host schedule template may record only scheduling states.  Those
states can never advance S2/S3.  A future compiled attempt must independently
bind the provenance-checked Candle executable and runtime roots, observe every
ordered action through the boundary, exit successfully, and supply the required
semantic fingerprints.
