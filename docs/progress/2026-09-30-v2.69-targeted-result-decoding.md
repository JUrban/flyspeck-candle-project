## Result

Candle commit `ef46b802` is pushed on
`codex/cv-nonlinear-disjunctive-v1`.

The compact proof adapter now makes two narrow theorem-construction changes:

1. after applying the universal result decoder, it reduces the encoded
   logical side with the general left-inverse theorem and reduces only the
   concrete computed side with the concrete decoder rules; and
2. after confirming that the constructed premise is exactly alpha-equivalent
   to the soundness theorem's antecedent, it uses direct kernel `MP` rather
   than running the general `MATCH_MP` matcher over the same large terms.

Neither change weakens validation. The computed result still has to satisfy
the complete-consumption/single-root shape, the decoded run theorem must have
no assumptions, and the complete soundness premise is compared exactly before
`MP` is called.

## Development-harness finding

Two attempts to reload a replacement adapter into a checkpoint retaining the
large exported prefix-8 fixture exited before evaluation. A separate attempt
computed the large raw fixture successfully but exited while the interactive
loader was pretty-printing that value. Neither reached the candidate proof.

The quiet combined harness keeps raw cells, theorem, topology, and result
local to one top-level unit. It completed and checkpointed reliably, so future
large fixture tests should use that shape rather than exporting enormous
interactive values solely for reuse.
