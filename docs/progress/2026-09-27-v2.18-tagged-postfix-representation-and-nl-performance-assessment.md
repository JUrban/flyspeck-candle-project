## Assessment of the September 25 performance note

The source review in
`docs/advice/Candle_NL_Performance_Research_2026-09-25.md` usefully separated
numerical work, evaluator setup, reusable preparation, and theorem handoff.
Subsequent measurements now classify its proposed explanations:

- **Material and being addressed:** exact-rational arithmetic and
  normalization.  The proved fixed-scale hybrid reduced the genuine 64-box
  `Kernel.compute` phase from 161.3204 to 63.0574 seconds (**2.56x**) and the
  complete theorem-producing batch from 180.7978 to 83.9137 seconds
  (**2.15x**).
- **Already amortized or repaired:** one internal multi-box compute call, one
  compact verdict, shared source programs, exact ordinary generation of
  untrusted square-root hints, compiled point-expression plans, universal
  analytic soundness, certificate erasure, and exact split-tree handoff.
- **Measured secondary or negative:** the 128-pair GCD fuel, constant/variable
  completion, explicit interval-product sharing, and a Hessian-only
  multiplication projection.  The proposed sharing rewrite regressed and the
  Hessian-only rewrite had no stable throughput benefit.
- **Still credible:** remaining rational inverse/square-root/arctangent work,
  fixed/rational conversions, dense list/pair traversal and allocation, and
  per-box certificate/variant preparation.
- **Equally important:** enclosure quality.  A real eight-leaf sample accepted
  only one leaf without further refinement.  Extra subdivisions can erase a
  per-box speedup, so acceptance coverage must be measured with runtime.

The next useful performance experiment is therefore a varied real-box batch
through the completely proved hybrid.  It should charge shared preparation
once; split certificate generation, variant theorem construction, program/job
encoding, `Kernel.compute`, and theorem handoff; and report acceptance plus
extra subdivisions.  Repeated evaluator setup is not a current priority: the
controls measured about 40 ms or below the 0.05-second observer resolution.
