## General exact refinement

The bounded-word branch now composes its already proved arbitrary `word64`
full product with the exact dyadic floor operation used by the genuine
case16594 endpoint path.  For arbitrary inputs, HOL4 proves:

```text
bounded128_to_num (bounded_word64_mul_shift_floor40 left right)
  = (w2n left * w2n right) DIV 2^40
```

The proof is not a finite input check.  It proves the cross-word movement of
the high product's low 40 bits, the low product's upper 24 bits, the new high
word, the absence of low-word overflow, and composition with the general
radix-22 full-product theorem.

The translated closed program repeats that exact product-plus-shift primitive
10,000,001 times, folds both result words into a digest, and exits successfully
only if the exact expected digest is obtained.  CakeML's verified translator
and verified x64 compiler both accept the program.
