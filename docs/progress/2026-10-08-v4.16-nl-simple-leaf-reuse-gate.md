## Question

The case16594 source repeats many exact constant, variable, and `pi/2` leaves.
The generic Taylor executor reconstructed and completed each occurrence even
when an identical immutable result already existed in the same box.  This
packet asked whether exact source-node reuse is a material part of the
remaining whole-expression cost.

The candidate is deliberately general and semantic rather than positional:

- source polynomials must first classify exactly as a rational constant or
  one of the six variables;
- constants are keyed by exact rational value, variables by coordinate, and
  `pi/2` by its logical instruction;
- the cache is fresh for each box, so variable results cannot cross box or
  environment identity;
- a miss invokes the unchanged Taylor constructor; a hit reuses that exact
  immutable result; and
- no approximate equality, basename, instruction index, or numerical alias
  authorizes reuse.

This changes neither arithmetic nor rounding.  The cached and uncached bound
streams are required to be byte-identical.

## Decision and larger-gap direction

No separate expression-specific proof packet is started for this cache.  The
durable formal route is to let the authenticated fixed-layout plan compiler
hash-cons exact logical leaves and prove that evaluating a shared immutable
node is extensionally equal to re-evaluating it.  Plans with no repeated node
must not pay a dynamic map/cache cost.

The next cheap native screen moves one level up rather than extending the
cache blindly:

1. construct an exact source-DAG/reuse census for case16594 after the current
   six delta roles and leaf lowering;
2. identify repeated nontrivial subexpressions and the common delta,
   gradient/Hessian, and three-angle tetrahedron data that can be shared;
3. estimate removable interval products, Taylor completions, and nonlinear
   calls before implementing a candidate;
4. prototype only a shared block with a credible material complete-batch
   ceiling, initially under the unchanged exact dyadic arithmetic;
5. require 875/875 acceptance, bound-quality accounting, and a substantial
   all-box speedup; and
6. validate any enlarged instruction vocabulary on an independent genuine
   family before proof expansion.

The separate approximately 24-times complete verified/native case16594 gap
still belongs to the fixed-layout checked-word executor.  Exact leaf sharing
can become a small lowering optimization there, but it does not replace the
representation/code-generation work.  Likewise, the historical roughly
340-times generic-native/specialized-C++ starting comparison remains an
algorithmic warning, not a residual to multiply by the Candle/native ratio.
