## Decision

The exact-algorithm gate is negative.  Per the predeclared staging, there is
no padded-hardware cross and no soundness-proof expansion for this candidate.
The isolated executable and evidence are retained because they establish two
useful facts:

1. the historical enclosure generalizes safely to all three real angle roles
   and greatly improves enclosure quality; but
2. splicing it into the current fixed certificate performs more numerical
   work per accepted leaf than the exact DAG.

This does not refute the historical specialized C++ result.  That verifier's
roughly 340-times starting advantage combined specialized formulas,
arithmetic, and a different subdivision/search architecture.  The present
experiment changes only three angle enclosures inside case16594's already
fixed 875-leaf certificate.  The two measurements must remain separately
labelled.

## Larger-gap direction

The next algorithmic measurement should include subdivision, rather than
rewarding tighter bounds only descriptively.  Once the current nonlinear
forest closure exposes stable parent boxes and split identities, a bounded
native replay can compare DAG and historical enclosures on the same parent
frontier and report:

- accepted parents and leaves visited;
- total numerical work through the complete frontier;
- bound margins at rejected and accepted parents; and
- preparation, total elapsed time, and memory.

That experiment can determine whether the 0.187 margin actually removes
enough subdivisions to repay the 1.27-times per-box cost.  It should not
invent parent certificates or launch a new heavy verifier load before the
already running forest state is ready.

Meanwhile the largest reusable measured boundary is proved execution, not
another local formula.  The broader main packet remains the fixed-layout
checked-word/overflow-fallback executor, with the v4.17 DAG result available
as a general plan-lowering opportunity.  The immediate gates are:

1. one actual checked word compute node refining the proved 128/192-bit total
   operation contract;
2. a closed repeated microbenchmark with exact fallback and theorem handoff;
3. only on a material win, the complete 875-cell case16594 verdict; and
4. the 4,173-cell independent case10173 family before broader integration.

This keeps the specialized proof effort bounded while preserving both routes
to the orders-of-magnitude objective: fewer subdivisions and cheaper verified
execution.  Full certificate time, reusable preparation, fallback, theorem
handoff, total elapsed cost, and RSS remain the production measures.
