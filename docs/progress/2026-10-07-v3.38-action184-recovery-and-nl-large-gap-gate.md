## Bounded proof work

The angle-polynomial proof remains deliberately bounded.  Candle commit
`d70c17ea` proves the logical fixed-scale representation of the specialized
`4*x0*delta` value, gradient, Hessian, and wrapper without assumptions.  It
does not yet claim analytic containment or authenticated dispatch.  The
remaining acceptable boundary is one reusable containment theorem plus exact
payload dispatch with generic fallback.  Fixture-specific arithmetic lemmas
or a larger family of local specializations are out of scope until the
complete-batch native gate demonstrates a material recurring benefit.

## Next large-gap implementation packet

The next native packet is a direct fixed-scale port of the historical complete
dihedral enclosure structure, not another jet micro-optimization:

1. represent the specialized value, gradient, and symmetric Hessian formulas
   as one native instruction over the same six-coordinate box;
2. keep checked fixed-scale endpoints and explicit outward rounding, and fail
   on any overflow or domain ambiguity;
3. authenticate the exact source subgraph corresponding to outer instructions
   31 through 38 (`pi/2`, `delta_x4`, `4*x0*delta`, square root, inverse,
   multiply, arctangent, add), with generic fallback for every other graph;
4. compare bound width, interval-operation counts, preparation, evaluation,
   peak memory, and acceptance on the 128-box fixture; and
5. only if that is materially better, export the necessary full-batch
   numerical inputs and run the 4,173-box architecture gate before proving a
   new instruction.

If the specialized fixed-scale lane wins, the proof target is one general
instruction soundness theorem and checked source-to-instruction
correspondence.  If it does not, the next controlled discriminator will keep
the specialized algorithm fixed while varying endpoint representation and
rounding.  This keeps the approximately 15-times encoded/native execution gap
separate from the still approximately 24--25-times checked-native/historical-
specialized numerical gap.
