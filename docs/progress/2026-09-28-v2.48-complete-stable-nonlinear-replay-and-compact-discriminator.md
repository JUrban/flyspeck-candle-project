## Compact topology and source-handoff experiment

A general proof-producing compact split-tree checker was completed and tested
on the authentic first 32 action-296 roots. It checks a tree-shaped verdict as
ordinary data and derives each original root theorem through one reusable
soundness theorem. The fixed-algebraic source version validated:

- all 32 verdicts;
- all exact root theorem conclusions against the retained recursive proofs;
- empty hypotheses;
- the unchanged global axiom set; and
- digest `7f5df8d12a23b58b73fee9e254b83753`.

The topology-only benchmark took 37.746 s, compared with 411.606 s for the
older explicit topology, aggregation, and root-handoff chain. This confirms
that the compact representation can eliminate explicit split-proof growth.

That comparison does not describe the already optimized stable production
path, which never constructs the older topology chain. A matched authentic
first-32 run gave the decisive result:

| First-32 stable path | Time |
|---|---:|
| Existing stable extraction and glue | 278.637 s |
| Stable checking plus compact per-root handoff | 391.184 s |

The compact experiment was **1.40x slower overall**. Its 173.953-second
per-root handoff was **2.69x slower** than the existing 64.700-second source
extraction and glue phases. The compact checker is therefore retained as a
sound bounded prototype and architectural discriminator, but it is not being
promoted into the full action-296 path. A future forest-wide theorem might
change that tradeoff; another full per-root compact replay would not currently
be informative.
