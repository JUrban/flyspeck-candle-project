## Correct interpretation of the large gaps

The historical approximately **340x** generic-native/specialized-C++ result
must remain visible as the starting architectural warning, but it is not the
residual optimized-native gap on case10173.  The later held-axis work reduced
that fixture's optimized exact-native residual to about **1.35x**.  Repeating
native scalar or generic-graph tuning there is therefore not the route to
another order of magnitude.

The large actionable residual is now the verified execution boundary and its
generalization to adaptive checking:

- matched case10173 stages remain about 13.91--34.365x slower at the encoded
  computation/handoff boundary than their native controls;
- the older case16594 reflected control was about 98x slower than the matched
  exact native candidate; and
- the later verified x64 source-DAG checker narrowed the complete 875-cell
  absolute time to 6.867778 seconds, but it is still **22.319x** slower than
  the 0.307713-second exact native source-DAG batch.

Thus the orders-of-magnitude objective remains valid, but the next work should
target compact verified execution and complete-search algorithms rather than
describe 340x as an unexplained remaining native arithmetic gap.

## Concrete next performance packets

The two lanes remain independent and bounded.

1. **Finish one historical-instruction proof, not a family of local lemmas.**
   Freeze the accepted sign-directed formula and the three fully authenticated
   permuted source motifs.  Prove one reusable interval/jet instruction theorem
   and integrate it into the universal evaluator contract, with the generic
   source-DAG path as the fallback.  The 6.890x complete-forest win justifies
   this packet; variants do not receive separate proofs without new measured
   evidence.

2. **Measure the historical path at the actual verified-execution boundary.**
   Run the complete streaming forest through the compact checker and charge
   input/preparation, traversal, numerical execution, compact handoff, peak
   RSS, and required repeats separately.  Compare it with native execution on
   the identical visited-node stream.  Acceptance of every surviving terminal
   is mandatory.  This determines whether the current roughly 20x compiled
   execution overhead is amortized or amplified by the lower-work algorithm.

3. **Keep arithmetic work experimental until it passes an exact-output gate.**
   The next arithmetic candidate, if pursued, is a fresh checked narrow-word
   endpoint/wider-product executor with explicit overflow and exact fallback,
   applied under the already selected historical algorithm.  It must preserve
   outward containment and complete acceptance on the 1,344-node forest.  The
   failed inherited block arrangement will not be patched or formalized.  A
   small constant-factor native win alone is insufficient to justify a large
   proof project.

4. **Validate the algorithm on a second expression family.**  A specialized
   primitive advances only if it reduces complete batch/forest time with exact
   arithmetic on another genuine source family.  This protects the universal
   checker from case-specific instruction growth.

5. **Use end-to-end gates.**  The deciding metrics remain complete acceptance,
   visited nodes, preparation amortized over the actual reusable batch,
   recurring computation, theorem/compact-verdict handoff, and memory.  Bound
   tightness is recorded because a faster box evaluator that causes more
   subdivision is not a win.

This plan preserves the small, reusable specialization proof already justified
by a 6.890x search-level result while keeping the broader execution and
algorithm investigation primary.
