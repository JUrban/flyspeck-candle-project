## Why this factorial was run

The fifth-sibling result established two independent gains on the same
whole-box algorithm: avoiding repeated Taylor completion improved exact
recurring time about 1.18 times and pruned 21 parents earlier, while changing
the resulting whole-box algorithm from checked dyadics to padded hardware
intervals improved recurring time about 2.05 times.

The required second-family test uses the distinct case10173 source: 54 outer
instructions and 4,173 genuine certificate boxes.  The generalized evaluator
retains exact source-DAG identities, reconstructed boxes, computed root
enclosures, center plus whole-box value/gradient/symmetric-Hessian state, one
final Taylor completion, and sign-classified interval products.  It crosses:

- generic source composition versus the authenticated historical angle
  operation; and
- checked exact `int128` dyadics versus outward-padded hardware intervals,
  both at scale `2^23`.

This is a source-authenticated development discriminator.  The padded lanes
remain untrusted, and no formal claim uses their output.

Nine warmed, rotation-balanced repetitions gave:

| Whole-box lane | Preparation | Evaluation | Complete batch | Accepted |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Generic, exact | 0.190269 s | 1.099497 s | 1.289765 s | 96/4,173 |
| Generic, padded | 0.188170 s | 0.644822 s | 0.832992 s | 94/4,173 |

| Historical angle, padded | 0.180734 s | 0.446284 s | 0.627018 s | **4,173/4,173** |

At fixed arithmetic, the strong angle operation is **1.557 times** faster in
exact recurring execution and **1.445 times** faster in padded recurring
execution.  More importantly, it changes an unusable generic enclosure into a
complete certificate check.

At fixed algorithm, padded arithmetic is **1.705 times** faster on the generic
lane and **1.582 times** faster on the historical lane.  The two factors are
therefore independent and composable; neither alone explains the desired
orders-of-magnitude improvement.

The generic padded lane loses two of the generic exact lane's 96 negative
decisions.  The historical lanes have identical 4,173/4,173 decisions with no
representation failure.  Relative to historical exact arithmetic, historical
padded bounds are wider on all 4,173 boxes, but the minimum acceptance margin
remains positive: approximately `3.50e-5`, versus `4.90e-5` exact.

The historical result is tighter than generic composition on every box under
both arithmetic representations.  On exact arithmetic, its upper-bound
improvement ranges from approximately `0.000594` to `0.676809`, with a mean
of approximately `0.04475`.  This is a mathematical-enclosure result, not an
interpreter artifact.

## Generality and the mature direct-plan control

This result passes the second-family gate for the reusable historical
dihedral primitive: the same canonical numerical operation used through
authenticated permutations in the fifth-sibling forest now gives a material
exact-arithmetic batch win and complete coverage on case10173.

It does **not** establish the dense whole-box interpreter as the final
production architecture.  The mature source-authenticated case10173 direct
plan previously measured 0.110757 seconds exact and 0.068003 seconds padded
for recurring evaluation.  The new historical whole-box lanes are therefore
about **6.375 times** and **6.563 times** slower recurrently.  With each
run's preparation charged, the gaps are smaller but still 1.708 and 1.465
times.

That difference is not a contradiction.  The direct plan validates the
54-instruction skeleton once and lowers constants, six coordinate roots,
the historical angle, scaling, and final assembly to compact typed data.  The
general whole-box lane still interprets dense jets for the surrounding source
operations.  The correct destination is consequently:

1. a general whole-box/analytic invariant and Taylor theorem;
2. a checked source-DAG-to-compact-plan compiler with exact role and source
   identities;
3. a small reusable vocabulary of strong proved operations, including the
   historical dihedral primitive where its exact motif is authenticated;
4. one computed batch verdict with intermediate intervals remaining data; and
5. exact fail-closed fallback for unsupported source, domain, certificate, or
   word-range cases.

The dense source evaluator remains a valuable semantic reference and fallback,
not the throughput target.

## Proof and larger-gap decision

The bounded historical proof packet remains justified and does not expand
into per-expression calculus.  Its next step is still the general
square-root/Hessian interval-containment lift, followed by one reusable
historical-instruction theorem and integration into the universal evaluator
contract.  The already authenticated source motifs and permutations remain
the only dispatch authority.

Once that theorem is closed, the next decisive performance measurement is at
the actual verified-execution boundary: run the compact historical path over
the complete fifth-sibling forest and compare it with the exact native run on
the identical visited-node stream.  Report authenticated plan preparation,
numerical traversal, compact theorem handoff, total elapsed time, peak memory,
and terminal acceptance separately.  This targets the current roughly
one-order-of-magnitude compiled/encoded residual; another native case10173
scalar or graph rewrite would duplicate already closed factorials.

In parallel, the compact-plan format should carry static slot identities,
support metadata, and primitive roles as checked data.  A representative
case16594 prefix can then determine whether executing that plan in one
`Kernel.compute` batch materially improves on the already verified source-DAG
checker before a broad plan-compiler soundness proof is attempted.

The original roughly 15-times Candle/native and 340-times initial
generic-native/specialized-C++ figures remain separate historical diagnostics.
This result assigns real portions of the numerical side but does not claim
either gap closed.
