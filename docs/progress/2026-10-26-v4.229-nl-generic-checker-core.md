## Result

1. a prepared analytic program;
2. the dimension;
3. the declared cell count;
4. the per-cell certificate count; and
5. the packed certificate bytes.

6 and 31.  The header and complete traversal use declared counts rather than

prepared program rather than `bounded_case16594_prepared_program`.

checks both natural-number counts are below 2^32 before converting them to
word64 for exact comparison.  It rejects a mismatched header, malformed cell,
failed numerical evaluation, nonnegative bound, trailing input, or incomplete
traversal.
