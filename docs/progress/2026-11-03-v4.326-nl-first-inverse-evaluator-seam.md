## Bounded failed route

A deliberately bounded probe tried to close the complete genuine first
inverse state by one monolithic `EVAL_TAC`.  The fixture used the production
decimal radius encoding, the captured reciprocal, and the zero-gradient,
zero-Hessian predecessor state.  Parsing and every fixture definition passed,
but the closed pure evaluator produced no theorem after **325.74 s**.  The HOL
worker was CPU-bound and memory-stable at roughly **1.3 GB**.  The run was
stopped manually; its timeout was not raised and the unfinished probe was not
committed.

This does not indicate a representation mismatch or a failed numerical
operation: the exact derivative boundary is green.  It shows that replaying
the whole state operation by raw evaluator reduction is already the wrong
proof-construction granularity.  The next work must instantiate the proved
unary/inverse composition theorems with bounded component results, especially
the second derivative, zero-list propagation, and recompletion.  The complete
167-entry native exact replay remains prohibited by LR-196.
