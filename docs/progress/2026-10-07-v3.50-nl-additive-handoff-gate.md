changes arithmetic/rounding only. A long-double development build will compare

unrounded-quotient lower bound. The latter cannot support a proof or release

the complete-batch and multi-certificate gates. Cold preparation remains in
the total throughout; reusable preparation is amortized only when actually
shared.
