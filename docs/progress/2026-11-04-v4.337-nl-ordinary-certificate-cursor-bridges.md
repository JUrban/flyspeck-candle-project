## The arctangent representation seam

The typed certificate stores lower/upper reciprocal choices plus center and
box derivatives.  The production evaluator instead consumes eight exact
interval records: center and box inputs, center result, two squares, two
derivatives, and the box-derivative square.  Equality of certificate
representations would therefore have been false.

The new theorem uses the existing typed arctangent execution trace to name
the derived intervals and proves that an authenticated eight-record packet
implements the same semantic operation.  It preserves the production checks
and does not whitelist packet aliases or bypass the certificate arithmetic.
