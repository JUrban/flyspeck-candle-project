## Universal analytic bridge is proved

Candle commit `30628fe` proves the end-to-end analytic contract for every
dimension-valid expression in the supported reflected polynomial language.
This removes the need for a separately developed calculus proof for each new
polynomial.

The contract deliberately uses two computed jets:

1. the midpoint jet supplies the source expression's value and gradient at
   the box center; and
2. the full-box jet encloses every Hessian component throughout the box.

The proof connects the source reifier, normalized shared-jet evaluator,
interval containment results, and Flyspeck's multivariate Taylor theorem. The
principal theorem is `candle_q_dim_poly_jet_whole_box_upper_sound`:

```text
candle_poly_valid_dim (dimindex (:N)) e /\
LENGTH boxes = dimindex (:N) /\
candle_q_box_valid_list boxes
==>
!p. p IN interval [lower boxes, upper boxes]
    ==> candle_poly_denote_dim e p
        <= candle_q_real (candle_q_dim_poly_jet_whole_box_upper e boxes)
```

Its supporting results prove gradient and Hessian list correspondence,
componentwise containment, both Taylor sums, the full `m_taylor_error`
contract, and reusable midpoint/full-box normalized-jet lemmas. The final
theorem has no assumptions beyond the displayed source validity, dimension,
and box-validity contract.
