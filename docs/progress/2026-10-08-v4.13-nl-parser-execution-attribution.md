## Matched parser versus complete-checker experiment

The complete specialized checker includes input reading, parsing, conversion
of signed fixed-scale numbers, construction of 875 boxes and 27,125 typed
certificate records, checked numerical execution, and the final verdict.
The existing verified streaming parser exercises the same packed header and
cell parser but discards each successfully parsed cell instead of evaluating
the numerical program.

A new pinned harness ran those two verified executables for five warmed,
rotation-balanced repetitions on the exact accepted 2,705,516-byte payload:

The parser is **2.965%** of complete wall time; the complete checker is
33.729 times slower.  Subtracting the two means leaves 10.146 seconds, or
97.035%, beyond the parser/conversion boundary.

That subtraction is an attribution bound, not a strict additive profile: the
complete checker interleaves parsing and evaluation, and different live data
can change allocation and collection behavior.  It is nevertheless decisive
for choosing work.  Even eliminating all observed input parsing and number
conversion cannot materially close the current gap.

The first version of the harness preserved all ten successful numerical runs
but failed while summarizing them because `system` was used as an `awk` array
name.  That directory remains preserved.  The corrected v2 changed only the
summary accumulator name and produced the result above in a fresh directory.

## Consequence for the two performance gaps

Against the matched 0.422735-second exact-native numerical lane, the newly
measured complete checker is 24.734 times slower.  Removing the entire
standalone parser mean still leaves a 24.001-times numerical/execution ratio.
This confirms that the current approximately 24-times boundary is primarily
inside checked interval/Taylor execution and its generated representation,
not the external packed format.

The historical approximately **340-times** generic fixed-scale native versus
specialized-C++ observation remains a separate algorithmic warning.  It is
not replaced by the approximately 24-times current result and the two ratios
must not be multiplied as though they were one matched factorial.

The already completed same-box 2-by-2 native factorial further separates the
current axes:

- authenticated derivative/Taylor lowering contributes about 2.054 times at
  fixed exact arithmetic;
- exact arithmetic versus the padded-hardware discriminator contributes about
  1.803 times at the fixed authenticated graph; and
- changing both contributes about 3.702 times.

Thus neither generic derivative dispatch nor scalar representation alone
explains the old architectural gap.  A larger numerical algorithm change is
still required if the project is to obtain orders-of-magnitude improvement.

## Bounded specialization proof decision

The six current delta roles retain a strong measured case: they transferred
about 91% of their native recurring factor and reduced the complete verified
batch from 19.05 to about 10.23 seconds in the earlier uncontended repeats.
Their source programs already have exact integer-semantic authentication, and
the direct checked evaluators are total and return well-formed Taylor states
on success.

The remaining release theorem is not an isolated tiny role lemma.  It depends
on the shared checker-wide interval multiplication, wide-arithmetic, Taylor,
and real-analytic containment obligations.  The bounded decision is therefore
to retain and finish the two reusable role-family refinements through that
common soundness layer, without adding inverse-root, whole-chain, or
expression-specific proof packets.

## Next larger-gap experiment

The next native experiment will change one substantial axis at a time on the
genuine boxes:

1. keep exact dyadic arithmetic, the reconstructed Taylor boxes, directed
   rounding, final Taylor contract, and certificate acceptance fixed;
2. replace one complete dominant dihedral enclosure block with a faithful
   specialized numerical data flow rather than another formula alias;
3. preserve the intermediate Taylor tightening whose removal caused the
   earlier whole-chain candidate to accept only 408/875 cells;
4. only after the exact algorithm accepts the complete batch, cross that same
   algorithm with the padded-hardware discriminator to isolate arithmetic and
   rounding from work per box; and
5. record preparation, recurring batch time, memory, upper-bound deltas,
   acceptance, and subdivision consequences, then repeat on an independent
   expression family before starting a production proof.

If the exact specialized block does not deliver a large recurring factor, the
formal lane should prioritize a lower-overhead fixed-layout word executor.
The parser result rules out external input conversion as that executor's main
target.
