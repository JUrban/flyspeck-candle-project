## Final-aggregation diagnosis

Three progressively smaller probes distinguish representation growth from
numerical enclosure quality.

1. The existing normalized split prover reaches the complete center and box
   jets, then exits during the final Taylor aggregation.  Thus the earlier
   failure is not specific to the unnormalized checker.
2. Computing the final components separately succeeds through domain guards,
   radii, center value, center gradient, box Hessian, and the gradient upper.
   The monolithic weighted-Hessian aggregation is the first failing call.
3. Computing each of the six Hessian row contributions succeeds.  Exact
   normalized accumulation succeeds for rows 0, 1, and 2, then the process
   exits while adding row 3.  No individual row is pathological; accumulated
   exact rational representation growth is the immediate execution failure.

Outward-rounding each proved nonnegative row contribution to the checker's
existing fixed denominator makes all six row sums and the final verdict
complete.  It returns, exactly:
