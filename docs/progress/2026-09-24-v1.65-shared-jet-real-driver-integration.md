## Why 43 programs appeared, and why they are not needed

The 43-program genuine-leaf experiment was a deliberately older comparison
boundary: one source program, six separately expanded first derivatives, and
36 separately expanded second derivatives. It tested the explicit-derivative
checker and exposed its preparation/term-size failure. It was never a
requirement of the shared-jet design.

The same captured Flyspeck formula and the exact boxes used by its real
certificate now pass through the shared-jet route. The source is reified and
compiled once to one **87-instruction** program. For every box, that program
computes the center value/gradient and the whole-box value/gradient/Hessian
enclosures. The language-wide
`candle_q_dim_poly_jet_whole_box_accept_sound` theorem turns acceptance into
the original strict real inequality. No expanded derivative program and no
expression-specific calculus proof is used.

The missing connection was therefore not differentiation. The standalone
shared-jet batch already proved source inequalities over encoded exact boxes;
the real verifier supplied live `Arith_float` endpoint syntax and required an
`m_cell_list_pass` theorem. The new adapter now:

1. obtains each leaf's box from its authenticated live domain theorem;
2. proves the live endpoints equal their exact rational normal forms;
3. checks the box with the one prepared shared-jet source program;
4. transports the accepted source theorem back to the live endpoint syntax;
5. returns the verifier's existing `m_cell_list_pass` result; and
6. leaves the existing certificate split/glue and final normalization logic
   in control of the rest of the proof.

This result definitively defers smart symbolic differentiation for the
supported polynomial family. It should be reconsidered only if a real source
construct cannot be covered by the shared-jet language or its general
soundness theorem.

## Performance interpretation and next boundary

This milestone settles the architectural comparison but is not yet a runtime
win. The ordinary real-driver baseline completes this small inequality in
about 34 seconds from its richer checkpoint. The integrated reflected replay
takes about 65.6 seconds for leaves plus glues and another 4.6 seconds for
final assembly from its prepared support state. Earlier leaf-only shared-jet
measurement took 9.531 seconds for all 16 boxes, including 4.725 seconds in
the general soundness handoff. The newly visible excess is therefore in live
endpoint/component transport and verifier theorem reconstruction, not in
shared-jet arithmetic or derivative generation.

The next bounded work is to profile and reduce that theorem-transport cost,
preserving the same universal source theorem and original certificate driver.
After that, a small batch of different genuine inequalities should charge
support and expression preparation once and compare recurring certificate
throughput. Source-language coverage should expand only in response to the
first real unsupported formula.
