Each positive failure touched exactly the configured heap size and emitted no
checker output.  The wrong-source mode parses the same certificate, packed
cells, topology tokens and jobs, then fails before numerical traversal.  Its
small successful result isolates the large allocation to the complete checker
path rather than the external parsers.

No 120--150 GiB retry is admitted.  Translation success and the earlier
10.82-second native checker result do not establish a usable CakeML execution
route.
