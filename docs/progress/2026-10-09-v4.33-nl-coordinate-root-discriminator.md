historical-dihedral soundness proof.  The approximately 15-times
Candle/native observation concerns encoded execution, representation, and
theorem handoff.  The approximately 340-times generic-native/specialized-C++

numerical algorithms, arithmetic, enclosure quality, and adaptive search.
It is not the present residual on every workload: later case10173 native work
already reduced that particular native comparison to a small factor.

independent questions:

- with the historical algorithm and its 1,344-node path fixed, changing exact

- the exact historical enclosure can be locally slower per fixed box, but it

  improves the end-to-end forest by 6.67 times; and
- the padded generic online search visits 9,996 nodes and leaves two terminal
  boxes unproved, whereas padded historical arithmetic still closes all
  1,344 visited nodes.

Arithmetic time, enclosure strength, and complete certificate acceptance
therefore cannot be collapsed into a single microbenchmark ratio.

## Decision and larger-gap plan

The coordinate-root candidate is retained as an exact native diagnostic but
does not earn a new proof packet.  It gives machinery and a regression oracle
for later fixed-layout work, but a 3--5% complete-run gain cannot justify
diverting the current analytic proof lane.

The already justified historical-dihedral proof remains bounded to one
general reusable instruction theorem.  Its native benefit is enclosure-driven
and survives complete online traversal, so closing that theorem and measuring
the exact instruction through `Kernel.compute` is still the next verified
execution boundary.

The next broader native candidate must be coarser than dispatch cleanup or a
single sparse root.  It should hold checked exact arithmetic, outward rounds,
the final Taylor acceptance contract, and the genuine boxes fixed while
replacing a reusable complete centered-jet or whole-leaf numerical block.
Selection will use the new profile: multiplication plus nonhistorical
square-root work is about 36.9% of outer time, while the historical primitive
itself is about 43.8%.  The candidate must preserve complete certificate
acceptance and exact or contained bounds on the fixed nodes, then win in a
root-started online run.  A second genuine expression family remains required
before general proof expansion.

This preserves the orders-of-magnitude objective without treating the old
340-times starting ratio as an unmeasured current residual or accumulating
small case-specific proof shims.
