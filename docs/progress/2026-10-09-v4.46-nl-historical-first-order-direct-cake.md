## Matched historical operation

The new word-bounded checker recomputes the historical center operation from:

- six scale-2^40 center coordinates;
- checked square-root certificates for `delta` and `4*x0`;
- five checked reciprocal certificates;
- the historical block-rounded `U126` and `U135` construction;
- `delta_x4`, `b`, `c`, the fixed-point arctangent, the final value, and all
  six first derivatives.

Untrusted preparation supplies roots, reciprocals, and expected outputs, but
the checker authenticates the numerical certificates before using them and
recomputes the value and gradient.  The authenticated input identities are:

- historical kernel trace: `a4591c1d5e93fc9fa3cfae61585d42b32f181afb69955d7dc85cebac45d55d3a`;
- reciprocal trace: `8983a97132fc395213c6bd0ff1eb24bbe7390480ddd04a3500ce6791cd095b92`;
- packed 4,173-cell payload: `669762dc3cbf13d097b14b02215dcdcea24a9ebb809c343505ede9f88b7f60be`.

The complete payload is 1,502,292 bytes.  A one-cell payload was retained only
for the edit/smoke-test loop.
