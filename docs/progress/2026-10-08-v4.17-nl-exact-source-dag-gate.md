## Held-axis question

The v4.16 leaf cache established that exact immutable leaves are worth
sharing, but it did not test repeated derivative/Taylor subgraphs.  A static
census of the genuine case16594 program found:

- 167 outer expression occurrences but only 76 exact structural identities;
- 91 duplicate occurrences, including four copies of one 21-node subtree;
- 27 occurrences of `pi/2` and substantial repeated nontrivial
  multiplication, addition, inverse, square-root, and arctangent subgraphs;
  and
- much less exact reuse in the independent case10173 plan: 54 occurrences,
  50 identities, and only four duplicate leaves.

The bounded candidate hash-conses only the exact authenticated outer source
structure.  It deliberately does **not** identify arbitrary algebraically
equal polynomials, because a different source evaluation order can change an
outward-rounded enclosure.  Source-authenticated specialized delta leaves
are keyed by logical role and coordinate.  The cache is fresh for every box.

Repeated square roots have a stronger runtime guard: both the original
rational certificate endpoints and the converted fixed endpoints must equal
the first occurrence.  A changed certificate fails closed instead of
authorizing an alias.  Collapsed, fused, traced, profiling, and external
certificate-capture modes are excluded from this first prototype so that the
comparison changes one axis only.

Nine warmed, rotation-balanced repetitions used all 875 genuine case16594
boxes.  The held pair uses the same mixed 128/192-bit exact dyadic arithmetic,
directed rounding, fixed scalar kernels, source-authenticated delta roles,
Taylor-box reconstruction, and 167-instruction graph.

| Lane | Preparation | Evaluation | Complete batch | Accepted |

| Exact source DAG | 0.064553 s | **0.243160 s** | **0.307713 s** | 875/875 |

The exact DAG gives a **1.5889-times recurring** and **1.4688-times fully
charged batch** speedup.  Its 79,625 hits and 66,500 misses are exactly 91 and
76 per box, matching the independent source census.  Relative to the held
baseline it reduces:

- Taylor completions from 146,125 to 66,500;
- interval products from 7,606,375 to 4,900,000;
- square-root steps from 8,750 to 6,125;
- inverse steps from 12,250 to 5,250; and

Every final bound record is byte-identical between the held pair.  Thus the
candidate removes repeated work without spending enclosure margin or changing

## Interpretation of the two larger gaps

This is a material reusable layer, but it is not the orders-of-magnitude
answer by itself.

1. The current matched complete verified/native case16594 boundary was about
   **24 times** before this native-only candidate.  A corresponding proved
   fixed-layout DAG instruction does not yet exist, so that ratio must not be
   silently recomputed from unmatched lanes.
2. The historical roughly **340-times** generic fixed-scale native versus
   specialized-C++ comparison remains evidence about algorithm and arithmetic
   architecture at the original case10173 starting point.  It is not a
   current matched residual for case16594; later case10173 work reduced its
   optimized native residual substantially.

The new result shows that repeated generic derivative/Taylor work is a real
cost on a reuse-rich expression.  The independent case10173 census also
predicts that exact DAG reuse alone will do almost nothing there.  Therefore
the optimization belongs in authenticated plan lowering, but cannot replace
specialized numerical algorithms or cheaper proved execution.

## Bounded proof decision and next factorial

No expression-specific DAG proof packet begins from this native result.  The
durable proof shape, if the multifamily gate warrants it, is one general
source-plan theorem: evaluating a hash-consed immutable node once and reusing
its result refines evaluation of the authenticated tree.  Plans with no reuse
should be statically lowered without a dynamic cache.

The existing six delta-role refinements continue through their already
bounded common soundness layer because they produced the larger verified
gain.  The three direct dihedral-identity proof packet remains stopped at its
approximately six-percent native result.

The next native experiment keeps the broader investigation explicit with a
two-axis gate:

1. retain the exact DAG as the control graph and share the common delta value,
   gradient, Hessian, reconstructed box-gradient data, and related
   tetrahedron inputs across the three coordinate-permuted angle blocks;
2. first hold mixed exact arithmetic and every rounding boundary fixed, and
   require byte-identical exact bounds and 875/875 acceptance;
3. only if that shared block materially reduces the complete exact batch,
   cross both DAG lanes with the existing padded-hardware arithmetic
   discriminator;
4. report preparation, recurring time, fully charged batch time, memory,
   operation counts, bound deltas, and acceptance for all four lanes; and
5. require an independent genuine family or another reuse-rich certificate
   before expanding a general source-to-shared-plan proof.

This factorial separates less generic derivative/Taylor work from arithmetic
representation and rounding.  If the exact shared block saves only another
small percentage, the algorithmic lane moves to a faithful specialized
historical dihedral/tetrahedron enclosure rather than accumulating local
aliases.  In parallel, the verified-execution lane remains the fixed-layout
checked-word/overflow-fallback project.  Complete certificate time and compact
theorem handoff remain the eventual decision measures.
