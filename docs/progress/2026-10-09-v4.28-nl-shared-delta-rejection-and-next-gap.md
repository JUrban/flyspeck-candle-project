## Exact held-axis experiment

The previous rounding factorial showed that removing all directed quotient
rounding from the hardware historical path gave only a 1.210-times recurring
and 1.182-times preparation-inclusive improvement.  The next cheap experiment
therefore held checked exact arithmetic and outward rounding fixed while
changing derivative data flow.

The case16594 source contains three authenticated permuted delta-dihedral
motifs.  The candidate computes the shared tetrahedrally symmetric delta
center/box jet once, transports it through the already authenticated
self-inverse permutations, and leaves each motif's coordinate-specific
`delta_x4`, triangle-`U`, roots, inverses, arctangent, Hessian construction,
and Taylor completion unchanged.

Nine warmed, rotation-balanced repetitions on the exact same 1,344 genuine
historical-forest nodes gave:

| Checked-exact historical lane | Pruning acceptances | Preparation | Evaluation | Complete batch |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Independent delta jets | 682 | 0.069805 s | 0.498118 s | 0.567923 s |
| Shared delta jet | 682 | 0.069852 s | 0.482700 s | 0.552551 s |

The gain is only **1.032 times** in recurring evaluation and **1.028 times**
for the complete batch.  Peak RSS remains approximately 15.9 MiB.

The candidate removes real work:

| Lane | Floor quotients | Ceiling quotients | Interval products | Endpoint products |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Independent | 5,977,937 | 5,977,937 | 7,722,701 | 15,446,302 |
| Shared | 5,516,777 | 5,516,777 | 7,261,541 | 14,541,982 |

That is 461,160 fewer calls in each directed-quotient class and 461,160 fewer
interval products, but it does not materially change elapsed time.  All 682
negative pruning decisions remain negative.  Of 1,344 result bounds, 1,342
are byte-identical; the other two are already-positive subdivision nodes and
widen outward by at most `4.3201e-10`.  Complete forest acceptance is
unchanged.

## Decision

The shared-delta candidate is **measured and rejected as a proof target**.
It remains committed as a reproducible diagnostic, but the general
historical-dihedral theorem will not be enlarged to prove a batched shared
variant for a 2.8% complete-batch gain.

Together, the two held-axis results now rule out two tempting local
explanations:

- eliminating outward quotient padding buys only about 18% of complete-batch
  time on the hardware path; and
- eliminating repeated delta jets buys only about 3% on the checked-exact
  path.

The large remaining opportunity lies in the coordinate-specific historical
work, sparse/symmetric derivative representation, complete search algorithm,
and verified execution boundary—not in another delta or rounding micro-shim.

## Concrete larger-gap plan

The approximate historical gaps remain explicit: roughly 15 times for the
earlier Candle/native execution boundary and roughly 340 times for the
original generic fixed-scale/specialized-C++ comparison.  They describe
different starting problems and are not current universal residuals.  The
current verified source-DAG/native boundary on case16594 is approximately 22
times, while native specialization has already reduced the original
case10173 numerical gap substantially.

The next work is ordered as follows:

1. Add one low-overhead stage attribution to the exact historical instruction
   across the same 1,344-node batch: `delta_x4`, the two triangle-`U` jets,
   root/inverse work, gradient assembly, Hessian assembly, and arctangent.
   The just-rejected delta stage is no longer a candidate.
2. Compare those counts and times with the pinned specialized C++ stage trace.
   This chooses between a compact sparse/symmetric jet, reusable triangle-`U`
   primitives, and one authenticated three-angle block.  It is not another
   formula-only substitution.
3. Require a material complete-forest improvement, unchanged acceptance, and
   a second genuine family before adding another analytic instruction theorem.
4. In parallel, finish the already selected single historical-dihedral
   analytic invariant and run it through the verified streaming forest.  That
   measurement separates numerical work from cval dispatch, allocation/GC,
   checked-word representation, and theorem handoff at the current ~22-times
   boundary.
5. If the verified instruction remains far behind its exact native analogue,
   prioritize a compact batched checked-word executor with explicit overflow
   data and exact fallback rather than further source-expression
   micro-specializations.

Complete-certificate or complete representative-forest cost remains the gate.
Reusable build/translation/proof preparation is reported separately and
amortized; evaluator-only wins do not decide the architecture.
