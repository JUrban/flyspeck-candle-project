## Assessment of the 2026-09-25 performance research note

The note is useful and consistent with the measured evidence, with the
important distinction it makes between source observations and runtime
causes.

Already covered or substantially narrowed:

- The shared-jet architecture already avoids separately expanded derivative
  programs.
- One-verdict internal batching substantially amortizes repeated evaluator
  setup and theorem-boundary costs.
- The fixed-scale experiment directly tests the rational normalization/GCD
  concern.  On 64 genuine boxes it took **13.101306 s**, versus
  **105.142893 s** for the rational path (**8.03x**), with **130/130**
  structurally equal outputs.
- Source preparation (**4.535997 s**) and box setup (**15.673204 s**) are
  visible reusable costs and are not charged per box in the intended batch
  architecture.
- Earlier ordinary-versus-reflected, Hessian-only, equal-denominator, and
  reused-point-plan discriminators already rule out several overly broad
  explanations.

Still relevant after the general soundness proof is integrated:

- constant/zero/sparse Taylor completion may perform avoidable dense work;
- full-jet operations whose callers retain only the Hessian may compute and
  allocate unused values and gradients;
- GCD-fuel construction remains relevant to any rational fallback path;
- untrusted point-hint preparation should not manufacture arithmetic proofs
  that the trusted checker subsequently discards; and
- source authentication, reusable expression preparation, and changing box
  certificates should remain cleanly separated.

The most discriminating next experiment is a warm batch of varied genuine
boxes with preparation charged once, after the complete proof handoff works.
It should time and, where cheap, count:

1. fixed evaluator setup and input/output conversion;
2. the numerical fixed-scale computation;
3. compact verdict conversion and final theorem handoff;
4. constant/zero and Hessian-only work against the unchanged full result; and
5. accepted boxes, subdivisions, integer sizes, and memory.

The current 8.03x result makes rational normalization the primary measured
explanation.  Sparse completion and compact results warrant targeted tests,
but not an architectural rewrite before the complete genuine-leaf theorem is
available.
