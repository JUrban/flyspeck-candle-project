The first authenticated action-296 formula was reconstructed from the frozen
Flyspeck closure and normalized without attempting its 1,061-leaf certificate.
The current polynomial source reifier rejects nine maximal subterms:

- six coordinate square roots, `sqrt (x$1)` through `sqrt (x$6)`;
- the constant `pi`;
- `inv (&2)`; and
- one `atn` application whose argument contains further square-root and
  reciprocal structure.

The stable result is:

```text

unsupported_terms=9
unsupported_heads=atn,pi,real_inv,sqrt
function_md5=14a01d94a2d263608ca2bb5ce211078d
```

`inv (&2)` can be constant-folded, but general reciprocal support is still
required inside the arctangent argument. The actual analytic language boundary
is therefore `sqrt` + `inv` + `atn` + an interval enclosure for `pi`, not a
special-purpose `dih` constructor and not another symbolic-differentiation
layer.

Flyspeck already proves the general analytic composition theorems
`m_taylor_sqrt_compose`, `m_taylor_inv_compose`, and
`m_taylor_atn_compose`. The selected direction is to reuse those
language-wide facts while adding reflected numeric constructors and their
generic soundness connections. New expressions should instantiate a supported
language theorem; they should not require expression-specific calculus proofs.
