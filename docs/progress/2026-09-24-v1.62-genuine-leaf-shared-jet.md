## Why the first reflected comparison had 43 programs

The initial genuine-leaf comparison deliberately targeted the older
`candle_cv_q_dim_whole_box_check` interface.  That interface accepts one
program for the function, six independently differentiated first-derivative
programs, and a full 6 by 6 matrix of independently differentiated
second-derivative programs:

```text
1 + 6 + 36 = 43 programs
```

This was a comparison adapter, not a mathematical requirement and not the
intended production architecture.  It tested whether exact symbolic
differentiation followed by the existing reflected arithmetic boundary could
reproduce a real leaf.  On this formula it exposed the wrong scaling boundary:
the expansion and encoding phase took about **23 minutes 56 seconds**, produced
a **10,450,794-byte** printed computation term, and the runtime exited as the
monolithic `Kernel.compute` call began.

were then sent through the existing shared-jet architecture.  The source was

1. the center environment;

3. one value/gradient/Hessian interval jet over the whole box;
4. the Taylor upper bound;
5. box validity; and

`candle_cv_q_dim_poly_jet_whole_box_check_correct`, and the language-wide

real bound.  Rewriting by the source-reification theorem yielded an
assumption-free theorem over the original captured formula.  No per-expression
calculus proof and no separately expanded derivative program was used.

## Decision and next measurement

For polynomial expressions already covered by the source reifier and universal
jet soundness theorem, the shared-jet evaluator is now the primary reflected
NL candidate.  Smart symbolic differentiation is deferred unless a later
unsupported expression family supplies evidence that it is genuinely needed.

session: reuse a compiled expression across several real boxes, measure source
preparation once, and separate center-jet, box-jet, Taylor handoff, and theorem
assembly costs.  In parallel, the protected cumulative direct run continues
