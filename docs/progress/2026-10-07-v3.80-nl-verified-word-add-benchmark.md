## Proved and executed boundary

The first bounded-word packet now covers a complete executable chain:

1. a Word64-only carry primitive is proved equal to HOL4's generic carry;
2. checked 128- and 192-bit limb additions are proved equal to their exact
   option-valued natural-number contracts;
3. a recursive checked-add workload is proved equal to the corresponding
   exact recursive contract, including early overflow;
4. CakeML's verified translator accepts that implementation without
   `w2n`/`n2w` in the recurring carry path; and
5. CakeML's verified x64 compiler produces closed add and control programs.

Each executable performs 10,000,000 iterations.  The add program then checks
for exactly `SOME (Bounded128 0w 10000000w)`; the control checks its exact zero
result.  A mismatch raises an uncaught exception.  This prevents an unused
result or accidentally retyped benchmark from supplying a false timing.

stack:

| Recursion/constructor control | **0.208571 s** | 0.127143 s | 0.075714 s | 132,608 KiB | 7/7 |

The incremental recurring cost is about **0.144286 seconds for 10,000,000
checked 128-bit additions**, or **14.43 ns per checked addition**.  Each
addition executes two Word64 carry stages plus the option/overflow path.  The
whole add lane, including program startup and the result check, costs about
35.29 ns per iteration.

This is a positive continuation gate.  Verified word execution is cheap
enough that the next arithmetic packet is justified.  It is not yet a
`Kernel.compute` or nonlinear-checker speedup: the benchmark is a separately
verified compiled CakeML program, and it does not produce a Candle theorem or
exercise the current `cv`/`ce` executor.

## Preparation and amortization

- logical theory plus base translations: 88 seconds;

  seconds;

- native linking: below one second.

Thus a coherent cold preparation is roughly **5.8 minutes**, with the largest
observed proof process at about 4.1 GiB RSS.  Preparation is reusable; the 14
accepted measured executions together took under five seconds.  These
component timings are not presented as a single clean end-to-end cold run,
because one quotation-library failure occurred during development and is not
charged to the final program.

## What remains of the two performance problems

The historical numbers must remain separate, but later experiments have
already refined their meaning:

- On case10173, controlled source-derived specialization reduced the original
  approximately 340-times generic-native/specialized-C++ starting gap to about
  **1.35 times** for optimized exact native execution.  Repeating that native
  factorial would not be informative.
- On the independent case16594 family, the completed two-axis factorial holds
  arithmetic or graph work fixed.  Safe derivative/Taylor lowering provides
  only **1.045 times**, while exact mixed 128/192 arithmetic versus padded
  hardware provides about **3.22 times**.  The dominant current boundary is
  roughly **98 times** between the 225.8-second compact reflected batch and
  the 2.31-second matched exact native candidate.

The present benchmark attacks that proof-producing execution boundary.  It
does not claim that the historical algorithmic opportunity disappeared: a
new expression family may still require a specialized analytic instruction,
and case16594's enclosure-sensitive 85-step blocks remain unresolved.  It
does show that another same-fixture comparator, check-removal, or generic
graph microvariant is lower value than carrying the winning word substrate
into the real operation mix.

## Concrete larger-gap plan

The next bounded packet is wide multiply plus directed shift/narrowing, not a
large checker rewrite.  It will preserve explicit overflow and the exact
fallback, obtain verified translations, and execute a closed trace whose
operation proportions come from the real case16594 ledger.  This holds the
mathematical graph and rounding fixed while testing the remaining arithmetic
representation.  The trace must include successful operations, narrowing,
and adversarial overflow/fallback cases.

Only a positive mixed-operation result advances the executor packet:

1. add bounded 128/192 values and the minimal operations to an experimental
   `compute_exec` path;
2. prove a general decode/refinement theorem back to the existing natural
   semantics, with conversion once at input and compact theorem handoff once
   at output;
3. measure conversion, recurring execution, fallback, handoff, total elapsed
   time, and memory independently; and
4. run all 875 case16594 cells with unchanged acceptance and exact outputs.

The first complete integration gate is at least an order-of-magnitude
improvement over the 225.8-second reflected batch, followed by confirmation
on all 4,173 optimized case10173 cells.  The longer-term target is a low
single-digit factor from matched exact native execution.  Failure of the
mixed-operation benchmark or experimental executor gate stops the datatype
extension before signed intervals and full certificate logic are added.

The next genuinely different algorithm experiment, when capacity permits, is
a source-authenticated specialized instruction for one case16594 85-step
inverse/square-root/arctangent block.  It must keep mixed exact arithmetic and
rounding fixed, improve enclosure work rather than merely delay
normalization, and pass all 875 cells before proof work.  This keeps the
broader architecture investigation active without repeating the completed
case10173 and case16594 factorials.
