## Checked exact-rational normalization

Candle commit `3a22440` adds a bounded exact-rational normalizer and its
correctness development. A 128-step Euclidean computation proposes a common
divisor. The result is used only after the checker has verified all exact
multiplication identities and a nonzero denominator quotient. If the bounded
search is exhausted or any check fails, the function returns a sign-normalized
but unreduced value.

The new ordinary theorems include:

- `candle_q_real_normalize`;
- `candle_q_real_add_normalized`; and
- `candle_q_real_mul_normalized`.

The reflected side proves the bounded GCD computation, checked division,
normalization, normalized addition and multiplication, and interval
normalization correct with respect to their ordinary counterparts. Thus
normalization is not an external arithmetic oracle and cannot change a
represented rational value.

## Six-coordinate scaling batch

Candle commit `648d913` exercises all six coordinates with cross derivatives
and reuses each prepared expression across several boxes. The largest family
contains 92 monomials of degree two through four. Its reflected program has
725 instructions and encodes to 21,912 term characters. The preceding family
contains 47 monomials, 347 instructions, and 10,611 encoded characters.

The 47- and 92-monomial programs are split into independently computed chunks
of at most 128 instructions. This avoids a large expression spine; exact
normalization keeps arithmetic values small between chunks. For each family,
the same prepared expression is checked consecutively on a narrow binary box,
an asymmetric prime-denominator box, and a wide binary box.

| Family | Monomials | Instructions | Box | Recurring check | Final upper rational bits (numerator / denominator) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Mixed | 47 | 347 | narrow binary | 1.666 s | 23 / 15 |
| Mixed | 47 | 347 | asymmetric primes | 1.688 s | 98 / 89 |
| Mixed | 47 | 347 | wide binary | 1.715 s | 9 / 1 |
| Dense | 92 | 725 | narrow binary | 3.836 s | 26 / 16 |
| Dense | 92 | 725 | asymmetric primes | 3.620 s | 144 / 133 |
| Dense | 92 | 725 | wide binary | 3.550 s | 14 / 4 |

All six large cases returned acceptance and an assumption-free
`Kernel.compute` equality. Smaller prefixes from 21 through 41 monomials also
passed. Before normalization, the 21- and 41-monomial narrow cases produced
roughly 19,334- and 66,674-bit raw rationals; chunking alone still failed to
finish the 47-monomial family. The normalized 92-monomial batch is therefore
direct evidence that unreduced rational growth, rather than polynomial size or
postfix depth alone, was the earlier scaling limit.

The complete clean final-code batch took **62.91 seconds** wall time with a
peak RSS of **2,251,648 KiB** (about 2.15 GiB). It used one computation core.

proved. The complete `cval` dimension-jet evaluator has not yet been related by
one end-to-end theorem to the ordinary dimension jet. The batch labels every
