## A source-identity mismatch was found and removed

While extending the literal registered-source evaluator, the exact conversion
of the registered `q_pi_half_interval` exposed a mismatch:

```text
literal registered source at scale 10^12: [1570796326734, 1570796326967]
previous specialized checker constant:    [1570796326794, 1570796326795]
```

Both intervals are valid analytic enclosures, and the specialized constant is
tighter.  They are not equal, however, so the tighter independently chosen
constant could not support an exact refinement theorem for the literal source
program.  The historical dihedral checker now uses the exact outward decimal
conversion of the registered rational interval.  The generic arctangent
constant remains unchanged.

  traces;
- a proved literal lookup/evaluation theorem for the registered rational
  source constant and its conversion to the bounded object;
