## Semantic boundary

`scripts/test-local-open-compatibility.sh` runs two bounded OCaml 4.14.1 versus
clean compiled Candle checks:

- `compatibility/oracles/open_local_parenthesized.ml` exercises simple and
  dotted module paths, custom symbolic operators, whitespace, and conflicting
  outer bindings.  Both frontends resolve the module operators and compute the
  same results.
- `compatibility/oracles/open_local_parenthesized_general.ml` demonstrates the
  broader boundary.  OCaml resolves `M.(value + 1)` as a local-open expression;
  Candle fails closed at `Undefined variable: Array.get`.

The broad repository inventory contains seven general-expression sites, but
all seven are outside the selected direct manifest.  This is not a claim of
general OCaml local-open compatibility.  The direct closure generator and
release lock require every selected occurrence to remain an operator reference;
introducing a general-expression body reopens the ledger entry.
