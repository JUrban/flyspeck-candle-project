## Result

CakeML commit `eb988ef16` adds the next decimal-scale slice of the specialized
Taylor engine.  At scale `10^12`, it now contains:

- signed 192-bit fused raw-product accumulation with one outward decimal
  normalization;
- interval-list scaling, fused scale-add, fused Hessian rows, and symmetric
  outer products;
- decimal gradient, Taylor-error, and final value completion;
- complete Taylor multiplication;
- a reusable unary chain-rule construction; and
- reciprocal and square-root Taylor lifts with checked decimal certificates.

The fused-normalization theorems prove that an accepted lower endpoint is no
greater than the exact raw product sum divided by the scale, and that the
accepted upper endpoint is no less.  This preserves the authenticated object
checker's important "sum raw products, round once" rule rather than silently
substituting per-product rounding.

The higher-level functions currently prove fail-closed execution and
well-formed accepted Taylor states.  Their full semantic relation to the
universal analytic contract is still an explicit proof obligation.  This
commit therefore does **not** claim a complete sound decimal expression
evaluator or an original Flyspeck theorem.
