## Outcome

The cheap direct-`cake` compiled-checker result is now backed by another
reusable HOL4 soundness layer.  The compact delta implementation proves that
successful execution of each of these structural helpers preserves its exact
fixed-point meaning:

- left-folded interval sum from an arbitrary checked accumulator;
- zero-initialized interval sum;
- multiplication by four through two checked additions;
- elementwise multiplication by four;
- a list of independent interval sums; and
- checked addition at one list index, represented logically by `LUPDATE`.

All theorems are polymorphic in the caller-supplied integer scale.  They do not
assume the historical fixture, particular endpoint bytes, or a successful
certificate in advance.  Overflow and bad list shapes still fail closed in
the executable operations; the semantic conclusions apply only when those
operations return `SOME`.

The sum contract deliberately uses an integer `FOLDL` rather than a
fixture-expanded expression.  The indexed-update contract identifies the
exact logical list produced, rather than proving only length or validity.
Those choices keep the lemmas useful for the universal jet connection.
