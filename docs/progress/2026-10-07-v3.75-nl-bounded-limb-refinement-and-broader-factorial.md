`codex/nl-bounded-compute-v1` at commit `ff1067b92`.  The new HOL4 theory
defines two- and three-`word64` values and proves:

- their exclusive natural-number limits are exactly `2^128` and `2^192`;

## The two performance gaps remain separate

The bounded representation work does not replace the broader numerical
investigation.  The two historical observations remain visible:

1. approximately 15 times from exact-output-matched native execution to the
   encoded proof-producing path; and
2. approximately 340 times from the initial generic native fixed-scale route
   to the historical specialized C++ route.

The second number is a starting architecture gap, not the residual after all
native prototypes.  On case10173, existing controlled experiments already
held the graph fixed while changing exact endpoints to padded hardware
endpoints (1.628 times), and held padded hardware arithmetic fixed while
changing the generic graph to direct authenticated data flow (1.087 times).
The optimized exact native route is consequently about 1.35 times from the
historical specialized control on that fixture.  Repeating those two axes on
case10173 would not answer a new question.  The remaining approximately
13--34-times matched encoded-execution results are a distinct formal
execution boundary.

Case16594 supplies the useful independent stress case.  Its unchanged generic
167-instruction graph has already separated arithmetic: exact mixed 128/192
execution takes about 2.447 seconds, whereas the earlier padded-hardware lane
takes about 0.77--0.79 seconds.  Preparation of the existing 39/85-step pair
without changing its operations gave no gain, so another decoder/cache
variant is not informative.

## Next native two-axis discriminator

The next native experiment will hold one axis fixed at a time on genuine
case16594 boxes:

1. authenticate one representative 39/85-step source pair and lower its
   polynomial value, center tangent, and box Hessian to a common-subexpression
   shared straight-line plan, rather than evaluating every operation as a
   dense generic six-coordinate jet;
2. run the generic and lowered plans under the same mixed exact 128/192
   arithmetic and identical outward rounding, separating derivative/Taylor
   work from representation;
3. run those same two plans under the existing padded-hardware diagnostic,
   separating arithmetic/rounding from the operation graph; and
4. screen cheaply on a genuine prefix, but make the decision only on all 875
   cells with preparation, evaluation, cold total, peak RSS, enclosure
   differences, and 875/875 acceptance reported.

The plan must be derived from and authenticated against the supplied
polynomial payload; it may not be a fixture-index or expression-name
whitelist.  Changing algebraic grouping may change interval widths, so
byte-identical outputs are required only within comparisons that preserve the
rounding schedule.  Every lane must retain complete certificate acceptance,
and containment/bound differences remain explicit.

A local prefix loss stops the lowering before proof work.  A full-batch win
must then generalize to another real expression/certificate family before it
earns a general source-to-plan theorem.  This makes the experiment capable of
identifying a substantial architectural gain while keeping the current
128/192 proof packet bounded.
