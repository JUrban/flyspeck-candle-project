## Universal result-decoder bridge

Candle commit `119fde3b` is pushed on
`codex/cv-nonlinear-disjunctive-v1`.

The compact reflected layer now has a total decoder for encoded root stacks
and complete compact-run results, with a universal left-inverse theorem:

```text
decode_result (encode_result result) = result
```

The adapter first uses the already proved reflected/logical correspondence to
establish that the computed raw result is in the logical encoder's image. It
then applies the left inverse once to recover the logical run equation. This
replaces three concrete-result operations from v2.66:

- canonicalizing the complete root interval list;
- rebuilding the expected encoded result; and
- applying the full encoder injectivity theorem.

This is not a relaxed raw-data path. The raw numerical jobs and token stream
are still authenticated exactly, the computed result must have success,
complete job consumption, and a singleton root stack, and the resulting run
equation must match the analytic soundness theorem's exact premise.

On the genuine four-cell fixture, the exact final candidate returned the
source theorem with zero assumptions and no axiom growth. The complete
post-numerical path measured **10.314 seconds**, versus 12.220 seconds after
the previous representation-reuse optimization and 17.043 seconds before
both optimizations. This is respectively a **15.6%** and **39.5%** reduction.

| Four-cell phase | Wall time |
|---|---:|
| reflected topology compute | 0.416 s |
| reflected/logical correspondence | 1.101 s |
| complete result decode | 1.724 s |
| analytic soundness composition | 7.069 s |

The exact final candidate also passed the existing seven-way adversarial
suite: malformed leaf and glue tokens, out-of-range and wrong-but-valid axes,
missing branches, changed split boxes, and leftover jobs all remain rejected.

next measured proof-side bottleneck. The raw numerical computation is still
the larger absolute cost for this batch.

## Decision and next experiment

The reflected architecture continues to produce compact source theorems on
larger genuine data, but host theorem construction is becoming superlinear.
The next discriminating experiment should avoid immediately doubling again.
It should profile whether the growth comes primarily from specializing the
universal analytic theorem with the large decoded job term, from constructing
the nested conjunction premise, or from kernel matching of that premise.

Any optimization should preserve the current universal theorem boundary and
source interface. A likely direction is to keep the accepted job batch behind
a reusable named theorem/abbreviation or derive soundness from the computed
result without repeatedly substituting the full concrete list through the
universal theorem. That hypothesis needs a focused measurement before an
architectural change.
