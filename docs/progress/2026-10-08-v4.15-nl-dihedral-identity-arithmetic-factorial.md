## Held-arithmetic dihedral experiment

The existing source-authenticated case16594 lane evaluates three eight-step
chains of the form

```text
pi/2 + atan((-d(delta)/dx_(i+3)) / sqrt(4*x_i*delta))
```

while retaining every intermediate generic Taylor completion.  A bounded
native candidate replaces those chains with the corresponding direct
value/gradient/Hessian identities for all three authenticated coordinate
pairs `(0,3)`, `(1,4)`, and `(2,5)`.

The candidate still checks exact source-program and logical-role identity.  A
separate diagnostic recomputes the original chains and requires overlap for
every center value/gradient, whole-box value/gradient, and Hessian enclosure
on every cell.  This is strong development evidence, not a soundness proof.

Nine warmed, rotation-balanced repetitions on all 875 genuine certificate
boxes, with exact mixed 128/192-bit dyadic arithmetic held fixed, gave:

| Exact native lane | Preparation | Evaluation | Complete batch | Accepted |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Sequential Taylor chains | 0.061480 s | 0.432725 s | 0.494205 s | 875/875 |
| Direct dihedral identities | 0.061670 s | **0.405700 s** | **0.467369 s** | 875/875 |

The recurring speedup is **1.0666 times** and the complete-batch speedup is
**1.0574 times**.  The candidate removes 511,135 interval products (6.16%)
and 13,125 Taylor completions (8.06%).  Its final upper bound is tighter on
all 875 cells and wider on none; the minimum margin below zero rises from
approximately 0.00109 to 0.15294.  The complete internal overlap diagnostic
also passes all 875 cells.

This is a useful enclosure improvement, but a 5.7--6.7% complete/recurring
win is not a large-gap result.  The optional tighter face-U calculation was
also measured and added work rather than reducing the batch time.

The held-axis recurring factors are:

Reusable native builds cost 13.06 seconds for exact arithmetic and 9.50

## Consequence for the two gaps

The two gaps remain explicit and distinct:

1. the current matched case16594 complete verified checker is about **24
   times** slower than its exact native numerical lane; and
2. the historical roughly **340-times** generic fixed-scale native versus
   specialized-C++ comparison is an algorithmic warning from the original
   case10173 starting point, not a matched residual for case16594.  Later
   specialization reduced the case10173 native residual to about 1.35 times.

The new result says that eliminating the generic calculus inside the three
dihedral chains is not enough on case16594.  Even after that replacement, the
batch still executes 7,782,115 interval products and 149,625 Taylor
completions.  A diagnostic sequential profile also distributes native time
across polynomial evaluation, general multiplication, reciprocal,
square-root, arctangent, and final Taylor work; no remaining scalar family
dominates by orders of magnitude.  Profile totals for the identity lane are
not used as timings because the fused identity body is outside the old
per-instruction timer.

## Bounded proof decision

The existing six delta-role refinements remain the bounded production proof
packet because they already transferred a 1.86-times complete verified gain.
The new generalized identity code is retained as an isolated native
candidate, but no production proof is started for a 1.057-times
complete-batch improvement.

Proof work on a larger numerical instruction will begin only after the exact
native version produces a material all-box win with sound outward-bound
evidence and a credible second-family route.  This prevents the current
specialization from growing into a sequence of low-yield per-expression
lemmas.

## Concrete larger-gap packet

The next algorithmic native prototype should be a source-authenticated
**shared tetrahedron/whole-expression data flow**, not another scalar
replacement:

1. compute common delta products, its gradient/Hessian data, reconstructed
   box facts, and the three related dihedral quantities once per box;
2. reuse those data across all three angles and the surrounding expression,
   while preserving the tightening needed for all 875 cells;
3. keep exact dyadic arithmetic and directed rounding fixed for the first
   gate, and report preparation, complete recurring time, peak memory,
   operation counts, bound deltas, and acceptance;
4. require 875/875 acceptance and a substantial complete-batch factor before
   crossing that identical algorithm with padded hardware arithmetic;
5. repeat a successful architecture on an independent genuine expression
   family before designing its reusable proof instruction; and
6. reject the route if sharing merely saves another small percentage.

In parallel, the separate approximately 24-times verified/native boundary
continues to motivate the general fixed-layout checked-word executor.  The
standalone parser is only about 3% of the present verified batch, so that lane
must target generated word/Taylor representation, allocation, and execution,
not input conversion.  Complete certificate time and theorem handoff remain
the formal decision measures.

This preserves both performance investigations: less mathematical work per
box for the historical algorithmic problem, and cheaper proved execution for
the Candle/native problem.  The ratios are neither conflated nor multiplied.
