## Result

The previously reported roughly 514-second cost for only 32 genuine
case-16594 cells was not an inherent arithmetic scaling result. Repeating the
same exact first-prefix computation in the sealed 16 GiB state reduced the
32-cell `Kernel.compute` phase to **14.586 seconds**, a **35.20x** change.

The complete 875-cell computation and exact source-theorem handoff reported in
v2.95 already used this large state. Completing the missing preparation
profiles now gives a warm, shared-checker total of **596.196 seconds** from
case-specific certificate construction through the final genuine source
theorem. The archived native Flyspeck record for the same case is **597.505
seconds**. Thus the large apparent reflected-checker gap is closed on this
case. The measurements do **not** establish an end-to-end speedup: they are
from different historical machines, and reusable expression/checker setup is
accounted separately.

## Genuine batch scaling

One authenticated source program was held fixed while exact prefixes of the
real 875-cell plan supplied changing square-root hints and boxes as ordinary
canonical data. Every reflected call accepted, all result theorems were
closed, and no large result was retained by the host REPL.

| Genuine cells | Encoding wall | `Kernel.compute` wall | Compute/cell |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 0.000534 s | 0.904948 s | 0.904948 s |
| 8 | 0.402308 s | 3.418049 s | 0.427256 s |
| 32 | 1.709870 s | 14.586099 s | 0.455816 s |
| 128 | 6.834716 s | 66.038987 s | 0.515930 s |
| 875, earlier complete run | 52.684838 s | 433.440363 s | 0.495360 s |

The 128- and 875-cell points remain near one-half second per cell. There is no
late superlinear evaluator growth in this range. Peak sampled RSS was
17,982,344 KiB (about 17.15 GiB) in both the prefix-scaling and complete runs.

The matched earlier first-32 run used the same logical jobs and checker
equations in a 2,253,704 KiB state and took 513.466 seconds. The new result is
therefore strong evidence that the old runaway was a managed-heap/GC-capacity
boundary, not evidence that exact rational checking intrinsically costs 16
seconds per cell. Production NL measurements must preserve the large-heap
condition; small-state timings are not representative.

The whole case-plan interval includes source reconstruction and validation in
addition to the two explicitly marked phases, so the 28.132- and 12.530-second
entries are not added to 42.035 a second time.

The raw-plan constructor was instrumented without changing its data:

- recursive cell and certificate construction: **41.292545 seconds**;
- flattening the completed tree: **0.000448 seconds**;
- counting leaves and nodes: **0.000347 seconds**;
- acknowledgement through readiness: **44.549578 seconds**.

Thus list flattening and topology counting are not current targets. The real
cost is creating the 875 exact numerical cells.

Excluding the 6.218-second checker-support load, which is reusable across
certificates, the case-specific total is:

Charging the raw-support load once gives 602.414410 seconds. Relative to the
597.504859-second historical record, those totals are respectively 0.219%
below and 0.822% above it. Those sub-one-percent differences are not treated
as speed measurements across different machines. Their useful meaning is
that the reflected architecture has reached the correct performance scale.

Within the 596.196-second recurring total:

- numerical `Kernel.compute` is **72.70%**;
- all measured case-specific preparation is **24.92%**; and
- final theorem and source handoff is **2.38%**.

This changes the optimization priority. The stable-program/data-only
architecture and compact theorem boundary are no longer the main gap on this
case. The next material gain must primarily reduce numerical execution or the
number of necessary cells, while preserving acceptance coverage.

## Consequence and next boundary

1. Keep the direct action-170 worker authoritative; seal its post-action
   checkpoint and advance the already staged actions 171--296 as soon as it
   completes.
2. Treat a roughly 16 GiB evaluator heap as part of the current NL execution
   configuration. Do not use the old 2.25 GiB results for throughput planning.
3. Use a bounded genuine-cell discriminator for fixed-scale multiplication
   and square after the final nonlinear instruction. Report runtime,
   acceptance, and any additional subdivision before extending its universal
   soundness proof.
4. Generalize the stable-program/raw-data complete checker to a second
   expression and full genuine certificate, with case 10173 as the prepared
   candidate. This tests whether the parity result generalizes beyond the
   disjunctive case-16594 family.
5. Only after a numerical candidate wins on varied genuine cells should it be
   proved through the reusable tagged invariant and rerun through the exact
   complete source theorem. Micro-optimizing flattening, counting, evaluator
   startup, or final theorem handoff is not currently justified.

The reflected NL lane now has a complete, exact theorem and a measured
target-scale runtime on one substantial real case. Full nonlinear S3 remains
open until the design is integrated across the corpus and direct action 296,
with acceptance coverage and complete source theorems preserved.
