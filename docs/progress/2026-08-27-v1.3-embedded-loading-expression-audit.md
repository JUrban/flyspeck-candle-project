# v1.3 embedded loading-expression audit — 2026-08-27

## Scope and result

This is a source-backed audit for future normalization, not an implementation,
execution, or normalization-proof claim.  It is anchored to the clean Candle
loader commit `fa209f9dd213292f05b3f4dbf7516b3148bc1319`, manifest SHA-256
`2c8dc12519f99c0ec9d08ecf8a9fa710ce50f01007d3f75042eabd343d8b46f1`,
and direct Flyspeck commit
`1ce0353008eba83d3c76ae9a25c3c242e4802d53`.

The manifest records exactly 12 loading tokens in expression position:
four `flyspeck_needs`, four `needs`, three `loadt`, and one `reneeds`.  They
divide into the following primary treatments:

| Primary treatment | Sites | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| ordinary verified evaluation or an exact generated root expansion | 2 | The expression is active on the selected route; the scanner must not treat its token as an eager directive. |
| exact branch selection | 3 | Evaluate only the branch selected by the pinned environment/version; executing both branches is invalid. |
| fail-closed unused binding | 6 | The selected graph constructs a function/alias, but has no active call site.  Preserve the binding; an unsupported unexpected call must abort rather than become a successful no-op. |
| generated-input lifecycle | 1 | The dynamic source does not have a static content hash and needs an authenticated producer/consumer lifecycle before support. |

The intended direct route is still blocked on exact loader actions, so
“selected” below means required by the pinned route once earlier gates close,
not observed end-to-end execution.

The absence-of-call conclusions are bounded static graph findings.  They do not
exclude reflective lookup, user interaction, or a future source change and are
not sufficient by themselves to delete the bindings.

There is also an exact failure-edge issue at site 1.  Pinned
`strictbuild.hl:187-216` invokes `needb`, restores `host`, then attempts
`State_manager.neutralize_state` even when `needb` returned `false`; it catches
`Failure` from neutralization and only afterward raises for the failed load.
The manifest's generated contract instead says to neutralize “exactly once
after success.”  The generated behavior is cleaner, but it is not yet proved
equivalent to the pinned function on failed evaluation or failed
neutralization.  A mechanical replacement must either reproduce those edges or
make the intentional strengthening explicit and prove that they are
unobservable on every accepted run.

## Generated digest-file lifecycle obligation

The runtime file's intended source form is produced by lines 480-496:
`module Theorem_digest = struct`, a `digest_list_extern` list, and `end`.
Supporting site 12 requires a versioned generated-input contract that at least:

1. creates a private, confined path without a symlink/race window;
2. writes only the validated module/list schema with correctly escaped digest
   strings, closes it successfully, and computes its byte count and SHA-256;
3. binds the consumer to that exact path/hash and to the theorem-digest producer
   state, rather than accepting an arbitrary mutable file;
4. evaluates it exactly once with normal failure propagation and only after the
   successful producer step;
5. records cleanup and replay policy, including whether an already-loaded
   basename/digest may skip evaluation.

The current pin does not call the consumer, but it does create the temporary
path when `serialization.hl` loads.  Eliminating even that side effect requires
a separate unused-lifecycle argument; this audit does not provide one.

## Mechanical-normalization rules

- Exact standalone loader actions may be recognized only when the token starts
  a complete top-level phrase.  These 12 tokens do not satisfy that grammar.
- Ordinary definitions and applications should go through verified expression
  evaluation.  A source scanner must neither eagerly execute their bodies nor
  erase the token before or after a failed evaluation.
- Site 1 may be expanded only to the already generated, authenticated 297-entry
  program, with every target key/hash/order checked and exact once/neutralize
  semantics implemented.  The generated program is not currently an execution
  proof.
- Sites 3 and 10-11 may be normalized only after checking all guard-input,
  whole-file, target, and span hashes.  Emit only the selected branch; a guard
  mismatch aborts regeneration rather than falling back to both targets.
- Sites 4-9 may remain ordinary bindings.  If the compiled path cannot support
  their bodies and a separate reachability argument justifies a route-specific
  stub, the binding must fail on invocation.  It must not report a successful
  no-op, and alias/function identity must be preserved where observable.
- Site 12 stays closed until its generated-input lifecycle is versioned and
  tested.
