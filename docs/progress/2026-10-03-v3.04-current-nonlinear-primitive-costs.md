## Plan consequence

Square-root work is the largest aggregate nonlinear subtarget because the
source invokes it seven times, but a square-root-only numerical redesign is
not supported by the per-step evidence. Common rational Taylor machinery used
by square root, inverse, and arctangent is the more credible reusable target.

The other half of current checker time remains in 44 fixed-class steps. Before
starting a new nonlinear representation or proof, the symmetric cheap
discriminator is to split those fixed steps into:

- 22 compiled polynomial pushes; and
- 22 outer additions/multiplications.

That measurement will show whether a shared fixed Taylor primitive or the
polynomial evaluator offers the larger reusable ceiling. Any implementation
candidate must still preserve enclosure tightness and certificate coverage.
